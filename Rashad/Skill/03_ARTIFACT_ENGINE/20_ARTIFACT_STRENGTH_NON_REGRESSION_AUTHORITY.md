# ART-NR-001 — Artifact Strength Non-Regression Authority

## Authority level
Priority 0 for all RFP summaries, proposal sections, presentation pages, HTML, PDF, and editable PowerPoint output.

## Purpose
Prevent safety, containment, RTL, or renderer corrections from weakening the analytical or visual strength of an already approved artifact system.

Correctness is mandatory, but correctness alone is not approval. A page that is safe, readable, and technically valid may still fail because it has become generic, repetitive, card-led, decorative, or weaker than the approved benchmark.

## Core rule
A new version may not score lower than the strongest approved prior version for the same page role or artifact family unless:
1. the source content materially changed;
2. the prior artifact was factually misleading;
3. the council approves the change with a documented rationale;
4. the user explicitly accepts the lower visual ambition.

## Safety fixes must preserve the artifact
When collision, overflow, font, PDF parity, or RTL problems are detected, the repair order is:
1. preserve the page thesis;
2. preserve the dominant information relationship;
3. preserve the selected artifact family;
4. recompose geometry and whitespace;
5. compress only supporting copy;
6. split the page while preserving the narrative role;
7. move to an equivalent power artifact;
8. use generic cards only when the information relationship is genuinely a taxonomy or parallel list.

It is forbidden to solve a safety defect by silently replacing a lifecycle, dependency map, decision model, governance system, or operating chain with an equal-card grid.

## Artifact Strength Score — 100 points
- Information-relationship fidelity: 20
- Evaluator decision utility: 15
- Thesis and synthesis strength: 15
- Structural originality and focal point: 15
- Narrative role and section rhythm: 10
- Evidence and source integration: 10
- Visual asset / icon / proof use where valuable: 5
- Density and compositional balance: 5
- Brand, RTL, editability, and renderer stability: 5

## Minimum release thresholds
- Critical executive / evaluation / methodology / governance page: 90
- Standard analytical page: 85
- Supporting control or evidence page: 82
- Section average: 87
- No production page below 82

A page may pass all collision and overflow tests and still be rejected for an Artifact Strength Score below threshold.

## Golden benchmark comparison
For every critical page:
1. retrieve three relevant benchmark pages;
2. identify the strongest approved prior artifact for the same purpose;
3. compare the new page across all score dimensions;
4. reject if any core dimension drops by more than 3 points without council approval;
5. save the comparison in the page audit record.

## Layout diversity floor
For any artifact pack of 10 pages or more:
- no more than 2 consecutive pages may use the same composition family;
- card-led pages should normally remain at or below 30%;
- at least one relationship-led power artifact must appear in every 3 analytical pages;
- a long pack must include deliberate narrative resets, evidence pages, or image/illustration-led pages where they add real value;
- the same 3-column, 4-card, 5-card, or 7-card grid cannot become the default page answer.

These are default floors, not reasons to force images or diagrams where precision requires a matrix, table, or native technical model.

## Unsupported visual encoding prohibition
Do not use different bar lengths, heat levels, maturity levels, severity levels, or quantitative-looking shapes unless the source or an explicitly approved analytical method supports the comparison.

When a page contains unscored risks or qualitative topics:
- use equal treatment;
- use categories;
- use a qualitative priority rationale;
- or clearly label the visual as an illustrative hypothesis requiring validation.

## Power artifact preservation examples
- Scope chain remains a connected operating chain, not four isolated cards.
- BOQ remains an output dependency architecture, not seven equal cards.
- Evaluation remains a decision funnel / weighted model, not detached percentages.
- Team remains a role-to-output responsibility map when accountability is central.
- Cybersecurity remains an obligation lifecycle when sequence is central.
- Penalties remain an escalation curve when progression is central.
- Quality and knowledge transfer remain a closed-loop control system when sustainability is central.

## Hard fail status
Any violation produces:

REJECTED — ART-NR-001 ARTIFACT STRENGTH REGRESSION — REBUILD REQUIRED
