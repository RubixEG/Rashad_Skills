| **C-02**        | **Financial Modelling**                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Builds or reviews financial models that support investment decisions, valuations, business cases, or strategic planning.                                                                                                                |
| **Delivers**    | Financial model (revenue, cost, cash flow, P&L, balance sheet), scenario models, sensitivity analysis, model documentation.                                                                                                             |
| **How**         | Model architecture design, assumption development and validation, model build, scenario and sensitivity design, model audit and stress testing, output interpretation.                                                                  |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi-specific considerations: SAR denomination, Hijri calendar alignment for government clients, Zakat calculation requirements, GOSI employer contribution modelling, and end-of-service benefit calculations under Saudi Labour Law. |
