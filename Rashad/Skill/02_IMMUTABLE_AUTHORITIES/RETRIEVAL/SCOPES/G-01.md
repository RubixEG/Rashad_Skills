| **G-01**        | **Process Mapping and Redesign**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Maps current-state processes end-to-end and redesigns them to eliminate waste, reduce cycle time, and improve quality.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Delivers**    | Current-state process maps, process gap analysis, redesigned processes, process documentation, implementation roadmap, performance metrics.                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **How**         | Process observation and mapping, waste identification, root cause analysis, process redesign, pilot design, change management planning, performance measurement design.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi government process redesign must account for regulatory approval steps that cannot be eliminated (ministerial signature requirements, MOF approvals) and cultural norms around verification and counter-signature. Digital government transformation provides the opportunity to redesign citizen-facing processes end-to-end using platforms like Absher. |
