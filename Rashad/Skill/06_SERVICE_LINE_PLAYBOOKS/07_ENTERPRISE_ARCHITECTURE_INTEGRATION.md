# Enterprise Architecture, Platforms, and Integration

## Senior-partner questions
- How do business, data, applications, technology, and security align?
- What is current versus target architecture?
- How will integrations, APIs, interoperability, and reuse work?
- How is architecture governed?

## Benchmark practice lenses
- DGA/NORA government EA lens
- BCG data and digital platform lens
- EY architecture lens
- PwC technology strategy lens
- vendor-neutral architecture lens

## Preferred artifact families
- Enterprise architecture landscape
- Current-to-target architecture
- Integration and API map
- Application rationalization heatmap
- Platform capability blueprint
- Architecture governance lifecycle

## Government evaluator test
- Is the section easy to score against the RFP?
- Are commitments feasible, measurable, and traceable?
- Are client dependencies and acceptance criteria explicit?
- Is the Arabic terminology native and precise?

## Evidence expectations
- direct relevance to the scope;
- recent and verifiable proof;
- named responsibility and outcome;
- no client leakage or unsupported credential.

## Anti-patterns
- vendor logo architecture
- unexplained boxes and arrows
- no nonfunctional requirements or transition

## Release condition
The line-of-service partner must approve the reasoning, the government evaluator must approve navigability and compliance, and the artifact director must approve the visual relationship before release.
