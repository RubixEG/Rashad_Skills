# Visual Anti-Patterns & Rejection Library — Consulting / Government Proposals

STATUS: ACTIVE REJECTION AUTHORITY

Reject the following unless a documented information relationship specifically justifies the pattern:

## AP-01 Generic equal-card default
Symptoms: every idea becomes the same-sized card; hierarchy and dependencies disappear.

## AP-02 Decorative arrows / orbit graphics
Symptoms: connectors exist visually but encode no true sequence, causality, ownership or dependency.

## AP-03 Startup / social-media visual language
Symptoms: playful layout, oversized slogans, consumer-app styling, weak evidence density.

## AP-04 Overloaded 3D / pseudo-futurism
Symptoms: glow, glassmorphism, neon, excessive depth, decorative technology effects.

## AP-05 Faux infographic
Symptoms: many icons/metrics with no decision logic, no thesis, and no evidence chain.

## AP-06 Ungrounded cityscape
Acceptable primarily as a purposeful cover/hero ingredient. Reject on analytical pages when it replaces analysis.

## AP-07 Meaningless radial/orbit model
Reject if ring position, distance, direction or grouping has no semantic meaning.

## AP-08 Table-as-universal-answer
Use tables for precise lookup. Reject when system/dependency/journey/decision logic would communicate the relationship better.

## AP-09 Diagram-as-decoration
Reject shapes and connectors that do not map to defined nodes/edges.

## AP-10 Tiny-text compression
Never solve density by unreadable type. Split or restructure.

## AP-11 Screenshot/certificate wall
Evidence must be curated, labeled and tied to the current claim/criterion.

## AP-12 Uncontrolled color coding
Semantic accents only. Avoid rainbow categorization and unsupported severity colors.

## AP-13 Repetitive page family
Long sections require rhythm. Repetition without purpose fails section quality.

## AP-14 Marketing poster instead of consulting page
Large decorative imagery plus slogans cannot replace an analytical artifact where decision support is required.

## AP-15 Safe-but-weaker renderer fallback
A renderer-created simplification that removes topology or analytical strength is a hard production failure.

## v2.6.4.7 card anti-pattern refinement
The problem is not the existence of cards; the problem is generic equal-card layout used as a substitute for relationship logic. Cards may appear inside a larger system, table, cockpit, roadmap or evidence model. Reject card-only pages when they erase sequence, causality, dependency, ownership, trade-off or decision structure.
