| **E-08**        | **Saudization Strategy and Compliance**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs the strategy and programme for achieving and sustaining Saudization (Nitaqat) compliance and for developing Saudi national talent.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Delivers**    | Saudization compliance assessment, Saudization roadmap, Saudi national development programme, Nitaqat band improvement plan, compliance monitoring framework.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **How**         | Current Nitaqat compliance assessment, gap analysis, role reclassification review, Saudi national development programme design, recruitment strategy, compliance monitoring design.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Saudi & GCC** | This scope is uniquely Saudi and critical for any private sector organisation operating in the Kingdom. The Nitaqat programme classifies employers into Platinum, Green, Yellow, and Red bands based on Saudization percentage by activity. Specific GOSI-registered activities have different Saudization requirements. Non-compliance carries significant consequences including restrictions on expatriate visa issuance. |
