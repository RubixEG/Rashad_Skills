| **E-02**        | **Workforce Planning**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs the workforce required to execute the organisational strategy, including headcount, skills, composition, and Saudization targets.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Delivers**    | Workforce plan, Saudization roadmap, capability requirements analysis, workforce gap assessment, talent acquisition plan, workforce cost model.                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **How**         | Demand analysis (strategic requirements), supply analysis (current and projected), gap identification, Saudization compliance planning, workforce scenario modelling, talent strategy development.                                                                                                                                          |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudization (Nitaqat programme) is the dominant constraint in Saudi private sector workforce planning. Each sector has specific Saudization targets, and each activity classification has a required percentage of Saudi nationals. Workforce plans must demonstrate compliance with current Nitaqat bands and a trajectory to improvement. |
