# Premium Artifact Catalog and Mapping Specification

## Purpose

This registry is the visual and analytical vocabulary of the Rashad Proposal Engine. It is not a template gallery. Each artifact is an information structure with defined inputs, evaluator purpose, density rules, Arabic RTL behavior, dependencies, and failure conditions.

The engine must first classify the information relationship, then retrieve candidate artifacts. It must never choose an artifact because it is aesthetically attractive or convenient to render.

## Candidate selection scoring

- Information-relationship fit: 20
- Evaluator usefulness: 15
- Evidence capacity: 15
- Technical or analytical explanatory power: 15
- Density suitability: 10
- Arabic RTL readability: 10
- Narrative variety versus adjacent pages: 5
- Rubix brand compatibility: 5
- Editable PPTX feasibility: 5

A registered artifact must score at least 82 to be selected. Otherwise, the engine creates a bespoke composition using the same page-contract, grading, and parity rules.

## Universal artifact rules

Every artifact must include:

1. A conclusion-led page thesis.
2. One evaluator question.
3. Source-linked claims and evidence.
4. Clear visual reading path.
5. Explicit ownership, timing, controls, or acceptance where relevant.
6. Minimum and maximum density rules.
7. Arabic RTL and mixed-language handling.
8. Native editable PPTX construction plan.
9. Upstream and downstream dependency references.
10. Hard-fail conditions.



# Decision and synthesis

Turns complex analysis into a decision, recommendation, or executive conclusion.


## DEC-01: Executive synthesis

**Purpose:** Multiple approved sections must be distilled into a small number of decisive messages.

**Evaluator question:** What must the evaluator remember and believe?

**Primary information relationship:** hierarchy and synthesis.

**Mandatory inputs:** Applicable RFP requirements; approved claims; evidence or explicit assumptions; relevant owners, measures, dates, interfaces, controls, or acceptance criteria.

**Mandatory visual components:** A conclusion-led headline, 3 to 5 messages, supporting proof, and explicit implications.

**Minimum meaningful content:** 3 to 5 substantive information units plus the page thesis and source note.

**Default density target:** medium. Density may change only with a page-contract rationale.

**Do not use when:** Do not use when the page is primarily a detailed process or architecture.

**Arabic and RTL rule:** Use an RTL top-down or right-to-left hierarchy; the conclusion must be read first.

**Editable PPTX construction:** Native text boxes, connectors, and editable evidence markers.

**Typical upstream dependencies:** RFP requirement register, evaluator question, proposal claim, evidence ledger, approved terminology, and relevant solution or delivery decisions.

**Typical downstream dependencies:** Executive Summary, compliance matrix, roadmap, governance, team, value, commercial basis, or other pages that summarize or operationalize this artifact.

**Hard failures:** Unsupported claims; missing labels or ownership; unreadable density; decorative icons replacing analysis; incorrect directionality; cross-section inconsistency; flattened screenshot-only PPTX output.
