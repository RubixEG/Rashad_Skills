
# Consulting Exhibit Title & Takeaway Standard

## Principle
A page title should tell the evaluator what to conclude, not merely name the topic, whenever the evidence supports a conclusion.

Use:
- insight-led title;
- concise context/subtitle only when needed;
- explicit implication/decision band where management action matters.

Avoid:
- topic-only title + unexplained artifact;
- slogan-like marketing copy unsupported by evidence;
- repeating the same statement in title, subtitle and footer.
