# Council and Decision Rules — Behavioral Mirror

STATUS: ACTIVE CONTEXT MAP

## Council truth
A council is not a decorative label. Do not claim it ran unless the required roles produced findings/decisions and the relevant gate can be evidenced.

## Risk Council core
- Partner: strategic/commercial exposure, commitments, margin/overcommitment.
- Director: solution feasibility and cross-workstream coherence.
- Senior PM / Team Manager: critical path, staffing, dependencies, milestones, acceptance/rework.
- Operations & Launch Lead: go-live, transition, handover, hypercare, support and BAU readiness.
- Relevant PMs/SMEs join only when the actual scope warrants them.

## Clarification Council core
Partner → Director → Senior PM as chair → relevant PM(s) → Associate/Analyst → Operations when relevant → specialists conditionally.

Every clarification must pass the material-decision test defined in the current authority.

## Artifact Council
Artifact Council validates semantic fidelity, relationship choice, archetype, hierarchy, narrative rhythm and visual direction before geometry/safety repair. Safety may fix clipping/overlap/fit but may not replace a strong artifact with a generic simplified layout merely to make production easier.

## Decision Ledger use
Before asking the owner to restate a preference, check `13_OWNER_DECISION_LEDGER.md`. If the topic is ACTIVE and applies, use it. Do not resurrect REJECTED/SUPERSEDED behavior.
## v2.6.4.2 production renderer council routing
After the existing Pre-Render Visual Excellence Council, use the Production Renderer Release Council for final-format evidence. It must review direction resolution, geometry/text fit, node/edge reconciliation, brand/asset preflight, Stress QA and format parity. Machine-test claims require actual runtime evidence; council wording alone is not proof.

## V5.1 council addition
Critical pages require an upstream Artifact Council decision before downstream render councils. A technically safe page cannot compensate for weak relationship truth, weak artifact synthesis or card-only downgrade.
