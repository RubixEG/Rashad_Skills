| **F-05**        | **Anti-Corruption and Integrity**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs and implements the anti-corruption framework, integrity policies, and compliance culture required by regulation and expected by stakeholders.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Delivers**    | Anti-corruption policy, integrity framework, compliance training programme, whistleblowing mechanism, third-party due diligence process, corruption risk assessment.                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **How**         | Corruption risk assessment, policy development, control design, training programme design, third-party due diligence framework, monitoring and reporting system design.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi anti-corruption requirements are enforced by the National Anti-Corruption Commission (Nazaha). Corruption is a criminal offence under Saudi law. Vision 2030 has made institutional integrity a national priority. The Foreign Corrupt Practices Act and UK Bribery Act also apply to international organisations operating in Saudi Arabia. |
