# Artifact Complexity Budget

Complexity and clutter are different.

## Levels
- AC-1 Executive: one dominant relationship, very restrained.
- AC-2 Analytical: one dominant + limited supporting relationships.
- AC-3 Multi-layer: multiple interdependent relationship zones.
- AC-4 Dense System: system/architecture with substantial nodes/edges/evidence.
- AC-5 Technical/Decision Cockpit: dense but structured decision/evidence surface.

QA must not simplify AC-3/4/5 pages merely because they contain many elements. The gate is readability and relationship truth, not low object count.

If a page cannot safely contain the required complexity, split while preserving the analytical chain; do not delete nodes or shrink everything indiscriminately.
