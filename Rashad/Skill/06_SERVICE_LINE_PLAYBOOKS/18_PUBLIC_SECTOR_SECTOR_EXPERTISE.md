# Public Sector, Policy, and Sector Expertise

## Senior-partner questions
- What public value and mandate drive the engagement?
- What sector constraints, stakeholders, regulations, and delivery realities matter?
- How does the solution align with national and entity objectives?
- How is public value measured?

## Benchmark practice lenses
- public-sector strategy lens
- government transformation lens
- sector-specialist lens
- procurement and public-value lens

## Preferred artifact families
- Mandate-to-outcome map
- Policy and stakeholder landscape
- Public-value tree
- Sector operating ecosystem
- Regulatory obligation map
- National-to-entity alignment
- Benefits and citizen-outcome dashboard

## Government evaluator test
- Is the section easy to score against the RFP?
- Are commitments feasible, measurable, and traceable?
- Are client dependencies and acceptance criteria explicit?
- Is the Arabic terminology native and precise?

## Evidence expectations
- direct relevance to the scope;
- recent and verifiable proof;
- named responsibility and outcome;
- no client leakage or unsupported credential.

## Anti-patterns
- private-sector language copied into government
- national-alignment slogans without evidence
- sector-neutral methodology

## Release condition
The line-of-service partner must approve the reasoning, the government evaluator must approve navigability and compliance, and the artifact director must approve the visual relationship before release.
