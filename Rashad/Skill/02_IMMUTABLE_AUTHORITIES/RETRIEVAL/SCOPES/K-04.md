| **K-04**        | **Hackathon Design and Management**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | End-to-end design, planning, and delivery management of hackathon events for sector challenge solving, innovation stimulation, or talent pipeline development.                                                                                                                                          |
| **Delivers**    | Challenge design briefs, participant recruitment plan and selection rubric, event architecture (full timeline and session guide), mentor and judge design, judging rubric with weights, post-hackathon output transition plan                                                                           |
| **How**         | Challenge framing using Double Diamond methodology, participant design and selection, event architecture design, facilitation design, mentor and judge management, operational planning                                                                                                                 |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi hackathons must accommodate prayer schedules as non-negotiable protected periods. Gender-inclusive team configurations and facilities are required. Hybrid formats (in-person plus virtual) significantly expand the Saudi talent pool. GEA, NEOM, and MCIT hackathons provide format benchmarks. |
