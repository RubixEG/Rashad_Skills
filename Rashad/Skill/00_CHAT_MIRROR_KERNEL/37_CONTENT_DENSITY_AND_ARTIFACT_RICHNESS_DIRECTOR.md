# Content Density & Artifact Richness Director

STATUS: CONTENT/VISUAL DEPTH GOVERNOR — v2.6.4.9

A consulting-grade page is not improved by being emptier. Density is valuable when it is structured and decision-useful.

Critical analytical pages should normally contain:
- one explicit management/evaluator question;
- one strong thesis;
- a dominant artifact;
- 2–5 supporting evidence points, labels or annotations;
- one implication, decision or action signal;
- source/evidence locator.

Do not compress multiple logical roles merely to reduce slide count. The 24 logical RFP Summary roles may expand physically when required by the Depth Contracts.

Fail pages that are visually clean but analytically thin, generic or unsupported.
