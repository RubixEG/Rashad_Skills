| **B-03**        | **Organisational Restructuring**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Redesigns the organisational structure - reporting lines, spans of control, layers, and unit boundaries - to improve efficiency and alignment with strategy.                                                                                                                          |
| **Delivers**    | New organisational structure, spans and layers analysis, role definition framework, headcount implications, transition plan, communication plan.                                                                                                                                      |
| **How**         | Current structure analysis, spans and layers benchmarking, structural design options development, evaluation against design criteria, detailed structure design, transition planning.                                                                                                 |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudization (Nitaqat) compliance is a mandatory constraint in any Saudi private sector restructuring. Government restructuring must follow Civil Service Bureau approval processes. Any change affecting more than a defined number of roles may require formal ministerial decision. |
