| **G-05**        | **Quality Management System Design**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs and implements the quality management system that ensures consistent delivery to required standards.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Delivers**    | Quality management framework, quality standards, quality processes and procedures, quality monitoring system, quality culture programme.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **How**         | Current quality assessment, standard selection and adaptation, process design, documentation development, monitoring system design, training programme design.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi quality management systems in healthcare must comply with CBAHI (Central Board for Accreditation of Healthcare Institutions) standards. Educational quality: NCAAA accreditation. Government quality: National Centre for Government Performance standards. ISO certification is valued in both government and private sector contexts. |
