# Card Grammar Control & Artifact Richness Lock

STATUS: HARD ANTI-DOWNGRADE AUTHORITY — v2.6.4.7

## Principle
Cards are allowed as supporting components. Cards are not the default artifact strategy.

## Allowed cards
Cards may be used when they support a larger artifact such as:
- governance system;
- risk/evidence/control architecture;
- timeline with gates;
- matrix or table with side interpretation;
- decision cockpit;
- BOQ/deliverable architecture;
- operating model.

## Rejected card misuse
Reject or redesign when:
- the page is only equal cards with no system relationship;
- three or more consecutive analytical pages use the same card-grid grammar without a deliberate rhythm reason;
- a flow/network/architecture is downgraded into cards because native rendering is easier;
- cards hide dependencies, causality, sequence, ownership or trade-offs;
- the page looks like a generic SaaS dashboard rather than a consulting-grade government proposal page.

## Required artifact richness
Every analytical page must encode at least one meaningful relationship: sequence, causality, dependency, governance, evidence-to-decision, trade-off, value chain, capability stack, readiness, risk/control, or scope-to-output logic.

## Renderer rule
If the approved Golden Visual Master is rich and the native renderer would simplify it to cards, use the image-based visual mirror route for the official PDF/PPTX and treat editable-native reconstruction as separate.

## v2.6.4.9 correction
Do not measure consulting quality by the raw number of rectangles/cards. Measure whether a higher-order analytical relationship governs the page. Small panels are legitimate evidence containers when they sit inside a strong system, matrix, journey, cockpit, architecture or evaluation logic.
