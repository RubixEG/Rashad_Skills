# Partnerships, Investment, Ecosystems, and PPP

## Senior-partner questions
- What opportunity and value exchange attract partners?
- How are partners segmented and selected?
- What legal, commercial, governance, activation, and performance mechanisms are needed?
- How is portfolio value managed?

## Benchmark practice lenses
- Strategy& public-sector partnership lens
- Big Four PPP and transaction lens
- Kearney ecosystem lens
- investment and portfolio lens

## Preferred artifact families
- Partner ecosystem map
- Opportunity screening funnel
- Partnership model options matrix
- Value exchange architecture
- Agreement-to-activation lifecycle
- Portfolio governance model
- Partner performance dashboard

## Government evaluator test
- Is the section easy to score against the RFP?
- Are commitments feasible, measurable, and traceable?
- Are client dependencies and acceptance criteria explicit?
- Is the Arabic terminology native and precise?

## Evidence expectations
- direct relevance to the scope;
- recent and verifiable proof;
- named responsibility and outcome;
- no client leakage or unsupported credential.

## Anti-patterns
- stakeholder list without value exchange
- agreement without activation model
- partnership KPIs without governance

## Release condition
The line-of-service partner must approve the reasoning, the government evaluator must approve navigability and compliance, and the artifact director must approve the visual relationship before release.
