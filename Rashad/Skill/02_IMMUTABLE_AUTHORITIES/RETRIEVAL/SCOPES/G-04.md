| **G-04**        | **Procurement Strategy**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
|-----------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs the procurement strategy and operating model that maximises value from supplier relationships while managing risk and ensuring compliance.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Delivers**    | Procurement strategy, supplier segmentation, category strategy, procurement operating model, supplier relationship management framework, procurement performance framework.                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **How**         | Spend analysis, supplier segmentation, category strategy development, procurement process redesign, supplier relationship design, performance management design.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi government procurement must comply with Government Tenders and Procurement Law (Royal Decree M/128) and its implementing regulations. Local content requirements (Saudi Vision 2030 localisation programme) mandate minimum percentages of Saudi national participation in major contracts. Private sector procurement must consider Saudization implications of supplier workforce composition. |
