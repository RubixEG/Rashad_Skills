| **D-05**        | **Automation and Artificial Intelligence Strategy**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
|-----------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Defines and prioritises the use of automation and AI to improve efficiency, quality, and capability across the organisation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Delivers**    | Automation and AI opportunity assessment, use case prioritisation, technology selection framework, implementation roadmap, governance framework, ROI analysis.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **How**         | Process mapping for automation opportunity identification, AI use case development, build/buy/partner assessment, technology evaluation, pilot design, governance framework design.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Rashad is itself an example of this scope applied to consulting. Saudi government AI strategy must comply with SDAIA AI governance framework. AI applications in regulated sectors (healthcare, financial services, government services) require specific regulatory clearance. Arabic language AI capability is a critical requirement for Saudi citizen-facing applications. |
