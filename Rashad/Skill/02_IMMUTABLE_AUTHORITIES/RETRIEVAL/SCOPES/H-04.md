| **H-04**        | **Pricing Strategy**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs the pricing architecture that maximises value capture while maintaining competitive positioning and customer loyalty.                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Delivers**    | Pricing strategy, pricing architecture, price-value assessment, pricing model options, implementation plan, pricing governance.                                                                                                                                                                                      |

| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi and GCC pricing must account for: government-regulated pricing in multiple sectors (utilities, healthcare, fuel, education), cultural sensitivity to price differentiation by nationality, VAT (15%) compliance in pricing design, and the premium pricing dynamics of the GCC luxury and semi-luxury markets. |
