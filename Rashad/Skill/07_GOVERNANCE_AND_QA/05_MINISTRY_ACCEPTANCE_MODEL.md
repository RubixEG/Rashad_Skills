# Ministry Acceptance Readiness Model

## Purpose
Measure whether a proposal is easy for a government evaluator to accept, score, defend, contract, and govern.

## Dimensions — 100 points
- mandatory compliance: 20
- evaluator navigability: 10
- technical clarity and feasibility: 15
- evidence and credentials: 10
- implementation and acceptance: 10
- governance, risk, and quality: 10
- security, data, and regulatory alignment: 10
- team and knowledge transfer: 5
- commercial clarity and risk allocation: 5
- Arabic editorial and document professionalism: 5

## Readiness bands
- 92–100: ministry-ready subject to commercial and credential confirmation
- 85–91: strong but requires targeted remediation
- 75–84: material evaluator friction
- below 75: not ready for submission

## Hard blockers
- missing mandatory response;
- unresolved pass/fail condition;
- unsupported credential;
- inconsistent scope, price, team, or timeline;
- missing acceptance criteria;
- security or data obligation ignored;
- wrong section shell or compliance reference;
- unreadable or visually broken output.
