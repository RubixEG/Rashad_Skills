# 74 — Consulting Exhibit Synthesis Engine

Artifact truth answers **what relationships exist**. Exhibit synthesis answers **what one coherent consulting page should be**.

## Required output for critical pages
Generate **5 materially different exhibit hypotheses**. A hypothesis is not a primitive expression; it is a whole-page communication system containing:
- visual thesis;
- dominant form;
- focal object / decision;
- primary reading path;
- spatial encoding for every critical relationship;
- evidence-packing zones;
- implication/decision channel;
- visual mass plan;
- negative-space plan;
- intended asymmetry;
- reference grammar IDs;
- anti-patterns.

## Candidate diversity
Two hypotheses are different only if their dominant spatial logic or reading path differs. Color, icon or card styling changes do not count.

## Selection principle
The strongest exhibit is the one that makes the evaluator understand the argument and the relationship structure fastest while preserving evidence. Beauty cannot override relationship truth; technical correctness cannot override weak executive communication.
