| **E-07**        | **Culture Assessment and Transformation**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|-----------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Assesses the current organisational culture and designs the interventions required to shift it to the culture the strategy demands.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Delivers**    | Culture assessment report, target culture definition, culture gap analysis, culture transformation interventions, culture measurement framework.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **How**         | Culture diagnostic (surveys, interviews, observations), target culture definition, gap analysis, intervention design, leadership behaviour modelling, measurement system design.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi organisational culture has specific characteristics that must be assessed on their own terms: hierarchy respect, relationship-based decision making, group harmony orientation, and different approaches to time and deadline management. Culture transformation interventions must be designed with these characteristics in mind, not against a generic Western cultural template. |
