# Narrative Rhythm Engine

## Purpose
Prevent long decks from becoming repetitive, fragmented, or visually monotonous.

## Section rhythm
A strong section generally moves through:
1. section promise;
2. context or diagnostic;
3. conclusion or design principle;
4. solution or methodology;
5. evidence and detail;
6. implementation and control;
7. transition to the next section.

## Visual rhythm rules
- do not repeat the same layout family on consecutive pages;
- change the dominant information relationship at least every three pages;
- balance low, medium, and high density intentionally;
- use one focal visual per page;
- use dense tables only when the evaluator needs precise lookup;
- use whitespace as hierarchy, not as emptiness.

## Duplication rule
A page must add a new conclusion, proof, decision, or implementation detail. Rewording the previous page is not progression.
