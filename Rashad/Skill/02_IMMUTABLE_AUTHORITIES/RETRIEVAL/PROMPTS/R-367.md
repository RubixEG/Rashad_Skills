| Step 1. SCOUTING THESIS: Define what you are looking for with precision. Three components: (a) Technology Focus - which InsurTech sub-sectors are strategically relevant (parametric insurance, embedded insurance, claims automation, fraud detection, underwriting AI, distribution platforms, regulatory technology)? (b) Stage Focus - what company stage produces the best programme participants? (c) Geographic Focus - Saudi nationals first, GCC regional, then international companies seeking GCC entry? Each combination requires a different scouting approach.                                                                                                                                    |
| Step 2. SCOUTING CHANNELS: Design the active scouting channel mix. For each channel: specific source, contact approach, expected yield, and effort required. Channels to design: (a) Database Scouting - CB Insights, Crunchbase, PitchBook, Tracxn for systematic market mapping. (b) Ecosystem Partner Networks - accelerators, VCs, and incubators globally encountering InsurTech startups early. (c) Conference and Event Intelligence - which global InsurTech events produce highest-quality deal flow? (d) Academic and Research Scouting - university technology transfer offices. (e) Inbound Funnel Optimisation - designing the open application process to attract quality from the right markets. |
| Step 3. EVALUATION AND SCORING METHODOLOGY: Design the startup evaluation framework that converts raw deal flow into a scored, ranked pipeline. Assess: technology differentiation (is this genuinely novel?), market timing (is now the right time?), team capability (does this team have the skills to build and sell?), regulatory compatibility (can this solution operate within the existing or near-term regulatory framework?), and commercial viability (credible path to revenue in this market?). Design the scoring rubric with explicit weights per criterion.                                                                                                                                    |
| Step 4. ENGAGEMENT PROTOCOL: Design: the initial outreach message and value proposition (why should a global InsurTech startup engage with this programme?), the due diligence call structure (what is assessed in 30 minutes?), the application facilitation process (how does the scouting team reduce friction in applying?), and the relationship management system (how are warm contacts tracked until they are ready to apply?).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Step 5. INTELLIGENCE INFRASTRUCTURE: Design the scouting intelligence system: technology platform for tracking the startup pipeline, how scouting leads are recorded and scored, how market intelligence is shared with the programme design team to ensure hackathon challenges reflect emerging developments, and how the scouting database is maintained.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Step 6. PARTNERSHIP AND NETWORK DESIGN: Which organisations can provide reciprocal scouting value? Design the partner network: global InsurTech accelerators (Plug and Play Insurance, Startupbootcamp InsurTech), strategic investors (CVCs of insurance companies, sector-focused VCs), and peer innovation authorities (SAMA FinTech Lab, ADGM Innovation Hub, Bahrain EDB). For each partner: the nature of the relationship, deal flow exchange mechanism, and value offered in return.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
