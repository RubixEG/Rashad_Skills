| **K-03**        | **Innovation Programme Portfolio Management**                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Design and management of an integrated portfolio of innovation programmes including hackathons, accelerators, incubators, and community programmes as a connected system with shared pipeline and data infrastructure.                                         |
| **Delivers**    | Programme portfolio design, integrated 18-month programme calendar, participant journey maps by participant type, data and knowledge management system design, portfolio-level impact measurement framework                                                    |
| **How**         | Programme pipeline logic design with explicit conversion rates, programme-level design for each component, integration architecture, resource planning, calendar sequencing                                                                                    |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi innovation programme design must account for cultural calendar (Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha), Saudization requirements for programme participants and staff, and MISA registration pathway for international startups participating in programmes. |
