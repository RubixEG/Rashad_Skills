| **\<thinking_instructions\>**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Step 1. CONCEPTUAL DESIGN: Define the Innovation Lab's identity - what it stands for, who it is for, and what experience it creates. Answer three questions specifically: (a) Why would a top-tier startup choose this lab over a commercial co-working space? (b) Why would a senior incumbent executive come here rather than stay in their office? (c) Why would an international investor visit this lab as part of their GCC market assessment? These answers define the lab's competitive positioning.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Step 2. ZONE DESIGN: Design the functional zones the lab requires. For each zone: name, purpose, primary activities, minimum specifications, and the user behaviour it is designed to enable. Assess at minimum: (a) Design Thinking and Workshop Zone - structured facilitation and problem framing. (b) Prototyping and Demo Zone - building and testing solutions at speed. (c) Community and Networking Zone - ecosystem building and relationship development. (d) Programme Management Zone - running cohorts, tracking participants, managing data. (e) Leadership and Partnership Zone - high-level meetings with government, investors, and international partners. |
| Step 3. BILL OF MATERIALS FRAMEWORK: For each zone, specify the equipment, technology, furniture, and infrastructure categories required. Define the specification standard for each category: level of quality, key functional requirements, and procurement approach. Tier every item as Essential (lab cannot function without), Important (significantly enhances outcomes), or Aspirational (adds value if budget permits).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Step 4. TECHNOLOGY INFRASTRUCTURE: Design the digital layer of the lab - Innovation Management Platform requirements (idea submission, workflow management, participant tracking, output documentation), AV and presentation infrastructure, collaboration tools, and data management system for tracking programme outcomes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Step 5. OPERATING MODEL: Define: the team required to run it (roles, not headcount), the booking and access policy, the daily operating rhythm, visitor and event management process, and governance of who gets space and under what conditions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Step 6. BRAND AND IDENTITY INTEGRATION: Specify how the lab's physical identity connects to the parent organisation's brand while conveying innovation, openness, and ambition. Address: naming conventions, visual language principles, the experience from entrance to workspace, and the elements that make this lab distinctive for media and social content.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |

| Zone design must accommodate Saudi professional settings: gender-appropriate prayer facilities, professional break spaces, public-facing areas with VIP reception capability for senior government visitors.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
