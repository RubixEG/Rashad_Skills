
# Cover Topic Relevance Contract

STATUS: HARD COVER CONTENT AUTHORITY — v2.6.4.10

A cover image must be related to the **engagement thesis**, not merely to Saudi Arabia, the client sector, or “digital transformation” in the abstract.

## Required semantic mapping
Before selecting/generating the hero, extract:
- client mission / sector;
- engagement type;
- 3–6 dominant scope concepts;
- beneficiary/user context;
- technology/AI role if material;
- delivery environment;
- one cover-level visual thesis.

Then define 3–5 visible cues that can communicate those concepts without relying on text.

## Example — innovation + digital culture + AI
Strong cues may include:
- facilitated co-creation / innovation workshop;
- idea-to-prototype activity;
- service-design / beneficiary-testing moment;
- digital product/prototype interaction;
- data/AI intelligence supporting a service or decision;
- Saudi institutional innovation environment.

A generic man/woman walking away through a city, a random skyline, or passive stock photography **fails** unless the visual action itself communicates the project thesis.

## Topic relevance score /100
- engagement-specific semantic relevance: 30
- activity/action relevance: 20
- scope-cue coverage: 20
- differentiation from generic stock: 15
- government/client appropriateness: 10
- composition support for title/brand: 5

Critical cover threshold: **≥85**, with no score below 15/30 on engagement-specific relevance.
