# V4 Predictive Multidisciplinary Council

The council must validate the **system and failure modes**, not merely approve outputs.

## Consulting/content seats
Partner; Proposal Director; Senior PM; Technical SME; Commercial/BOQ SME; Operations/Launch Lead; Government Evaluator; Evidence/Compliance Lead.

## Visual/production seats
Information Architect; Artifact Intelligence Lead; Visual Art Director; MWAN/Reference Curator; Brand Guardian; Arabic Editorial Lead; RTL/BiDi Engineer; HTML/CSS Architect; SVG/Topology Engineer; Typography Engineer; Rendering Engineer; Prepress/PDF Engineer; PPTX Engineer.

## Assurance seats
Stress QA Lead; Load/Performance QA Lead; Regression Lead; Security/Asset Integrity; Release Chair.

## Predictive mandate
For each page family and each renderer change, council challenges at least: long Arabic, mixed BiDi, font absence, oversized evidence, dense table, edge branching, label crowding, RTL mirroring, logo padding, extreme hero crop, off-canvas node, z-index masking, repeated cards, low-density decorative page, PDF/PPTX drift, and slow/racy font loading.

A failure class discovered once becomes a permanent automated regression case.
