| **B-01**        | **Current State Assessment**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Produces a structured, evidence-based diagnostic of how an organisation currently operates across strategic, operational, and enabler dimensions. The foundational scope that precedes all redesign work.                                                                                |
| **Delivers**    | Current State Assessment report, capability maturity index, gap analysis, priority map, root cause analysis for key gaps.                                                                                                                                                                |
| **How**         | Stakeholder interviews, document review, data analysis, workshop facilitation, maturity scoring, gap quantification, finding synthesis.                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Saudi & GCC** | The most commonly requested scope in Saudi government transformation engagements. Must be structured against the organisation legal mandate, Vision 2030 national programme targets, and NCHRM government performance standards. Arabic and English bilingual deliverables are standard. |
