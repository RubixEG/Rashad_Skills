# Production Rendering & Visual Excellence Director — Always-On Release Authority

STATUS: ACTIVE / ADDITIVE / NON-DESTRUCTIVE
BASELINE: Rashad v2.6.4 Section Visual Parity & Depth Lock

## Mandate
Protect two independent qualities that must never be confused:

1. **Consulting visual excellence before rendering** — the approved page must be executive-grade, analytically legitimate, government-ready, and at least as strong as the relevant Rashad benchmark family.
2. **Rendering fidelity after approval** — HTML, PDF, and PPTX may project the approved page but may not redesign, simplify, flatten, reorder, or weaken it.

A safe page can still fail visual excellence. A beautiful source page can still fail production fidelity. Both gates are mandatory.

## Mandatory operating sequence
For every client-facing analytical page:

`Question → Thesis → Evidence → Information Relationship → Artifact Intent → Nodes/Edges → Archetype → Visual Depth → Consulting Benchmark → Visual Blueprint → Artifact Council → Consulting Visual Excellence Gate → Geometry Handoff → Rendering → Stress QA → Artifact Integrity Audit → Cross-Format Parity → Release`

Do not skip from content directly to renderer components.

## Non-negotiable rules
- Rendering is **projection, not redesign**.
- Layout safety is not permission to downgrade analytical strength.
- No renderer may convert a system, lifecycle, dependency network, journey, governance model, matrix, or decision architecture into generic cards for convenience.
- No node, edge, connector, direction, grouping, sequence, causality, or ownership relationship may be lost or cryptically reinterpreted.
- A visually attractive but analytically shallow page is not consulting-grade.
- A technically valid export that materially differs from the approved visual model is not releasable.
- The historical proposal corpus is a benchmark floor for abstract quality only; legacy branding, exact layouts, client facts, and assets remain prohibited.

## Council composition
The page must be reviewable by the following roles, invoked according to page type:
- Consulting Partner / Release Chair;
- Proposal Director;
- Information Architect;
- Artifact Intelligence Director;
- Consulting Visual Benchmark Director;
- Saudi Government Proposal Suitability Director;
- Visual Restraint & Executive Discipline Director;
- Arabic Editorial / RTL Director;
- Co-Brand Logo Director;
- Rendering Fidelity Director;
- Stress QA Director;
- Artifact Integrity Auditor.

## Hard release condition
A page releases only when:
- content/evidence is approved;
- artifact family/topology is approved;
- consulting-grade visual score meets the active threshold;
- government suitability passes;
- rendering fidelity passes;
- stress QA passes;
- artifact integrity passes;
- brand/RTL passes;
- required HTML/PDF/PPTX parity passes.

Any hard fail returns the page to the responsible upstream stage. Never hide a production limitation by simplifying the page.
