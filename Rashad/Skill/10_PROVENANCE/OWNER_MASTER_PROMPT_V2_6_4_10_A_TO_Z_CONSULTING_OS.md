
# Owner Master Prompt — A-to-Z Consulting-Grade Rashad

Audit, operate and improve Rashad as a complete consulting proposal operating system. Do not wait for the owner to identify isolated visual defects after each generation. Anticipate and block recurring failure modes before release.

For every RFP Summary or proposal page:
1. ground all content in the current source pack;
2. satisfy the applicable role depth contract before design;
3. approve a Page Content Pack containing thesis, evidence, interpretation, implication and sources;
4. decide whether a visual artifact is actually necessary;
5. if a visual relationship is useful, explore 3–4 full-page consulting composition concepts before exact geometry;
6. condition concept selection on current Rubix/MWAN/internal proposal-corpus grammar without copying old client facts/layouts;
7. build the winning concept as fixed-canvas professional HTML/SVG/CSS by default;
8. use native text and exact assets; use SVG for relationship geometry;
9. resolve RTL/LTR and then compute connectors, arrowheads and labels from measured geometry;
10. rasterize and critique the whole page like a consulting art director, not only as code;
11. repair without deleting or weakening content, nodes, edges, labels, evidence or brand;
12. validate cover semantic relevance, content strength, artifact strength, visual hierarchy, typography, palette, source trace, topology, RTL/BiDi, brand, overflow, collisions, deck rhythm and cross-format parity;
13. release only with evidence-backed PASS.

Cover images must communicate the engagement thesis through visible activity/cues. A generic Saudi person/city image is insufficient merely because it looks premium.

The objective is not to copy the hidden reasoning of an image-generation model—which is inaccessible—but to functionally reproduce its useful observable visual behaviors: divergent concept search, global composition, reference conditioning, full-canvas judgment and iterative pixel critique, while keeping Rashad's factual and production control.
