| **F-01**        | **Enterprise Risk Management**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs and implements the enterprise risk management framework that enables the organisation to identify, assess, prioritise, and mitigate risks systematically.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Delivers**    | ERM framework, risk appetite statement, risk register, risk governance structure, risk monitoring and reporting system, risk culture assessment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **How**         | Risk identification workshops, risk assessment methodology design, risk register development, risk appetite statement development, governance design, monitoring system design.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi government ERM must align with the Saudi Audit Bureau requirements and the Government Risk Management Framework. For financial sector entities: SAMA has specific risk management requirements. Vision 2030 has created new risk categories (reputational risk from national programme delays, talent risk from Saudization requirements) that must be reflected in ERM frameworks. |
