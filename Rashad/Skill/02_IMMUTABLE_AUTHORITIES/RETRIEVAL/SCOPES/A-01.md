| **A-01**        | **Corporate Strategy**                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Defines the organisation overall direction, the markets it will compete in, and the logic of its portfolio of activities. Answers: where should we play and why?                                                              |
| **Delivers**    | Corporate strategy document, strategic position statement, portfolio analysis, strategic choices cascade, board-ready strategy summary.                                                                                       |
| **How**         | External environment analysis, internal capability assessment, strategic scenario development, option generation and evaluation, strategic choice recommendation.                                                             |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Vision 2030 alignment is mandatory. Every corporate strategy in Saudi Arabia must connect to the relevant national programme (NTP) and articulate the organisation contribution to national economic diversification targets. |
