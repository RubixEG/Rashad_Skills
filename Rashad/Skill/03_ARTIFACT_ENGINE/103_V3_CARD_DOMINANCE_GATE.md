# 103 - V3 Card Dominance Gate

Cards are allowed as supporting components. Card-only thinking is blocked.

Fail when an artifact-led or hybrid page is dominated by equal-sized independent rounded rectangles without a higher-order relationship, such as:
- operating spine;
- journey rail;
- risk matrix;
- weighted evaluation architecture;
- dependency graph;
- layered system;
- governance decision model.

Legitimate card use:
- peer comparison;
- controlled evidence panels inside a larger artifact;
- small support labels attached to a system map;
- formal table/matrix where matrix is the artifact.
