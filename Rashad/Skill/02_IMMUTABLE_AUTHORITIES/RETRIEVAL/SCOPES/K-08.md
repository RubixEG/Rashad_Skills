| **K-08**        | **Innovation Community Building**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
|-----------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Design and management of an innovation ecosystem community that sustains engagement between programme cycles and builds relationships between startups, incumbents, investors, and researchers.                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Delivers**    | Community thesis, architecture design (membership tiers and engagement model), 12-month programming calendar, digital infrastructure specification, growth strategy, community health metrics framework                                                                                                                                          |
| **How**         | Audience segmentation and value proposition design, programming design, platform selection and setup, founding member recruitment, growth strategy execution                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Saudi & GCC** | WhatsApp remains the dominant community management tool in Saudi Arabia - the digital infrastructure must acknowledge this. The concept of Majlis (open gathering for discussion) provides a culturally resonant format for innovation community events. The regulatory authority convening power is the most valuable community-building asset. |
