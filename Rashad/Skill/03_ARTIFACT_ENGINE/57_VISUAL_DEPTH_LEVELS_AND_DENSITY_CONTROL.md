# Visual Depth Levels & Density Control

STATUS: ACTIVE PAGE-DESIGN CONTROL

## Purpose
Prevent every page from having the same density or visual ambition.

### VD-1 Executive Synthesis
Use for decision/synthesis pages.
- one dominant thesis;
- one primary artifact or decision signal;
- limited supporting evidence;
- high whitespace;
- rapid comprehension.

### VD-2 Analytical Page
Use for normal consulting analysis.
- one primary relationship artifact;
- supporting evidence and implications;
- moderate controlled density;
- clear hierarchy.

### VD-3 Dense Evidence Page
Use when the evaluator needs detailed evidence, criteria, requirements, or structured comparison.
- higher information density;
- strong navigation and grouping;
- readable evidence locators;
- no tiny text or uncontrolled mega-table.

### VD-4 Technical / Commercial Drilldown
Use for technical architecture, BOQ/commercial mapping, contractual obligations, detailed team/evidence controls, or other specialist analysis.
- precision over decoration;
- explicit labels and relationships;
- may span multiple pages when required;
- native tables/diagrams only when they preserve readability.

## Rules
- Density level is chosen from evaluator need and evidence volume, not aesthetics alone.
- One logical role may use multiple depth levels across its expanded pages.
- Do not force VD-4 content into VD-1 geometry.
- Do not decorate VD-1 until it becomes visually noisy.
- If content exceeds the approved density capacity, split the page before reducing readability.
