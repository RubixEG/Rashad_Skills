| **C-06**        | **Valuation**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Values a business, asset, or investment opportunity using multiple methodologies appropriate to the asset type and purpose.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Delivers**    | Valuation report, DCF model, comparable company analysis, precedent transaction analysis, valuation summary and recommendation.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **How**         | Financial model development, DCF valuation, comparable company analysis, precedent transaction research, valuation synthesis, sensitivity analysis.                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi market valuation must apply GCC-specific discount rates, consider Vision 2030 policy tailwinds or headwinds to specific sectors, and reference GCC-comparable transaction multiples. Government entity valuations (for privatisation or PPP) require specific public sector valuation methodologies. |
