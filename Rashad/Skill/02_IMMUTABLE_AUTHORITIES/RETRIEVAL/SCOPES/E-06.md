| **E-06**        | **Compensation and Benefits Design**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs or reviews the compensation and benefits architecture to attract, retain, and motivate the required talent.                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Delivers**    | Compensation benchmarking analysis, grading and banding framework, base salary structure, variable compensation design, benefits framework, total reward strategy.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **How**         | Market benchmarking, internal equity analysis, job evaluation, compensation structure design, variable pay design, benefits assessment, total reward communication.                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi compensation design must comply with Saudi Labour Law minimum wage requirements, GOSI contribution rules, and Nitaqat Saudization requirements. End-of-service benefit (EOSB) calculations and annual leave entitlements are legally mandated. Government compensation follows civil service grade scales governed by NCHRM. |
