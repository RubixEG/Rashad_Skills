# Golden Visual Master Director — Page-by-Page Production Strategy

STATUS: ACTIVE HARD VISUAL-PRODUCTION AUTHORITY — v2.6.4.4

## Mission
Restore the visual strength of full-page image generation without surrendering factual accuracy, exact brand control, RTL, or deck continuity.

## Core principle
The image model may create the **complete visual composition** of a page as a `GOLDEN_VISUAL_MASTER`, but it is not the authority for exact production text, tender numbers, page numbers, Rubix/client logos, source labels, or factual UI/data.

The final client-facing page is:

`Golden Visual Master underlay + exact native production overlay`.

The renderer is not asked to rebuild the visual artifact from cards/HTML primitives. It must preserve the approved master and add only governed native layers.

## Why this mode exists
A native composer that cannot reproduce the approved visual artifact may degrade strong systems, journeys, maps, matrices and decision architectures into cards/tables. v2.6.4.4 treats that downgrade as a production failure and reduces the composer's job instead of weakening the artifact.

## Allowed contextual scene content
When materially relevant, Golden Visual Masters and cover heroes may include:
- Saudi flags in environmental/contextual scenes;
- people and institutional environments;
- laptops/tablets/phones/screens;
- dashboard-like or platform-like visual cues;
- architecture, service environments, operations, data/AI/network cues;
- contextual signage or environmental text that is incidental rather than proposal copy.

These elements are visual context only. They may not be cited as facts or treated as exact official UI/data/brand evidence.

## Still forbidden as generated authority
- exact Rubix logo;
- exact client logo/official seal required for brand identity;
- final proposal copy / factual labels / page number;
- tender IDs, dates, scores, prices or other authoritative values;
- fabricated screenshots presented as real client systems;
- generated text used as the factual source of a claim.

## Anti-card default
Equal-card grids are not a safe default. The page generation brief must begin from the approved information relationship and artifact family. Cards are allowed only where discrete categorical grouping is genuinely the best relationship model.

## Continuity rule
No page is generated from prompt memory alone. Every page generation uses the frozen Deck Visual DNA, the approved Style Anchor Set, its Section Anchor, and the adjacent-page continuity record where available.

## v2.6.4.9 mode priority
**VERSION 4 UPDATE:** Golden Visual Master / approved whole-page visual board is the visual-design truth for artifact-heavy pages. Production execution is fidelity-based: semantic-native reconstruction when it preserves the approved visual, or governed hybrid GVM+native overlays when native reconstruction would weaken it. HTML/SVG is not the source of visual imagination.
