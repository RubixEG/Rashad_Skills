# 79 — Consulting Exhibit Quality Score (CEQS)

CEQS is independent from Artifact Truth and from render safety.

## CEQS /100
- Answer-first argument clarity — 15
- Whole-page exhibit synthesis — 15
- Artifact integration (not merely presence) — 15
- Evidence integration & proximity — 10
- Spatial hierarchy & focal dominance — 10
- Visual mass / balance / useful density — 10
- Executive scanability — 10
- Consulting reference-grammar compliance — 5
- Brand restraint / typography discipline — 5
- Anti-template originality / deck rhythm — 5

## Release algebra
`ARTIFACT_TRUTH >= 90` AND `CEQS >= 90` AND `UNIFIED_QA = PASS`.
The legacy ≥85 Artifact Truth floor is superseded for the current v6.2.2/OpenAI Runtime program.

A technically perfect page with CEQS 72 is rejected. A beautiful page with Artifact Truth 70 is rejected. Scores cannot compensate for hard blockers.
