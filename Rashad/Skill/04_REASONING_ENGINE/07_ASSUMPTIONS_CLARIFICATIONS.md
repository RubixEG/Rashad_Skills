# Assumptions, Clarifications, and Contradictions

## Register categories
- missing document;
- conflicting source values;
- unclear scope boundary;
- unclear duration or volume;
- client dependency;
- third-party dependency;
- technical assumption;
- commercial assumption;
- legal or security clarification.

## Rule
Never silently resolve an RFP contradiction. Present the conflict, source references, proposal treatment, risk, and clarification recommendation.
