| **B-06**        | **Shared Services Design**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|-----------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs and implements a shared services centre that consolidates common functions (HR, Finance, IT, Procurement) across multiple business units or entities.                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Delivers**    | Shared services business case, service scope definition, service catalogue, governance model, operating model, technology requirements, transition plan, performance management framework.                                                                                                                         |
| **How**         | Service scope assessment, make vs buy analysis, shared services model design, service level agreement development, governance design, location and staffing analysis, transition planning.                                                                                                                         |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi shared services design must account for Saudization targets across the shared services centre, GOSI requirements, and Saudi Labour Law implications for staff transitions. For government entities: shared services design often requires an enabling regulatory instrument before implementation can begin. |
