| **I-03**        | **Benefits Realisation Management**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs and manages the framework through which a programme delivers and demonstrates its promised benefits.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Delivers**    | Benefits realisation framework, benefits register, benefits measurement methodology, benefits reporting system, value realisation milestones.                                                                                                                                               |
| **How**         | Benefit identification and mapping, benefit baseline establishment, measurement methodology design, tracking system design, reporting framework development.                                                                                                                                |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Benefits realisation in Saudi government must include non-financial benefits: citizen service improvement, national KPI contribution, Vision 2030 target achievement. MOF increasingly requires benefits tracking as a condition of major budget allocations for transformation programmes. |
