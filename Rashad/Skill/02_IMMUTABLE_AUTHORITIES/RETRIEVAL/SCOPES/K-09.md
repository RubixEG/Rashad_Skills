| **K-09**        | **Innovation Impact Measurement**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Design and operationalisation of a comprehensive impact measurement framework for innovation programmes covering financial return, ecosystem development, sector transformation, and Vision 2030 national contribution.                                                                                                                        |
| **Delivers**    | Theory of change logic model, measurement framework (four dimensions: financial, ecosystem, sector, national), attribution methodology, data collection architecture, reporting architecture (four report types), benchmark design                                                                                                             |
| **How**         | Theory of change development, metric design per dimension, attribution methodology design, data collection system design, reporting template design, benchmark identification                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi government bodies require impact reporting that connects to Vision 2030 KPIs. Financial Sector Development Programme, Quality of Life Programme, and National Transformation Programme have specific measurable targets that innovation impact frameworks should reference for institutional credibility with MOF and senior leadership. |
