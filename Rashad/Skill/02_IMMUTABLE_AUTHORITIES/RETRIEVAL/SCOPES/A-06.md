| **A-06**        | **Portfolio Strategy**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Evaluates and optimises the mix of businesses, products, or programmes across an organisation portfolio against strategic and financial criteria.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Delivers**    | Portfolio map and assessment, investment priority ranking, portfolio optimisation recommendation, capital allocation framework, portfolio governance model.                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **How**         | Portfolio performance assessment, strategic fit analysis (BCG matrix, GE-McKinsey), synergy identification, investment case per portfolio element, portfolio optimisation scenario development.                                                                                                                                     |
| **Saudi & GCC** | For Saudi government: portfolio strategy applies to programme portfolios within a ministry (which national programmes to prioritise), to sovereign wealth fund investment portfolios (sector allocation and return optimisation), and to government-owned enterprise portfolios (privatisation candidates, divestiture sequencing). |
