| **B-05**        | **Post-Merger Integration**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs and manages the integration of two or more organisations following a merger, acquisition, or consolidation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Delivers**    | Integration strategy, integration workplan, Day 1 readiness plan, operating model design for the combined entity, synergy realisation tracker, integration governance.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **How**         | Integration assessment, Day 1 readiness planning, integration workstream design, operating model design for combined entity, synergy identification and quantification, integration programme management.                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi consolidation programmes (driven by Vision 2030 sector restructuring) frequently involve integration of government-related entities with specific legal structure requirements. MISA, MOC, and sector-specific regulatory approvals are typically required. Cultural integration in Saudi organisations carries particular complexity when combining entities with different Saudization levels or workforce compositions. |
