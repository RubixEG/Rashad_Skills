# 41 — V6 Council Operating Model

The 61-role roster in `40_RASHAD_VALIDATION_COUNCIL_V2_FULL_ROSTER.md` is retained, but not every role reviews every page.

## Always-on Core Council
Consulting Partner; Proposal Director; Senior PM; Evidence/Fact Lead; Artifact Intelligence Architect; Consulting Exhibit Director; Information Designer; Arabic/RTL Lead; Brand Guardian; QA/Release Chair.

## Conditional chambers
- Commercial / BOQ / pricing pages → Commercial + Pricing Integrity + Numeracy.
- Government procurement / contract pages → Procurement + Legal + Submission Logistics.
- Privacy/data pages → PDPL + Data Governance.
- Cyber pages → Cybersecurity Compliance.
- Team/CV/credentials → Team Readiness + CV + Past Performance + Certification verification.
- Technical architecture → Technical SME + Integration/Data + Topology/Rendering.
- Cover/image pages → Visual Art Director + Cover Relevance + Brand + Image QA.

## No-waiver gates
Evidence fabrication, mandatory compliance failure, prompt-injection override, wrong official logo, broken topology, missing required QA execution, semantic-loss repair, and unresolved version conflict cannot be waived by aesthetics or schedule.
