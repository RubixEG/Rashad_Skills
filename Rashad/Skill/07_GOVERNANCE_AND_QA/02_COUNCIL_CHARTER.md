# Integrated Proposal Council Charter

## Status
This is a simulated council used to create independent professional lenses. It is not an external endorsement.

## Council structure

### A. Executive proposal council
- Managing Partner / final bid owner
- Public-Sector Partner
- Proposal Strategy Partner
- Commercial and Value Partner
- Delivery and Transformation Partner

### B. Service-line partner council
- Strategy and policy
- Operating model and organization
- Transformation and PMO
- Operations and process excellence
- Digital and technology strategy
- Data, analytics, and AI
- Enterprise architecture and integration
- Cybersecurity, privacy, risk, and compliance
- Finance, value, and commercial
- Human capital and change
- Beneficiary/customer experience and service design
- Marketing, communications, and brand
- Market research and economics
- Partnerships, investment, and PPP
- Sustainability and ESG
- Events and activation
- Quality, performance, and compliance
- Sector and public-policy specialists

### C. Government evaluator council
- procurement and pass/fail evaluator
- technical evaluator
- financial evaluator
- legal and contracts reviewer
- cybersecurity reviewer
- data and privacy reviewer
- digital-government and enterprise-architecture reviewer
- PMO and acceptance reviewer
- local-content and capability-transfer reviewer
- Arabic-government communication reviewer

### D. Vendor and architecture council
- cloud architecture
- enterprise applications and ERP
- CRM and beneficiary service
- workflow and ITSM
- data platform and analytics
- AI and model operations
- integration and APIs
- cybersecurity
- observability and operations
- vendor-neutrality and exit strategy

### E. Artifact and production council
- storyline architect
- information designer
- creative director
- Arabic editorial director
- icon and imagery director
- PowerPoint engineer
- HTML/PDF renderer engineer
- accessibility and QA reviewer

## Council behavior
Each lens must challenge, not merely agree. Reviews must expose contradictions, weak claims, missing evidence, unrealistic commitments, generic artifacts, visual defects, and evaluator friction.
