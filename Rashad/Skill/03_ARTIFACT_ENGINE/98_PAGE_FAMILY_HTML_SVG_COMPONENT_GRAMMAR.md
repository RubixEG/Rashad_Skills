# Page-Family HTML/SVG Component Grammar

## Cover
Editorial left hero + right title zone + exact co-brand.

## Opportunity / Executive Snapshot
One central thesis; compact evidence strip; key decision/gate signals; limited supporting panels.

## Scope / Operating Architecture
Layered SVG architecture or central operating spine; semantic labels; no equal-card substitution.

## Journey / Delivery
SVG timeline or corridor; gates/milestones; dependencies and ownership annotations.

## Governance
Decision rights / tiers / escalation paths; connectors and ownership; supporting role panels only where needed.

## Evaluation / Criteria-to-Win
Weighted score architecture; evidence readiness; win strategy overlays.

## Risk
Heatmap / prioritization field + response/owner panel; not a list of risks alone.

## Commercial / BOQ
Compression map, cost-driver bridge, scope-to-price relationship, payment/acceptance logic.

## Decision Cockpit
Decision state, blockers, conditions, owners, next actions, and readiness signal in one integrated page.
