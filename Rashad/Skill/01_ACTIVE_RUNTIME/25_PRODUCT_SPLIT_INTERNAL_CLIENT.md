# Product Split — Internal Pursuit Brief vs Client-Facing Understanding

## Hard separation

`INTERNAL_PURSUIT_BRIEF` and `CLIENT_FACING_RFP_UNDERSTANDING` are different products with different audiences and disclosure rules.

### Internal pursuit brief
Audience: Partner, Director, bid team, PM, commercial, operations, SMEs.
May show: source gaps/conflicts, win strategy, commercial commitments and risks, assumptions, internal risks, clarification strategy, proposal Gantt, delivery Gantt, procurement maturity, bid decision.

### Client-facing understanding
Audience: client/evaluator.
May show only externally appropriate understanding, scope, delivery and value logic. It must not disclose margin, internal weaknesses, source-control mechanics, bid/no-bid deliberation, red-team findings, procurement criticism or private team debate.

## Contamination gate
No internal-only page may be copied into the client-facing product without an explicit disclosure review and rewrite.
