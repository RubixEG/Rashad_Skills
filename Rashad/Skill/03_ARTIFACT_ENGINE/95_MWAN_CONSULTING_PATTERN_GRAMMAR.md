# MWAN Consulting Pattern Grammar

MWAN is a reference quality floor. Extract the **grammar**, not the client/template.

Recurring successful pattern:
- strong right-aligned page title / context line;
- one decisive thesis or analytical question near the top;
- dominant artifact occupying the visual center;
- supporting metrics, proof points or evaluator signals around the artifact;
- controlled use of small panels/cards as substructure;
- bottom implication / decision / risk band;
- dense but ordered information;
- restrained brand magenta with neutral structure;
- exact, visible co-brand signature;
- purposeful whitespace, not empty pages.

Do not reproduce MWAN client data, old logos or exact layouts. Translate the grammar into the current engagement.
