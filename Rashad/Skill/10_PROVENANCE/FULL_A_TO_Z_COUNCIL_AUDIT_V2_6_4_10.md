## v2.6.4.9 gaps found
- conflicting active wording remained between image-first and HTML-default production routes;
- cover rules required topic relevance but did not operationalize semantic relevance strongly enough;
- content depth existed, but there was no single content-before-artifact constitution across the production flow;
- visual reasoning emulation was present but not bound into one complete A-to-Z runtime constitution;
- proposal corpus knowledge was not distilled into a current quality-weighted authority;
- HTML/SVG composer lacked an explicit layout-aware DOM/connector/label execution contract in the highest routing path;
- stress repair needed explicit semantic-signature invariants to guarantee QA cannot weaken artifacts/content;
- deck-level repetition/rhythm and content-artifact balance needed harder release gates;
- public-sector evaluator usability needed an explicit static e-proposal scanability standard.

## Decisions
All above gaps are closed at the knowledge/governance layer in v2.6.4.10.

## Residual implementation truth
The portable Skill is not the browser runtime. It can be complete as the A-to-Z reasoning/governance specification while executable PASS still depends on a runtime actually implementing the contracts and producing evidence.

## Verdict
**GO — A-to-Z Knowledge/Governance Constitution.**
**CONDITIONAL for production execution until executable browser/QA evidence exists for the current run.**
