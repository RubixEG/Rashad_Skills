# Final Decision Ledger — Distilled from Full ChatGPT Context and Version History

This ledger is the runtime-safe distillation of the complete `Chatgpt Context.md` chronology (49,874 lines / ~1.09M characters), the previous context-mirror extraction, all detected Rashad version branches, and the newest 2026-08-11 risk/clarification correction.

## ACTIVE decisions

### D-001 — Exact Rashad corpus
The 388-prompt / 96-scope Rashad corpus is immutable analytical intelligence. Exact prompt text is retrieved, not recreated.

### D-002 — Proposal architecture responsibilities are separated
Reasoning, proposal structure, information design/artifacts, branding, and rendering are separate concerns. The deck/brand must not decide analytical logic.

### D-003 — RFP Summary is a pre-proposal consulting decision brief
It is not the final Executive Summary and not a document checklist. It is adaptive, issue-led, question/thesis/evidence/implication driven.

### D-004 — Source Coverage split by product
Source completeness and confidence remain internal QA/evidence controls. They are not default client/executive Summary slides.

### D-005 — Summary adapts to pack completeness
`SCOPE_ONLY`, `PARTIAL_RFP_PACK`, `FULL_RFP_PACK`. Never invent absent annex categories to fill a fixed template.

### D-006 — Summary slide count is dynamic
No fixed 15/20-page requirement. A page exists only for a material executive/evaluator question.

### D-007 — Engagement reset is mandatory
No prior client facts, logos, sector imagery, risks, or numbers carry into a new RFP.

### D-008 — Artifact-first
Question → thesis → evidence → relationship → artifact intent/family → semantic nodes/edges → geometry → production. Generic cards are not a default solution.

### D-009 — Artifact before Safety
Safety repairs layout after artifact lock. Safety cannot change family, delete nodes/edges/evidence, or weaken the page to fit.

### D-010 — Historical proposals are temporary reference only
They may inform abstract visual storytelling but cannot become production authority or leak client-specific content/assets.

### D-011 — Image models are visual ingredients, not analytical authority
Full-page generated visuals may be concept references; final analytical text, exact numbers, official identifiers, logos, BOQ, risk/compliance, architecture, timelines and other exact structures are native/vector/hybrid.

### D-012 — Arabic/RTL is physical, not cosmetic
Ordered sequences use explicit physical coordinates. Mixed English/technical tokens stay LTR inside RTL. Numeral/date style follows the authoritative RFP unless explicitly overridden.

### D-013 — Current Rubix identity only
No old/generated logo, no legacy client identity, no black/near-black slide backgrounds.

### D-014 — Councils must execute, not role-play
A routed role must produce a finding. Do not invent roles to match a historic claimed 67-role count.

### D-015 — Risks are bid/delivery/launch/operations risks
Newest 2026-08-11 correction. Risks must affect solution, price/margin, time, staffing, acceptance, launch, operations, responsibility split, or material contractual delivery exposure. Tender-document observations and generic risks are rejected.

### D-016 — Dedicated Risk Council
Core views: Partner, Director, Senior PM/Team Manager, Operations & Launch Lead. Specialists join conditionally.

### D-017 — Clarifications are scope-expert decision questions
Newest 2026-08-11 correction. Core: Partner, Director, Senior PM chair, workstream PM(s), Associate/Analyst, conditional Operations/SMEs. Every question must pass the A-vs-B proposal-change test.

### D-018 — Etimad-awareness gate
Ordinary Etimad mechanics are not clarification questions for an experienced bidding team unless the competition introduces an exception/conflict.

### D-019 — Executive Summary and CEO Letter are late products
They synthesize approved downstream sections. Compliance Register begins early; final Compliance Matrix closes after page references stabilize.

### D-020 — Context must be selectively loaded
Raw ChatGPT transcript, old versions, generated engagement state, all historical outputs, and all prompt libraries must not be injected into every generation request.
