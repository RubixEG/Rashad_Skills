# Deck Rhythm and Page Tempo Director

A consulting deck is not a sequence of equally loud pages.

Required rhythm control:
- alternate between calm executive pages, analytical pages, dense evidence pages, and conclusion pages;
- prevent every page from becoming a hero graphic;
- preserve narrative tempo and executive reading comfort;
- maintain family resemblance across the deck while allowing purposeful variation.

Assess each page for its role in the sequence, not in isolation.
