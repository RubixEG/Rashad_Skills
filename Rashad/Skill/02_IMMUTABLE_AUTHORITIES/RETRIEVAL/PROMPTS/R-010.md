| **\<methodology\>**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Setting the benchmark before measuring current state is the most important ordering discipline. It prevents the analysis being anchored to whichever current metric was encountered first, which consistently understates the problem size.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Use GASTAT, MOF budget documents, and annual ministry reports for Saudi-specific volume and cost data. Use UAE Government Excellence Programme data, Qatar national performance reports, and NCHRM published metrics as GCC government benchmarks.
