# 30 — Visual Corpus Learning Policy

STATUS: HARD REFERENCE POLICY — v2.4
PURPOSE: Learn from strong visual examples without turning them into templates or importing contamination.

## Accepted source types
- historical Rubix proposals;
- previously generated concept pages;
- approved external consulting visual references;
- current Rubix brand/deck examples;
- engagement-specific reference visuals explicitly supplied by the user.

## Four-way disposition
Every reference must be classified:
1. `AUTHORITY` — only current approved brand/rules/evidence qualify.
2. `PATTERN_INTELLIGENCE` — abstract relationship/composition/rhythm may be learned.
3. `REFERENCE_ONLY` — useful for comparison, never copied.
4. `REJECT/QUARANTINE` — misleading, contaminated, old identity, weak analytical design, or unsafe.

## What may be learned
- information relationship;
- evaluator journey;
- composition hierarchy;
- focal-point strategy;
- whitespace behavior;
- section rhythm;
- density balance;
- how methodology becomes a journey;
- how governance becomes decision rights;
- how evidence becomes a proof chain;
- how parallel workstreams become a roadmap;
- how risk becomes prioritization/action;
- how a system map expresses flows and dependencies.

## What may never be learned as authority
- old logos;
- old colors/theme;
- exact old slide coordinates;
- client-specific facts/assets;
- copied Arabic text;
- copied numbers/dates;
- exact historical compositions;
- decorative motifs with no analytical purpose;
- image-generated text/logos as production truth.

## Decomposition record
For each useful reference, capture:
`purpose → question → relationship → archetype → hierarchy → focal point → density → successful pattern → weakness → reusable abstract rule`.

Never record “copy slide X.”

## Portable-core rule
Reference images themselves are not bundled in the core skill unless they are current approved brand assets. The portable skill stores extracted Markdown intelligence only.


## v2.4 historical proposal corpus integration
The 2026-08-11 `Proposals.zip` reverse-engineering is governed by `34_HISTORICAL_PROPOSAL_NARRATIVE_INTELLIGENCE.md` through `38_HISTORICAL_TO_CURRENT_TRANSLATION_GATE.md` and `07_GOVERNANCE_AND_QA/30_HISTORICAL_CORPUS_INTEGRATION_GATE.md`. Raw proposal files remain external and reference-only.
