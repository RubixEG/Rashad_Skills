Every session, every mentor connection, every milestone gate exists to move startups faster toward market. |

| **\<thinking_instructions\>**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Step 1. COHORT DESIGN: Define the ideal cohort. Cohort size (10-15 is optimal for intensive support without diluting mentor attention), stage profile (be specific about revenue and traction thresholds for eligibility), technology focus (InsurTech sub-sectors to prioritise based on sector challenge areas), geographic scope (Saudi nationals, GCC region, international companies seeking Saudi market entry), and team composition requirements. Design the application and selection process with explicit scoring rubric.                                                                                                   |
| Step 2. CURRICULUM ARCHITECTURE: Design the programme curriculum in weekly modules across the programme duration (typically 12-16 weeks). For each module: the learning objective, format (workshop, clinic, masterclass, peer learning), facilitator type (specialist mentor, guest speaker, programme staff), and the output the startup must produce. Non-negotiable curriculum elements: regulatory navigation (how to work with the regulator, sandbox pathway), distribution strategy (how to sell to incumbents), investor readiness (pitch and due diligence preparation), and data and compliance architecture.               |
| Step 3. MENTORSHIP MODEL: Design the mentorship structure. Distinguish between: (a) Programme Mentors - assigned to each startup for the duration, meeting weekly, accountable for startup progress. (b) Expert Mentors - brought in on-demand for specific technical, legal, or commercial questions. (c) Industry Mentors - executives from incumbent companies who can open doors and validate solutions. For each type: profile requirements, engagement model, session structure, and accountability mechanism.                                                                                                                   |
| Step 4. MILESTONE GATE DESIGN: Design the explicit go/no-go gates. At minimum: Entry Gate (who gets in), Mid-Programme Gate (who continues vs is counselled out), and Graduation Gate (who has achieved programme outcomes). For each gate: the specific criteria, scoring method, decision-maker, and what happens to startups that do not pass.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Step 5. REGULATORY ACCESS PATHWAY: Design the specific mechanism by which accelerator participants access the regulatory body's expertise, data, and sandbox capabilities during the programme. This is the unique value that no private accelerator can replicate. Design: regulatory office hours schedule, sandbox fast-track application process, and data sharing protocol.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Step 6. GRADUATION AND POST-PROGRAMME SUPPORT: Design the Demo Day structure (investor audience composition, pitch format, evaluation criteria), the post-programme support model (what continues for 6 months after graduation), and the explicit pathway into the Incubator programme for startups that need deeper venture building support.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **\</thinking_instructions\>**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **\<task\>**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Design a complete accelerator programme from cohort definition through graduation and post-programme support. Every design element must connect to the singular outcome: startups ready to raise their next round or close their first major commercial deal.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **\</task\>**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **\<methodology\>**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Apply the Lean Startup methodology as the programme operating framework: every milestone gate should be designed around a key assumption the startup must validate. Teach Build-Measure-Learn as the core operating rhythm.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| For Saudi accelerators: MISA fast-track registration for international companies, the SAMA regulatory sandbox pathway, and access to government procurement fast-track for validated solutions are the three most valuable unique benefits. Design around these.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| The mentor network is the most important and hardest-to-build asset of any accelerator. Design the mentor onboarding, engagement, and recognition programme with as much care as the startup curriculum.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| The Demo Day investor audience determines whether the accelerator produces fundable companies. Design the investor invitation strategy specifically: which VCs, CVCs, and family office investors attend, and what preparation do startups receive to convert Demo Day attention into term sheets.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
