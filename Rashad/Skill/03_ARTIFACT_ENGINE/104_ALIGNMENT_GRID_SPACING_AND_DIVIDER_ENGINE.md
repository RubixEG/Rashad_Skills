# Alignment, Grid, Spacing & Divider Engine

The page must feel deliberately composed, not merely non-overlapping.

Validate:
- alignment groups within tolerance;
- repeated columns/rows share baseline or edge alignment;
- spacing groups have controlled gap variance;
- dividers sit on the intended axis and do not float/overshoot;
- title/subtitle/artifact/footer zones maintain consistent page rhythm;
- margins are deliberate and visually coherent.

A divider, label or micro-element in a strange/unanchored position is a QA failure even if it does not technically overflow.
