| Step 1. KNOWLEDGE ARCHITECTURE: Map all knowledge that must be transferred. Three categories: (a) Explicit Knowledge - documented processes, SOPs, templates, playbooks, evaluation rubrics, and databases. (b) Tacit Knowledge - judgment calls, relationship intelligence, facilitation skills, negotiation approaches, and programme intuition that must be transferred through working alongside and coaching. (c) Relationship Capital - the network of startups, mentors, investors, and corporate partners built during the programme and must be transitioned to client ownership. For each category: what it contains, how it is transferred, and how readiness for handover is verified.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Step 2. SOP DESIGN: Design the operating procedures for each programme component: (a) Innovation Lab Operations SOP - daily operations, visitor management, event hosting, equipment maintenance. (b) Hackathon Delivery SOP - full programme guide from challenge design through post-event winner transition. (c) Accelerator Programme SOP - cohort management, curriculum delivery, mentor management, milestone gate execution, graduation and Demo Day. (d) Incubator Operations SOP - portfolio company management, venture building support, investment readiness programme, portfolio review cadence. (e) Scouting and Sourcing SOP - database management, outreach protocol, evaluation scoring, pipeline management. (f) Community Management SOP - content calendar management, event delivery, member management, platform administration. Each SOP must be at the level of operational detail that a new team member with the right professional background could use it to run the programme without the original designer present. |
| Step 3. TRAINING PROGRAMME DESIGN: For each role in the innovation function: the competency profile required, the current capability gap, the training intervention (formal training, on-the-job coaching, external programme), and the assessment method that verifies readiness. Design the training calendar aligned with the handover timeline - training must precede responsibility transfer, not follow it.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Step 4. PHASED HANDOVER DESIGN: Design responsibility transfer in four stages. Stage 1: Observer - the authority team member watches and asks questions. Stage 2: Participant - the authority team member executes specific tasks with external team present. Stage 3: Lead with Safety Net - the authority team member leads with the external team available for rapid consultation. Stage 4: Independent - the authority team member executes fully independently. Design the handover timeline and the gate criteria for each stage transition.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Step 5. KNOWLEDGE ASSET LIBRARY: Design the comprehensive knowledge asset library. Library components: all SOPs, programme templates (all documents, forms, evaluation rubrics, scoring sheets used in programme delivery), facilitation guides (for Design Thinking workshops, hackathon sessions, accelerator workshops, community events), database and contact management protocols, and programme technology user guides.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
