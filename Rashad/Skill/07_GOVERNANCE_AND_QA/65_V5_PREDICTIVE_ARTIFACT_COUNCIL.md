## Mandatory questions
1. Does the page have enough content to deserve a slide?
2. What relationship is being explained?
3. Is the chosen artifact stronger than a table, list or cards?
4. Is the artifact synthesized from evidence or copied from a template?
5. Does the page have a dominant artifact and supporting evidence?
6. What can break in render: text, labels, numbers, connectors, anchors, fonts, RTL, export?
7. Does the page beat the relevant MWAN/corpus family benchmark?
