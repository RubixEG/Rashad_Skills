| Step 1. CAMPAIGN ARCHITECTURE: Design the 18-month campaign as three phases. Phase 1 (months 1-4) - Awareness and Credibility: building the lab brand, establishing the authority's innovation leadership position, and generating the first wave of programme applications. Phase 2 (months 5-12) - Programme Activation: marketing each programme launch as a distinct campaign event, celebrating outputs, building community around live programme activity. Phase 3 (months 13-18) - Impact and Legacy: showcasing portfolio results, publishing the Annual Impact Report, attracting international attention, building the case for programme renewal. For each phase: primary campaign objective, hero content piece, media mix, and success metric. |
| Step 2. CAMPAIGN CALENDAR: Design the month-by-month campaign calendar. For each month: the primary campaign activity, content required, media channels activated, event or milestone being communicated, and team effort required. Identify the 4-5 hero moments in the 18-month calendar (lab launch, first hackathon, accelerator cohort announcement, Demo Day, Annual Impact Report) and design each as a distinct high-intensity campaign moment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Step 3. CONTENT PRODUCTION PLAN: Design the full content production plan. Video content (launch film, programme documentary episodes, startup story series, expert interview series): brief per asset, production timeline, distribution plan. Written content (Annual Insight Report on sector innovation, monthly newsletter, programme case studies, regulatory perspective pieces): author, length, review process, distribution.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Step 4. MEDIA AND PR STRATEGY: Design the media relations strategy. Target publications and journalists (sector media, business media, innovation media, international media), the story angles that will generate coverage (regulatory innovation is globally unusual and genuinely newsworthy), the press release calendar aligned with programme milestones, and the thought leadership placement strategy (op-eds by programme leadership in target publications).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Step 5. DIGITAL MARKETING ARCHITECTURE: Design the digital marketing system. Website structure for the innovation programme, SEO strategy for programme discovery by target applicants, paid advertising strategy (LinkedIn Ads for professional audiences, targeted geographic campaigns for international startup recruitment), and email marketing architecture (newsletter design, drip campaigns for applicant nurturing, alumni communication).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Step 6. AGENCY BRIEFING PACKAGE: Design the complete agency brief including: background and context, campaign objectives, target audiences, brand guidelines, campaign phases and calendar, deliverables required, budget parameters, KPIs and reporting expectations, and approval process.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
