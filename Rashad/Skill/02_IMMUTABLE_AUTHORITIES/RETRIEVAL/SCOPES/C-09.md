| **C-09**        | **Synergy Assessment and Quantification**                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
|-----------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Identifies, quantifies, and designs the realisation plan for synergies in M&A, consolidation, or restructuring transactions.                                                                                                                                 |
| **Delivers**    | Synergy identification report, synergy quantification model, synergy realisation plan, synergy governance framework, integration cost assessment.                                                                                                            |
| **How**         | Commercial synergy analysis, operational synergy analysis, financial synergy analysis, digital synergy assessment, integration cost estimation, synergy prioritisation, realisation planning.                                                                |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi consolidation synergies often include workforce Saudization improvement (where the combined entity can achieve higher Nitaqat ratings), shared government licensing and regulatory costs, and combined procurement leverage with government suppliers. |
