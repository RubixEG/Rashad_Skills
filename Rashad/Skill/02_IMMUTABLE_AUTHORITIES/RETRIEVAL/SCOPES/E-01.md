| **E-01**        | **HR Transformation**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Redesigns the HR function from an administrative service to a strategic business partner, with new capabilities, processes, and operating model.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Delivers**    | HR transformation strategy, HR operating model, HR capability requirements, HR technology roadmap, HR service delivery model, HR transformation roadmap.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **How**         | HR diagnostic, HR maturity assessment, HR operating model design, process redesign, capability gap assessment, technology requirements definition, transformation planning.                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi HR transformation must align with NCHRM government HR standards, integrate Saudization (Nitaqat) compliance management, and comply with Saudi Labour Law. HR functions in government entities operate within civil service regulations that constrain the extent of transformation possible without formal regulatory change. |
