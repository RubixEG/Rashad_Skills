| **G-02**        | **Operational Excellence Programme**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs and implements a systematic programme to improve operational performance across efficiency, quality, and cost dimensions.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Delivers**    | Operational excellence strategy, improvement opportunity assessment, programme design, KPI framework, quick wins implementation, capability building plan.                                                                                                                                                  |
| **How**         | Operational diagnostic, opportunity identification, programme design, initiative prioritisation, quick win implementation, performance management design, capability building.                                                                                                                              |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Operational excellence in Saudi government requires framing in terms of citizen service quality and national KPI improvement, not internal cost efficiency. For private sector: operational excellence must demonstrate value to Saudi customer expectations and competitive positioning in the GCC market. |
