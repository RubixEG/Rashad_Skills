## Findings
1. **Root cause addressed correctly.** The council agrees the visible quality gap originated mainly in pre-generation art direction and conditioning, not only in render/export stages.
2. **Golden Visual Master approach retained.** The release does not revert to generic native-card composition as the default visual-production path.
3. **Reference conditioning is now explicit.** This materially improves the likelihood of MWAN-like continuity and consulting-grade visual behavior.
4. **Palette drift is now governed.** The release directly addresses prior uncontrolled color expansion.
5. **Deck rhythm is now governed.** This reduces the risk of every page becoming equally loud, decorative, or repetitive.
6. **Cover default is clarified.** Left-side topic-related editorial hero image plus right-side content zone remains the standard.
7. **No protected regression detected in the sampled protected surface.**

## Residual truthfulness note
This release is still a knowledge/governance release. It strengthens how Rashad should think and brief generation, but does not itself claim that every runtime will execute those authorities perfectly without external compliance.

## Verdict
