| **A-07**        | **Strategic Planning Process Design**                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs and implements the annual or multi-year strategic planning process that enables the organisation to set direction and allocate resources systematically.                                                                                  |
| **Delivers**    | Strategic planning process design, planning calendar and governance, planning templates and frameworks, strategy review design, planning capability building programme.                                                                           |
| **How**         | Current process assessment, best practice research, process redesign, template and tool development, pilot facilitation, capability building.                                                                                                     |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi government strategic planning cycles are typically aligned to the Five-Year Development Plan and the NTP programme review cycle. Planning processes must integrate with MOF budget allocation timelines and Cabinet reporting requirements. |
