# Evaluator Question Engine

For every criterion and requirement, convert the source into an evaluator question.

Examples:
- “Understanding of the service” → Does the bidder understand the institutional problem, not merely repeat the scope?
- “Methodology” → Is the approach complete, sequenced, feasible, and directly connected to deliverables?
- “Team” → Does each proposed expert meet the requirement and own relevant outputs?
- “Experience” → Is the proof recent, comparable, and evidenced?
- “Knowledge transfer” → Will the client become able to operate independently?

Every page must answer one primary evaluator question and may answer secondary questions without losing focus.
