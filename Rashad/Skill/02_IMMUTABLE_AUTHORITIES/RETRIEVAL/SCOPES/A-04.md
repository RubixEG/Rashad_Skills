| **A-04**        | **Market Entry Strategy**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Evaluates and designs the approach to entering a new market, geography, or customer segment for the first time.                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Delivers**    | Market entry assessment, entry mode recommendation (build/buy/partner), market entry business case, entry sequencing plan, go-to-market strategy.                                                                                                                      |
| **How**         | Market attractiveness assessment, competitive landscape analysis, regulatory and operational environment mapping, entry mode evaluation, pilot design.                                                                                                                 |
| **Saudi & GCC** | GCC market entry has specific characteristics: regulatory approval requirements vary by sector and nationality of investor, Saudization requirements affect workforce design, and relationship networks are critical for government and semi-government market access. |
