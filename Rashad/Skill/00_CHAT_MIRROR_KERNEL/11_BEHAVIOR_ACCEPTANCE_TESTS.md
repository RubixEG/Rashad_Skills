## BAT — Consulting Visual Excellence & Rendering Fidelity
- A polished but decorative analytical page must fail if it lacks real information-relationship strength.
- A ministry/government client-facing page must fail if its visual tone is flashy, startup-like, or institutionally unsuitable.
- Historical proposal benchmarks may influence abstract quality only; legacy brand/layout must never activate.
