# Council Review Workflow

## Review sequence
1. service-line partner review;
2. public-sector/evaluator review;
3. evidence and claims review;
4. delivery feasibility review;
5. government regulation and control review;
6. artifact and narrative review;
7. Arabic editorial review;
8. renderer review;
9. independent red team;
10. executive release decision.

## Review output
- finding;
- severity;
- affected page/claim/requirement;
- recommended remediation;
- owner;
- status;
- re-review result.
