## Story → artifact contract

Storytelling does not replace Artifact Engine. Order:

```text
Content correctness
→ Storyline (evaluator journey)
→ Artifact Intent (relationship, nodes, edges, reading path, focal point)
→ Artifact Council
→ Visual planning
→ Geometry
→ Safety (layout only)
```

Storytelling Council may strengthen hierarchy, focal point, reading path, and executive comprehension. It may **not** authorize generic-card collapse or node/edge deletion.

## Section narrative rhythm

A strong section generally moves through:

1. Section promise
2. Context or diagnostic
3. Conclusion or design principle
4. Solution or methodology
5. Evidence and detail
6. Implementation and control
7. Transition to the next section

Duplication rule: a page must add a new conclusion, proof, decision, or implementation detail. Rewording is not progression.

## Visual rhythm rules

- Do not repeat the same layout family on consecutive pages
- Change dominant information relationship at least every three pages
- Balance low / medium / high density intentionally
- One focal visual per page
- Dense tables only when evaluator needs precise lookup
- Whitespace is hierarchy, not emptiness
- No empty / near-empty slides as filler

## Visual quality standard (consulting-grade)

Avoid:

- Repeated generic cards
- Empty slides
- Decorative diagrams with no information relationship
- Default Microsoft Office masters
- Black / near-black backgrounds (Aug 8 deck rule)
- Random gradients
- Inconsistent icons
- Weak hierarchy / uncontrolled density / arbitrary shrink-to-fit
- Broken RTL
- Inconsistent client identity

Use:

- Strong thesis headlines
- Deliberate focal point and reading path
- Structured light canvas
- Rubix visual grammar (see `01_ACTIVE_RUNTIME/13_RUBIX_DECK_AND_BRAND.md`, `BRAND/*`)
- Semantic artifacts (native/hybrid analytical core)
- Purposeful whitespace

Current color authority is `08_BRAND_CURRENT/RUBIX_ARTIFACT_PALETTE.md`: magenta is the brand anchor; verified purple/teal/blue/navy/orange/red are controlled semantic accents on a light-first canvas. Color must encode meaning, not decoration.

## Cover and section openers

- Cover may use AI-generated **strictly text-free** hero imagery only when the isolated-asset gate permits generation; titles, client name, section markers, and Rubix identity overlay as **native text/assets** (never image-model text).
- Never invent client logos in imagery.
- Section openers: hero / sector / conceptual illustration may support; analytical core remains artifact-led.
