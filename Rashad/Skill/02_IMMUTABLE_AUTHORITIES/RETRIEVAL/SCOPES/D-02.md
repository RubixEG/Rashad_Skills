| **D-02**        | **Digital Transformation Programme**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs and manages the end-to-end transformation of an organisation to a digital-first operating model.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Delivers**    | Digital transformation strategy, transformation roadmap, change management plan, technology architecture, data strategy, governance framework, programme management structure.                                                                                                                                                              |
| **How**         | Digital diagnostic, transformation design, technology architecture design, process redesign for digital, capability building plan, change management, programme governance.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi government digital transformation must comply with SDAIA standards, integrate with Absher and government shared services platforms, follow the National Data Management Office guidelines, and meet PDPL data protection requirements. Arabic language capability in digital systems is a regulatory and user experience requirement. |
