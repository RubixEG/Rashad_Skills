# V4 Predictive Multidisciplinary Council Audit

## Council composition
Consulting Partner; Proposal Director; Senior PM; Technical SME; Commercial/BOQ SME; Operations & Launch Lead; Saudi Government Evaluator; Content/Evidence Editor; Artifact Intelligence Architect; Information Designer; Visual Art Director; MWAN/Reference Curator; Brand Guardian; Arabic Editorial Lead; RTL/BiDi Engineer; HTML/CSS Architect; SVG/Topology Engineer; Typography/Font Engineer; Rendering Fidelity Engineer; Prepress/PDF Engineer; PPTX Parity Engineer; Stress/Load QA Lead; Regression Lead; Security/Asset Integrity Reviewer; Release Chair.

## Council findings
1. **Visual thinking must remain upstream of geometry.** Version 4 keeps whole-page concept search/GVM as visual truth and prevents HTML from becoming the creativity bottleneck.
2. **Content/artifact balance is preserved.** Strong Page Content Pack remains mandatory before visual design; artifact cannot replace evidence or interpretation.
3. **Native vs hybrid mode conflict is resolved.** Fidelity determines production mode; HTML/SVG is not a universal default, and hybrid GVM+native overlays is mandatory when native reconstruction would materially weaken the approved design.
4. **QA is materially stronger.** Every significant production object is instrumented and executable checks cover overflow, collisions, z-index, regions, alignment, spacing, dividers, font/glyph readiness, RTL/BiDi, nodes, edges, endpoints, arrowheads, labels, card dominance, debug leakage, balance, stress and repeat-render determinism.
5. **Safety cannot destroy artifacts.** Semantic signatures are frozen before repair and compared after repair.
6. **Predictive testing is required before full deck.** A representative pilot must pass before batch generation.
7. **MWAN remains quality lower-bound, not template.** Its pages demonstrate dense content plus higher-order relationship artifacts rather than sparse decoration.

## Predicted failure classes now covered
Long Arabic expansion; mixed LTR islands; font absence/fallback; source/footer overflow; title overflow; off-canvas nodes; node collision; hidden labels; wrong z-index; floating divider; inconsistent alignment/spacing; missing/detached/reversed connectors; wrong arrowhead; card-only downgrade; generic cover; logo transform drift; palette drift; internal metadata leakage; repeated page grammar; nondeterministic font/layout timing; PDF/PPTX parity drift; destructive auto-repair.

## Verdict
**GO for Version 4 knowledge/governance architecture + executable HTML QA companion integration.**

Final client release still requires actual per-engagement execution evidence; the council does not convert unexecuted gates into PASS.
