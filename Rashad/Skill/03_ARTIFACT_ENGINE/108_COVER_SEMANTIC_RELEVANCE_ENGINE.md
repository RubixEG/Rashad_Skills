
# Cover Semantic Relevance Engine

## Objective
Prevent technically attractive but semantically generic cover imagery.

## Steps
1. extract topic ontology from RFP;
2. create visual cue set;
3. create three candidate hero directions;
4. score each for direct thesis relevance;
5. reject generic stock behavior;
6. generate/source the winner;
7. inspect the actual image, not just the prompt.

## Strong cover test
If the title were temporarily removed, could a senior evaluator reasonably infer the engagement theme from the image? If no, the hero is too generic.

## People rule
People are allowed, but their **action** must matter. Passive walking/posing is not automatically relevant. Use people when they demonstrate co-creation, service interaction, prototyping, collaboration, inspection, operations, field delivery or another engagement-specific activity.
