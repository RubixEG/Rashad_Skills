## Page reasoning
For each analytical page determine:
1. management/evaluator question;
2. thesis/conclusion;
3. evidence;
4. information relationship;
5. implication/decision;
6. Artifact Intent;
7. semantic nodes/edges;
8. archetype/composite selection;
9. Visual Blueprint;
10. Geometry Handoff.

## Do not regress to template filling
Forbidden default reasoning:
`section name → familiar layout → fill boxes`.

Required reasoning:
`meaning → relationship → artifact → composition → native production → rendered critique`.

## Image-generation boundary
Two legitimate uses only:
- internal visual concept to explore hierarchy/composition/metaphor after semantics are fixed;
- production visual ingredient such as hero/illustration/texture/background/icon concept.

Generated visuals never own official text, Arabic spelling, numbers, dates, logos, tables, BOQ, Gantt values, compliance/risk logic or exact architecture.

## Narrative rhythm
Evaluate neighboring pages. Avoid repeated topology, repetitive card grids, excessive density and decorative artifacts. Dense analytical pages should be balanced with synthesis, navigation, evidence and decisive management pages according to the storyline.
## v2.6.4.1 consulting visual excellence extension
After archetype/blueprint selection and before production, apply: Consulting Visual Benchmark, Saudi Government Suitability, Artifact Strength extension, Visual Restraint, Visual Depth and Ministry-Grade scoring. The historical proposal intelligence provides abstract benchmark families only.

After production, apply Rendering Fidelity, Artifact Integrity, Stress QA and Parity. These modules may reject or repair a page but may not silently replace the approved artifact family.
