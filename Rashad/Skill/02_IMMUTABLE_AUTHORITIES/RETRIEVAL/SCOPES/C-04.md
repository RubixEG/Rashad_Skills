| **C-04**        | **Revenue Optimisation**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Identifies and designs interventions to grow revenue through improved pricing, customer mix, channel effectiveness, or product portfolio.                                                                                                                                                |
| **Delivers**    | Revenue opportunity assessment, pricing architecture, customer portfolio analysis, channel optimisation recommendations, revenue improvement roadmap.                                                                                                                                    |
| **How**         | Revenue decomposition, pricing analysis, customer LTV analysis, channel performance assessment, opportunity identification, intervention design, business case development.                                                                                                              |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi market revenue optimisation must account for price sensitivity specific to GCC consumer and government procurement dynamics, Saudization requirements in sales force design, and regulatory pricing constraints in controlled sectors (healthcare, utilities, financial services). |
