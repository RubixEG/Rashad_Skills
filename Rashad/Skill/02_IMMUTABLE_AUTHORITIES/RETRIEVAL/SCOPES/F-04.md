| **F-04**        | **Internal Control Design**                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs or reviews the internal control framework to ensure accurate financial reporting, operational efficiency, and regulatory compliance.                                                                                                              |
| **Delivers**    | Internal control framework, control matrix, risk and control mapping, control testing protocol, remediation roadmap, control monitoring framework.                                                                                                        |
| **How**         | Process mapping, risk identification, control identification and assessment, control design, testing protocol development, monitoring framework design.                                                                                                   |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi internal controls must align with SOCPA auditing standards, GAZT requirements, and sector-specific regulatory requirements (SAMA for financial sector, SFDA for healthcare). Government internal controls are subject to Saudi Audit Bureau review. |
