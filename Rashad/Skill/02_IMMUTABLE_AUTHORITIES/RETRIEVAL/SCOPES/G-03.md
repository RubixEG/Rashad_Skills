| **G-03**        | **Supply Chain Optimisation**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Analyses and optimises the supply chain from upstream suppliers to downstream customers to improve cost, speed, quality, and resilience.                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Delivers**    | Supply chain assessment, optimisation opportunities, supplier segmentation strategy, procurement strategy, logistics network design, resilience assessment.                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **How**         | Supply chain mapping, performance benchmarking, supplier analysis, network modelling, inventory optimisation, demand planning assessment, resilience risk assessment.                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi supply chain strategy must account for the Vision 2030 logistics hub ambition, the geographic concentration of population in the central and western regions, import dependency risks, and the Saudization requirements for supply chain workforce. MHRSD and MOC both have regulatory influence on supply chain operations. |
