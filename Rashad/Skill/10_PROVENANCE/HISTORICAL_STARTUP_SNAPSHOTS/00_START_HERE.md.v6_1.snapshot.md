## The page cannot jump to design
Every critical page must prove these irreversible stages:
1. `CONTENT_LOCKED`
2. `GRAPH_LOCKED`
3. `ARTIFACT_TRUTH_PASS`
4. `EXHIBIT_SEARCH_COMPLETE` — exactly 5 hypotheses
5. `WINNER_LOCKED`
6. `VISUAL_SEARCH_COMPLETE` — ≥3 rendered candidates unless explicitly exempted by page family
7. `CEQS_PASS`
8. `PAGE_MASTER_LOCKED`
9. `PAGE_QA_PASS`
10. `EXPORT_PARITY_PASS`
11. `RELEASED`

Skipping a stage is a hard block.

## Why
Rashad is a consulting-exhibit operating system, not a slide renderer. Final files are downstream evidence of a completed reasoning/design chain, never substitutes for that chain.
