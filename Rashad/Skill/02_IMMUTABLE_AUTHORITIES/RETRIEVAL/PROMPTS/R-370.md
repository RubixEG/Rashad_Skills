| **\<thinking_instructions\>**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Step 1. METHODOLOGY ARCHITECTURE: Design the full facilitation methodology across the five Design Thinking phases. For each phase - Empathise, Define, Ideate, Prototype, and Test - specify: the specific activities to be used (from the Design Thinking toolkit: interviews, empathy maps, journey maps, HMW questions, brainstorming, concept posters, paper prototypes, usability tests), the facilitation technique for each activity, the expected output, the time allocation, and the specific adaptation required for the Saudi professional context.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Step 2. SESSION TYPE DESIGN: Design specific session formats for the Innovation Lab's recurring needs: (a) Challenge Framing Session (3-4 hours) - converts a sector problem statement into a specific, actionable innovation challenge brief. (b) Solution Sprint (1-2 days) - intensive problem-solving session producing a tested concept. (c) Prototype and Test Session (half-day) - rapid prototyping and user testing with target stakeholders. (d) Strategic Co-Design Workshop (full day) - collaborative strategy development with mixed internal and external participants. For each: full agenda, facilitation guide, materials list, room setup requirements, and facilitator briefing.                                         |
| Step 3. FACILITATION GUIDE: Design the facilitator's reference for each session type. For each activity within each session: the facilitation opening and close (what to say), the facilitation moves (how to handle silence, manage dominant voices, surface quieter participants' ideas), the time management protocol (how to keep energy without rushing), and the output documentation method (how to capture what is produced in a way that is usable after the session).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Step 4. SAUDI CULTURAL ADAPTATION: Design specific adaptations for Saudi professional settings. Address: hierarchy management (how to create psychological safety when participants have significantly different seniority levels), gender-inclusive facilitation (designing activities and seating for mixed-gender professional settings), Arabic-English bilingual session management (when to use which language), and authority-in-the-room management (how to facilitate effectively when a senior official is present).                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Step 5. MATERIALS AND TOOLS: Specify physical and digital materials required. Physical: post-it notes, marker types, template printouts, whiteboard configurations, timing tools. Digital: collaboration platforms (Miro, Mural, or Arabic-compatible alternatives), output documentation templates, and session recording protocol. For each session type: the complete materials kit list.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Step 6. TRAINER-OF-TRAINERS DESIGN: Design the capability building programme for transferring the methodology to the client's internal team. Include: the facilitator competency framework (skills a facilitator must have), the training programme (how to develop internal facilitators), the certification criteria (what evidence proves a team member can facilitate independently), and the ongoing quality assurance mechanism.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

| The most common Design Thinking facilitation failure in Saudi professional settings is the silence problem - participants uncomfortable generating ideas publicly in the presence of senior colleagues. Design facilitation techniques specifically to overcome this: anonymous idea generation, small group breakouts before plenary.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Arabic language facilitation requires additional preparation: key Design Thinking concepts (empathy, prototype, pivot) do not have direct Arabic equivalents and must be introduced with definition and example before being used as working concepts.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
