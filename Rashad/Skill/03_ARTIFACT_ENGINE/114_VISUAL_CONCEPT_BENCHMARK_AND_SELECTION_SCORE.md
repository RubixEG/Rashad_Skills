- content accommodation 15
- artifact/relationship strength 15
- full-page composition quality 15
- reference/house grammar fit 10
- government evaluator scanability 10
- palette/brand restraint 5

- implementation robustness 5

Critical concept threshold: **≥85**.

No concept may win because it is easiest to code. If all candidates fail, reopen the visual framing.
