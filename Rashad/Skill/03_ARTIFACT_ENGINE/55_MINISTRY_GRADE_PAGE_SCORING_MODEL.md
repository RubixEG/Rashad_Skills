# Ministry-Grade Consulting Page Scoring Model

STATUS: HARD VISUAL EXCELLENCE GATE

## Purpose
Provide a single 100-point quality model for consulting-grade, government-ready page quality. This score is independent from, and does not weaken, existing safety/release gates.

## Score — 100
- Visual clarity: 10
- Argument clarity: 10
- Artifact strength / relationship embodiment: 20
- Analytical legitimacy / evidence discipline: 15
- Executive polish: 10
- Saudi government suitability: 10
- Visual restraint: 10
- Density control / whitespace discipline: 10
- Brand / RTL / production-readiness preview: 5

## Thresholds
- Critical executive / methodology / evaluation / governance / risk / decision page: **92+**.
- Other analytical page: **86+**.
- Artifact-heavy page: artifact-strength component must be at least **17/20**.
- No core dimension may score below **8/10 equivalent** on a critical page without explicit Council rationale.

Existing Rashad release thresholds remain authoritative where stricter.

## Automatic visual-excellence fails
Regardless of total score:
- generic-card downgrade of a relationship-heavy artifact;
- decorative visual with no analytical relationship;
- unsupported quantitative-looking encoding;
- major evidence separation from the claim it supports;
- weak/ambiguous focal point on a decision page;
- government-unsuitable visual tone;
- historical-brand leakage;
- visually attractive page that omits required nodes/edges;
- page materially weaker than its applicable approved benchmark without approved rationale.

## Decision
`VISUAL_EXCELLENCE = RELEASE | REWORK | REGENERATE | REDESIGN`
