# Consulting Visual Fidelity Score — CVFS

Score each critical page out of 100:
- Argument / thesis clarity: 15
- Artifact strength & analytical relationship: 20
- Evidence richness & decision usefulness: 15
- Spatial hierarchy & composition: 15
- Typography / readability: 10
- Visual restraint & palette discipline: 10
- Benchmark / deck continuity: 5
- Brand / co-brand / RTL production quality: 10

## Threshold
- Critical page release: **≥90/100**
- Other analytical page release: **≥86/100**
- Artifact-heavy page: artifact strength **≥17/20**

Hard blockers override the numeric score.

This score measures consulting-quality parity, not pixel identity to MWAN or any historical page.
