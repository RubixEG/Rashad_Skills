# Artifact Quality Floor and Golden Benchmark Gate

## Release principle
Passing layout safety does not equal passing artifact quality.

The release process now has two independent gates:

1. **Safety Gate**
   - no collision;
   - no overflow;
   - correct RTL sequence;
   - correct numerals;
   - correct logo and fonts;
   - HTML/PDF/PPTX parity.

2. **Strength Gate**
   - correct relationship-to-artifact mapping;
   - equal or stronger than the approved benchmark;
   - sufficient narrative variation;
   - evaluator-oriented synthesis;
   - no generic-card fallback;
   - no unsupported quantitative-looking graphics.

Both must pass.

## Golden MWAN benchmark roles
The benchmark registry should prioritize strong approved examples for:
- evaluator decision model;
- problem / challenge system map;
- connected lifecycle and stage-gate logic;
- governance and control model;
- evidence-based decision gate;
- strategic synthesis with one clear focal point.

These benchmark roles are visual and analytical references only. Current Rubix identity remains authoritative.

## Critical review question
Before release, the council must answer:

> Did this page become merely safer, or did it remain equally strong and useful to the evaluator?

If the answer is "safer but weaker," the page fails.
