| **\<thinking_instructions\>**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Step 1. INVESTMENT BASELINE: Document the full programme investment. Direct costs (programme management, lab construction and operation, programme delivery, marketing, prize money, stipends, expert fees). Indirect costs (regulatory authority staff time, value of regulatory access provided to participants, brand and credibility investment). Total cost of ownership over the programme duration. The baseline must include all costs for the ROI calculation to be credible.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Step 2. RETURN CATEGORISATION: Design the return framework across four categories. (a) Direct Financial Return: investment raised by portfolio companies (contribution-adjusted), revenue generated by alumni companies, exits or licensing deals, operational savings from adopted innovations in incumbents. (b) Ecosystem Financial Return: increase in total InsurTech investment in the sector (attribution-adjusted), new insurance products available to consumers, reduction in sector operational costs. (c) Economic Contribution: jobs created by portfolio companies (direct and indirect), GDP contribution of portfolio company revenue, tax contribution, Saudization improvement in sector technology workforce. (d) National Programme Contribution: measurable contribution to Vision 2030 Financial Sector Development Programme KPIs. |
| Step 3. CALCULATION METHODOLOGY: For each return category, design the explicit calculation: the data source for each input, the attribution percentage applied and its justification, the time horizon for return realisation, and the discount rate applied to future returns. Present: Total Investment, Total Discounted Return, Net Present Value, and Internal Rate of Return.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Step 4. SCENARIO MODELLING: Build three ROI scenarios. Conservative (30th percentile of comparable programme outcomes globally), Base (50th percentile), and Optimistic (70th percentile). For each: the key assumptions that drive the difference, the probability weighting, and the scenario-weighted expected return.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Step 5. BENCHMARK COMPARISON: Compare programme ROI against: the best-performing government innovation programmes globally (FinTech Saudi, MAS FinTech Festival, ADGM Innovation Hub), the average return profile of GCC government innovation investment, and the alternative use of the same investment in traditional government programmes.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Step 6. REPORTING FORMAT: Design the financial impact section of the Annual Impact Report. Narrative (how programme investment became economic value, written for a non-financial audience), the financial dashboard (key ROI metrics in a visually clear format), the portfolio performance breakdown (how each programme contributed to total return), and the forward projection (the compounding effect at years 3 and 5 if continued).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
