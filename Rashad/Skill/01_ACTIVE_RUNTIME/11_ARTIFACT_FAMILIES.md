## Semantic preservation rules

Every selected family must declare:

- **Semantic nodes** — entities that must remain visible/readable
- **Semantic edges** — relationships that must remain visible/readable
- **Reading path** — order the evaluator should traverse
- **Focal point** — primary visual emphasis

Geometry repair may change x/y, spacing, routing, pagination, safe font-size, margins.  
Geometry repair may **not** remove nodes/edges/dependencies, alter family, or replace with generic boxes.

Overflow default: `continuation_page_same_family`.

## Selection sequence

1. Evaluator question
2. One-sentence page thesis
3. Supporting claims and evidence
4. Dominant information relationship
5. Service-line owner (when applicable)
6. Benchmark pages (archetype / relationship / density)
7. Select or design artifact (bespoke allowed when no fit / dual relationship / unique evaluation logic)
8. Validate density, RTL, editability, section rhythm
9. Artifact Council lock
10. Then Visual Spec → Geometry → Render — execute only through an available deterministic composer after firewall PASS; otherwise emit the specification

## Anti-patterns

- Generic-card fallback
- Relationship-less boxes
- Choosing a family because it “looks premium”
- Using MWAN layouts as permanent family authority
- Treating `REFERENCE_ONLY` compositions as production families
