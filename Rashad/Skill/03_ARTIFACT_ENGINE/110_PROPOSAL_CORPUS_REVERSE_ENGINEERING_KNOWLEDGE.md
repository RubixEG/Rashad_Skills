
# Historical Proposal Corpus — Reverse-Engineering Knowledge v2

STATUS: REFERENCE-ONLY CONSULTING KNOWLEDGE

## Corpus signal
The audited Rubix proposal corpus demonstrates that high-quality proposal pages are usually **content-rich and argument-led**, not minimal visual posters. Strong pages combine text, evidence, and an appropriate visual relationship.

## Cover grammar observed
Common house grammar:
- significant visual on left / content on right;
- prominent Rubix and client identity;
- clean light background in many decks;
- geometric image masks / faceted crop language.

However, repeated legacy stock images are **not** treated as proof of quality. Topic-specific image choice is governed separately by the current Cover Semantic Relevance Engine.

## Analytical page grammar observed
Across the corpus, recurring useful patterns include:
- process/phase architectures;
- evaluation tables and criterion maps;
- maps/geography and market context;
- target/goal relationships;
- journey/timeline structures;
- risk tables/matrices;
- methodology narratives;
- case-study evidence and image proof;
- team/CV/capability evidence;
- compliance and operational matrices.

## Key lesson
Consulting-grade does not mean every page is a custom illustration. It means every page uses the **right communication form** for the argument and preserves sufficient explanatory content.
