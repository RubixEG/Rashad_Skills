
# Non-Destructive Safety & Stress Gate

Run stress scenarios for long Arabic content, dense tables, complex graphs, mixed scripts, extreme logos, export parity and 30+ pages.

Safety passes only when:
- no overflow/clipping/collision;
- all semantic signatures remain unchanged;
- no artifact family downgrade;
- no evidence deletion;
- no label/edge loss;
- no global unreadable shrink.
