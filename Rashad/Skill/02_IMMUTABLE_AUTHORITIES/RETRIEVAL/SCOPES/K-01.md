| **K-01**        | **Innovation Strategy (Regulated Sector)**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Design of the complete innovation ecosystem for a regulatory body or government authority, covering innovation strategy, architecture, governance, and 18-month implementation roadmap.                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Delivers**    | Innovation strategy document, innovation architecture design (three-layer: open innovation, structured development, market integration), programme portfolio framework, governance model, implementation roadmap                                                                                                                                                    |
| **How**         | Sector innovation maturity diagnostic, strategic intent design, three-layer architecture design, governance and operating model design, implementation sequencing                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Relevant for SAMA, Insurance Authority, SFDA, MCIT, MISA, and other regulatory bodies with an innovation mandate under Vision 2030. SAMA FinTech Lab and SDAIA AI governance framework are the primary Saudi precedents for regulatory innovation enablement. Regulatory sandbox design is a mandatory component of any Saudi regulated sector innovation strategy. |
