## Predictive challenge
Before release, each specialist asks not only “does it pass now?” but “how can this page fail under content growth, font drift, export, scaling, RTL, asset variation or topology complexity?” Required mutations are selected from the Render Threat Model.

## Council rule
A page may not receive RELEASED if any required threat surface is untested, not instrumented, or supported only by self-declared attributes without geometric/pixel evidence.
