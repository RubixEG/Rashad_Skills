# RFP Summary — 24 Role Understanding Depth Contracts

STATUS: HARD PRODUCT DEPTH AUTHORITY

## Core rule
The canonical RFP Summary contains 24 logical roles. These roles are not fixed physical slides. A complex role MUST expand to multiple pages when one page cannot satisfy the mandatory understanding, evidence, artifact, and management-implication requirements without compression or illegibility.

Page count follows evidence and analytical depth, never an arbitrary target.

## Role expansion policy
- Standard role: 1 page only when all mandatory content and artifact meaning fit at the approved strength floor.
- Complex role: 2–5 pages when required by item count, evidence density, distinct analytical relationships, or management decisions.
- BOQ, Team, Technical Requirements, Evaluation, Qualification, Commercial Exposure, Risks, and Clarifications are explicit high-expansion candidates.
- Splitting a role is preferred over shrinking text, deleting evidence, collapsing relationships, or converting the role to generic cards.

## Mandatory role contract fields
Every role must define and satisfy: Purpose → Mandatory Content → Required Analysis → Evidence → Artifact Expectation → Management Implication → Expansion Decision → Release Failure Test.

## 01. Cover (`COVER`)

**Purpose:** Identify the competition and product without analysis.

**Mandatory content:**
- Competition name
- Client entity
- Competition/reference number when evidenced
- Product label
- Current Rubix + client co-brand
- Engagement-specific hero

**Required analysis:**
- Reconcile competition identity across all authoritative documents; surface identity conflicts without adding analysis to the cover.
- Verify current Rubix/client asset identity and exact competition/reference metadata.

**Evidence:**
- Authoritative competition title/client entity/reference number when present.
- Verified current Rubix assets and engagement-supplied client assets.

**Artifact expectation:** Premium cover composition; no analytical artifact.

**Management implication:** The page/role must state why the evidence matters for bid, solution, delivery, pricing, compliance, or management action when applicable.

**Release failure:** Never add analytical conclusions to the cover.

**Expansion test:** Expand this role whenever one page cannot preserve the mandatory evidence, analytical relationships, and readable artifact strength without compression.
