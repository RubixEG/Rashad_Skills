# v2.6.4.1 Rendering QA & Consulting Visual Excellence Integration Report

## Objective
Add two independent controls to v2.6.4 without replacing its knowledge or workflow:
1. consulting-grade / Saudi-government-ready visual quality before rendering;
2. rendering fidelity / stress QA / artifact-integrity / parity after rendering.

## User-provided notes treatment
The user notes were compiled into formal English operating authorities. Raw conversational wording was not copied into active rules.

## Historical proposal corpus
The existing v2.6.4 reverse-engineering outputs remain the basis for abstract benchmark intelligence. Raw historical proposals remain reference-only and are not reactivated as brand/layout authority.

## Architectural rule
`Intelligence → Artifact → Visual Excellence → Geometry → Render → Stress QA / Integrity / Parity → Release`

Safety and visual quality are independent. A safe but weak page fails. A strong source artifact damaged by export also fails.
## Added council and benchmark registry
The release adds a two-stage Visual Excellence & Rendering Integrity Council and a 24-pattern abstract consulting benchmark registry compiled from existing audited proposal/artifact intelligence.
