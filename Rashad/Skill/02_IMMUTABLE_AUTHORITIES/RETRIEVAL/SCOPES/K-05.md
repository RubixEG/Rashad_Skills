| **K-05**        | **Startup Accelerator Programme**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Design and operation of a structured accelerator programme for early-stage startups in a specific sector, providing curriculum, mentorship, regulatory access, and Demo Day commercialisation support.                                                                          |
| **Delivers**    | Cohort design and selection framework, curriculum architecture (week-by-week), mentorship model, milestone gate design, regulatory access pathway design, Demo Day and graduation design                                                                                        |
| **How**         | Cohort profile and selection design, curriculum development, mentor network build, milestone gate management, investor engagement, Demo Day production                                                                                                                          |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Monshaat, Flat6Labs Saudi, and STC Ventures provide local accelerator precedents. MISA fast-track registration, SAMA regulatory sandbox, and SVC co-investment facility are the key Saudi-specific accelerator enablers that a regulator-backed programme can uniquely provide. |
