
# Proposal Corpus Reference Knowledge Contract

STATUS: ACTIVE REFERENCE-LEARNING AUTHORITY — v2.6.4.10

The internal historical proposal corpus is a **knowledge and benchmark source**, not a production template library.

## Corpus audited
- 34 historical proposal/capability files in `Proposals.zip`;
- 31 PDF + 3 PPTX;
- ~1,976 countable pages/slides in the available package;
- 17 technical-PDF covers/mid-pages rendered successfully for visual sampling in this audit;
- 16 unique text-readable technical PDFs provided 1,508 extractable technical pages for density analysis.

Text-extractable technical pages showed a median of ~2,827 characters/page and a mean of ~3,089 characters/page. This is **not a target word count**; it is evidence that the stronger historical corpus is materially denser than minimalist summary-page outputs.

## Learn separately
### Learn as positive grammar
- strong title hierarchy;
- meaningful explanatory content;
- visible evidence / source / proof;
- one dominant analytical structure when a relationship exists;
- maps, matrices, timelines, process architectures, methodology structures, team views, case-study proof and compliance artifacts;
- consistent header/footer rhythm;
- restrained Rubix identity with client integration.

### Do not learn blindly
- repeated stock imagery merely because it appears often;
- old client identities;
- outdated logos/fonts/themes;
- exact layouts;
- text-heavy walls that reduce scanability;
- weak/generic cover photos;
- historical mistakes.

**Frequency is not quality.** A repeated legacy pattern is admitted only when it also passes current consulting-quality gates.

## Win-history truthfulness
The owner has stated that the corpus includes partner proposals and that some historical proposals were successful/won. Unless outcome metadata is supplied per file, do not label any specific file “winning” or weight it as such.
