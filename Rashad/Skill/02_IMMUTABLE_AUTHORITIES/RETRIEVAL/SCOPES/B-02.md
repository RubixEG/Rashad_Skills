| **B-02**        | **Target Operating Model Design**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs the future state operating model across five dimensions: Structure, Process, People, Technology, and Governance. The core design scope of any transformation engagement.                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Delivers**    | Target Operating Model document, design principles, organisational structure design, governance architecture, process target state, technology requirements, capability requirements, implementation roadmap.                                                                                                                      |
| **How**         | Current state synthesis, design principle development, TOM scenario development and evaluation, operating model detailed design, alignment workshops, Civil Service Bureau pathway planning (government), implementation planning.                                                                                                 |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi government TOM design must align with Civil Service Bureau authority matrix requirements, ministerial mandate boundaries, and NCHRM organisational design standards. Any structural change below a defined threshold is within ministerial authority; changes above it require Cabinet or Council of Civil Service approval. |
