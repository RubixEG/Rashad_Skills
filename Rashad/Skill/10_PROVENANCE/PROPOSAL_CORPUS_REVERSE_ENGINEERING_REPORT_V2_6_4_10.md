
# Proposal Corpus Reverse-Engineering Report — v2.6.4.10

## Corpus
Audited `Proposals.zip` already available in the conversation workspace.
- 34 files: 31 PDF + 3 PPTX.
- Approximately 1,976 countable pages/slides in the package.
- 17 technical-PDF cover/mid-page samples rendered for visual review.
- 16 unique text-readable technical PDFs yielded 1,508 extractable technical pages.
- Extractable technical-page text density: median ~2,827 characters/page; mean ~3,089. This is a corpus signal, not a target.

## Strong recurring grammar
- content-rich analytical pages;
- clear page titles and section identity;
- meaningful body explanation rather than artifact-only posters;
- tables/matrices/processes/timelines/methodology visuals used when relevant;
- evidence/case-study imagery where proof matters;
- restrained Rubix/client identity;
- white/light analytical pages dominate many technical decks.

## Cover learning
A strong structural pattern is image-led left side + title/client identity on right in many decks. However, the corpus also repeats generic legacy stock imagery; frequency is therefore not accepted as quality. Current cover image selection must pass semantic topic relevance.

## Anti-patterns learned
- generic repeated stock hero with weak engagement specificity;
- dense text walls with weak scanability;
- old client/brand identity contamination;
- equal panels without a higher-order analytical relationship;
- visual decoration that does not clarify the argument.

## Integration decision
Use the corpus as `REFERENCE_ONLY_VISUAL_AND_CONTENT_KNOWLEDGE`, never as a template or source of current client facts.
