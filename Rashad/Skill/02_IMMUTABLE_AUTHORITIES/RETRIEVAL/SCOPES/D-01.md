| **D-01**        | **Digital Strategy**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Defines the organisation digital ambition, the specific digital initiatives that will deliver value, and the capabilities required to execute them.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Delivers**    | Digital strategy, digital initiative portfolio, digital capability roadmap, digital investment case, digital governance framework.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **How**         | Digital maturity assessment, digital opportunity identification, initiative prioritisation, business case development, capability gap assessment, roadmap design.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi digital strategy must align with Saudi Vision 2030 digital economy objectives and the MCIT national digital strategy. Government entities must align with the Government Digital Transformation Strategy and SDAIA requirements. Cloud-first policy, data localisation requirements, and PDPL compliance are mandatory considerations. |
