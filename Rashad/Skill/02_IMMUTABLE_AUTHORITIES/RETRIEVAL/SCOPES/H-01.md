| **H-01**        | **Market Feasibility Study**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Assesses the feasibility of entering a new market, launching a new product, or making a major investment from a market demand perspective.                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Delivers**    | Market feasibility report, TAM/SAM/SOM analysis, demand assessment, competitive landscape, financial viability assessment, go/no-go recommendation.                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **How**         | Market sizing, demand analysis, competitor assessment, regulatory environment mapping, financial modelling, stakeholder interviews, investment case development.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi market feasibility studies must account for Vision 2030-driven demand creation (giga-projects, tourism, entertainment, sports) that is fundamentally changing the demand landscape in ways that historical data cannot predict. Government procurement patterns and sovereign wealth fund investment priorities are key demand drivers. |
