| **F-03**        | **Regulatory Compliance Framework**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs the compliance management framework that ensures the organisation meets all applicable regulatory requirements across all jurisdictions of operation.                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Delivers**    | Compliance framework, regulatory obligation inventory, compliance monitoring system, compliance governance, compliance training programme, regulatory change management process.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **How**         | Regulatory landscape mapping, obligation inventory development, gap assessment, compliance control design, monitoring system design, governance framework design.                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi regulatory compliance is complex: multiple regulators with overlapping jurisdiction (SAMA, CMA, MOC, MHRSD, MOH, MCIT, MIM, NCA) and frequent regulatory change driven by Vision 2030 reform programmes. Regulatory compliance frameworks must include a regulatory monitoring process that tracks pending changes and assesses their operational implications. |
