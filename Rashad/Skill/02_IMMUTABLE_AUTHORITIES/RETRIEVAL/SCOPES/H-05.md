| **H-05**        | **Competitive Intelligence Programme**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs and implements an ongoing competitive intelligence capability that provides systematic insight into competitor strategies, capabilities, and moves.                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Delivers**    | Competitive intelligence framework, competitor profiles, competitive monitoring system, intelligence reporting cadence, early warning protocol.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **How**         | Intelligence needs assessment, competitor profiling, source identification, monitoring system design, analytical framework development, reporting cadence design.                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi competitive intelligence must use only legal and ethical sources. Key intelligence sources for Saudi market: Tadawul (Saudi Stock Exchange) filings for listed companies, MISA investment data, Government tenders database, GAZT VAT registration data, and industry association publications. Social media monitoring in Arabic is a significant intelligence source. |
