# Strategy, Policy, and Strategic Planning

## Senior-partner questions
- What decision must leadership make?
- What is the fact base and strategic diagnosis?
- What choices, trade-offs, and priorities create value?
- How will strategy translate into execution and measurement?

## Benchmark practice lenses
- McKinsey strategy and corporate-finance discipline
- BCG strategy, transformation, and value lens
- Bain results and implementation lens
- Strategy& practical strategy-to-execution lens
- EY-Parthenon investor and value-creation lens

## Preferred artifact families
- Executive decision pyramid
- Strategic choice cascade
- Issue tree and hypothesis map
- Portfolio prioritization matrix
- Strategy-to-execution bridge
- Policy impact chain

## Government evaluator test
- Is the section easy to score against the RFP?
- Are commitments feasible, measurable, and traceable?
- Are client dependencies and acceptance criteria explicit?
- Is the Arabic terminology native and precise?

## Evidence expectations
- direct relevance to the scope;
- recent and verifiable proof;
- named responsibility and outcome;
- no client leakage or unsupported credential.

## Anti-patterns
- generic vision statements
- frameworks without choices
- recommendations without evidence or owners

## Release condition
The line-of-service partner must approve the reasoning, the government evaluator must approve navigability and compliance, and the artifact director must approve the visual relationship before release.
