| Step 1. POPULATE ALL 6 CATEGORIES FIRST before scoring or prioritising anything: People (specific capability gaps, behavioural patterns, leadership qualities - name the role not the person), Process (specific undocumented workflows, approval bottlenecks, handoff failures - name the process), Technology (specific system limitations, data quality issues, integration gaps - name the system), Data (specific availability, accuracy, accessibility, or governance failures - name the data type), Strategy (specific unclear mandates, conflicting priorities, or absent direction - name the strategic ambiguity), External Factors (specific regulatory requirements, stakeholder mandates, market pressures - name the body or requirement). |
| Step 2. RATE EACH CAUSE on two dimensions - force differentiation, avoid clustering in the middle: Strategic Impact (1-5) measuring how much this cause contributes to the overall problem, and Actionability (1-5) measuring how tractable addressing this cause is. Name the 2-3 highest-priority individual causes using the Step 2 scores.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Step 4. BLIND SPOT CHECK: Which category received the fewest and least specific causes? Revisit it deliberately. Answer explicitly: is it under-represented because it is genuinely less relevant, or because it is harder to see from inside the organisation?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Step 5. CLOSE: Most important finding. Most important action. Biggest risk if no action. Strategic so-what and operational now-what for the top-priority cause - keep them strictly separated.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
