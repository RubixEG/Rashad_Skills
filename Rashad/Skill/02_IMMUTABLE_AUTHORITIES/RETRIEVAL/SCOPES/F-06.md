| **F-06**        | **Legal Structure and Regulatory Advisory**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Advises on the legal structure options for a new entity, restructuring, or specific transaction, ensuring compliance with Saudi and GCC legal requirements.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Delivers**    | Legal structure analysis, regulatory compliance assessment, structure recommendation, regulatory pathway design, implementation roadmap.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **How**         | Legal landscape mapping, structure option analysis, regulatory requirement assessment, compliance pathway design, implementation planning.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi legal structure options are defined by the Companies Law (1437H): Limited Liability Company (LLC), Joint Stock Company (JSC), Branch, and Representative Office each have different Saudization, ownership, and governance requirements. MISA regulates foreign investment and sets sector-specific foreign ownership limits. GCC company structures have additional considerations for entities operating across multiple GCC countries. |
