# V7 — Golden Reference Grammar Contract

**STATUS: REFERENCE GOVERNANCE — NOT TEMPLATE AUTHORITY**

Approved/golden examples may teach abstract grammar: question/thesis pattern, topology, density, focal hierarchy, reading path, evidence integration, connector discipline, typography rhythm, and why a composition worked.

A golden example must store `why_it_works` and `anti_copy_rules`. It may never authorize copying exact layout, client facts, logos, colors, wording, quantities, or Western/Arabic numeral style against current authority.

The current GP01 demo may be treated as a **candidate exemplar only** until its connector/bottom-rail polish and independent runtime QA are complete; it is not promoted as an immutable gold template by this release.
