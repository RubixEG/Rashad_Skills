# Human Capital, Organization Change, and Capability Transfer

## Senior-partner questions
- Who must adopt what behavior?
- What roles, skills, culture, and leadership are required?
- How will readiness, training, adoption, and capability transfer be measured?
- How will change sustain?

## Benchmark practice lenses
- McKinsey people and organizational performance lens
- BCG people-strategy lens
- Deloitte human-capital lens
- PwC workforce lens
- Mercer-style workforce analytics lens

## Preferred artifact families
- Stakeholder change map
- Change journey
- Role and skill architecture
- Training-to-proficiency pathway
- Adoption dashboard
- Capability-transfer staircase
- Leadership alignment map

## Government evaluator test
- Is the section easy to score against the RFP?
- Are commitments feasible, measurable, and traceable?
- Are client dependencies and acceptance criteria explicit?
- Is the Arabic terminology native and precise?

## Evidence expectations
- direct relevance to the scope;
- recent and verifiable proof;
- named responsibility and outcome;
- no client leakage or unsupported credential.

## Anti-patterns
- training calendar as change strategy
- communications-only change
- no adoption measurement

## Release condition
The line-of-service partner must approve the reasoning, the government evaluator must approve navigability and compliance, and the artifact director must approve the visual relationship before release.
