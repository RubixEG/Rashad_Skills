# Golden Visual Master → Semantic Reconstruction Contract

The approved Golden Visual Master (GVM) is the visual-design truth for artifact-heavy pages.

## Reconstruction map
For each page, preserve:
- focal anchor and eye path;
- relative visual mass;
- page-family composition;
- artifact nodes and grouping;
- semantic edges/directionality;
- labels and annotation positions;
- hierarchy, whitespace, density and rhythm;
- imagery crop/focal point;
- text-safe zones;
- brand signature zone.

HTML/SVG is successful only if it reproduces the intended visual relationship without cardifying or flattening the artifact.

If native reconstruction cannot reach the fidelity floor, declare `NATIVE_RECONSTRUCTION_FIDELITY_FAIL` and switch to governed hybrid raster + native text/brand overlays.
