# Bid and Win Strategy Engine

## Bid thesis
Define:
- what the client is really trying to achieve;
- why the challenge is difficult;
- what must be true for success;
- why the proposed approach is credible;
- what differentiates the bidder;
- what evidence proves it.

## Win-theme test
Each win theme must be:
- relevant to an evaluator criterion;
- differentiated;
- provable;
- repeatable across sections without copy-paste;
- translated into a delivery commitment or design choice.

## Prohibited
- generic claims such as “best-in-class” without proof;
- differentiation based only on adjectives;
- promises that exceed the evidence, team, budget, or timeline.
