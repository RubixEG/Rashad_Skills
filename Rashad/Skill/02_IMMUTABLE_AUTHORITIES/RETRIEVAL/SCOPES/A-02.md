| **A-02**        | **Business Unit Strategy**                                                                                                                                                                                        |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Defines the competitive strategy for a specific business unit or division within a larger organisation. Answers: how do we win in this specific market?                                                           |
| **Delivers**    | Business unit strategy, competitive positioning statement, value proposition design, business unit performance targets, strategic roadmap.                                                                        |
| **How**         | Market analysis, competitive landscape mapping, customer segmentation, value proposition design, strategic option development and evaluation.                                                                     |
| **Saudi & GCC** | For Saudi government: equivalent to directorate-level strategy within a ministry. Must align with the ministry overall mandate and the relevant Vision 2030 sector strategy (health, education, logistics, etc.). |
