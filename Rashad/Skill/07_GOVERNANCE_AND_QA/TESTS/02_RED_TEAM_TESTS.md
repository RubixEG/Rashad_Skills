# Red-Team Tests

The red team attempts to:
- find an unanswered mandatory requirement;
- reject the proposal on a pass/fail condition;
- identify an unsupported credential or claim;
- expose contradiction between scope, team, timeline, and price;
- find an unrealistic acceptance or security commitment;
- identify vendor lock-in or missing exit strategy;
- show that a visual is decorative rather than analytical;
- detect repeated slides or section duplication;
- find Arabic ambiguity or mistranslation;
- break HTML-to-PDF or PDF-to-PPT parity.

Release is blocked until critical and major findings are closed or explicitly accepted by an authorized decision maker.
