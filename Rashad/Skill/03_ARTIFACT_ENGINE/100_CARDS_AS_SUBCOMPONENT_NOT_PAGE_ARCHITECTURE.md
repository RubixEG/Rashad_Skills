# Cards as Subcomponents, Not Page Architecture

Cards are not banned. MWAN-quality pages often use bounded panels effectively.

The rule is:
- use cards/panels for discrete evidence, labels, evaluative criteria or supporting annotations;
- do not let equal cards become the **entire analytical architecture** when a stronger system/flow/network/matrix exists;
- a page may contain many small panels if they are governed by a visible higher-order artifact.

The anti-pattern is not “card”. The anti-pattern is **card-only thinking**.
