| **B-10**        | **Mandate Review and Clarity**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Reviews and clarifies the organisation legal mandate, strategic role, and boundaries of authority to resolve ambiguity and enable effective operation.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Delivers**    | Mandate analysis report, mandate gap identification, recommended mandate clarification, authority boundary recommendations, regulatory pathway for mandate formalisation.                                                                                                                                              |
| **How**         | Legal mandate review, stakeholder interviews on mandate interpretation, gap between mandate and operation identification, overlap and ambiguity mapping, regulatory clarification pathway design.                                                                                                                      |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Mandate clarity is one of the most common root causes of performance problems in Saudi government entities. Multiple ministries and agencies frequently have overlapping mandates. Resolution requires understanding the relevant Royal Decrees, Council of Ministers decisions, and organisational legal instruments. |
