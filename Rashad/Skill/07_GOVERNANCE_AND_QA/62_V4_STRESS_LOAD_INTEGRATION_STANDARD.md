# V4 Stress, Load & Integration Standard

## Unit level
Each machine gate has at least one clean fixture and one deliberately broken fixture.

## Integration level
A representative six-page pilot must include:
- cover hero;
- strategic relationship/system page;
- dense evidence/table page;
- timeline/journey page;
- risk/clarification page;
- final decision page.

## Stress level
Inject long Arabic, mixed Latin tokens, additional evidence density, font scaling, and connector rerouting.

## Load level
Render the full pilot repeatedly and compare stable geometry/pixel fingerprints after `document.fonts.ready`.

## Cutover
Full-deck generation is blocked until the representative pilot passes all applicable hard gates.
