| **H-03**        | **Customer and Market Research**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs and executes primary and secondary research to understand customer needs, market dynamics, and competitive landscape.                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Delivers**    | Research design, data collection, analysis and synthesis, insight report, strategic recommendations.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **How**         | Research design, primary research (interviews, surveys, focus groups), secondary research (desk research, data analysis), synthesis and insight development, recommendation development.                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi market research requires Arabic-language instruments, understanding of gender-separated research requirements, awareness of response bias in authoritarian contexts, and sensitivity to topics that may be culturally sensitive. Online panels in Saudi Arabia have significantly improved in quality and scale over recent years. |
