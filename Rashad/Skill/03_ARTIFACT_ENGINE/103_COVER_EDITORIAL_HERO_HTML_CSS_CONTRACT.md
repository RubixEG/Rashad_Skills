# Cover Editorial Hero HTML/CSS Contract

Mandatory default:
- image occupies the physical **left** side, normally 52–60% of the canvas;
- image must be directly relevant to the engagement topic and consulting-grade/editorial in tone;
- right 40–48% remains a clean native text zone;
- `Rubix | Client` appears on one optical line on the left, exact assets only;
- title has decisive scale and limited metadata;
- no generic card layout on the cover;
- no random futuristic visualization unless it directly fits the engagement and remains restrained.

The image may be generated or sourced, but final brand/text remain native governed elements.
