# Golden Visual Master Full-Page Generation Authority

STATUS: HARD VISUAL DELIVERY AUTHORITY — v2.6.4.7

## Purpose
Use full-page generated images to preserve consulting-grade artifact richness and avoid renderer-driven downgrades.

## Admission conditions
A full-page generated slide image may become a Golden Visual Master only if:
- it was requested as a page master under a Page Spec and Deck Visual DNA;
- it follows the approved section/page artifact intent;
- it is visually stronger than a generic card-grid fallback;
- Arabic visual flow and reading logic are structurally correct;
- logos and official marks are either exact governed overlays or explicitly accepted after brand QA;
- official facts and values are checked against the source content;
- it passes page-level visual QA and continuity QA;
- it is frozen with SHA-256.

## Allowed visual language
Government-ready and consulting-grade compositions may include:
- institutional architecture, cityscapes and Saudi context;
- people/operations/devices/screens when relevant;
- dashboard-like or system-like visuals as illustrative/analytical metaphors;
- complex maps, flows, decision systems, Gantt-like visuals, governance models, risk architectures and evidence maps;
- high-density pages where the density is controlled, readable and analytically justified.

## Banned weak substitutes
- generic equal cards used as the dominant layout across many pages;
- decorative icons with no relationship logic;
- random AI glow/orbits without analytical purpose;
- repeated page grammar that ignores the page relationship class;
- native reconstruction that weakens the artifact.

## Quality floor
A page may be rejected even if it is technically clean when it is materially weaker than the MWAN/reference visual quality benchmark for its page family.

## v2.6.4.9 usage
Full-page generation is now primarily used for visual imagination/reference or explicit GVM mode. It is not the default final production route when HTML/SVG/CSS can reproduce the approved composition faithfully.
