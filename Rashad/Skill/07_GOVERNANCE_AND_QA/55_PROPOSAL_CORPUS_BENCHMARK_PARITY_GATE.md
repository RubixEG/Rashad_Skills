
# Proposal Corpus Benchmark Parity Gate

The current page is compared with relevant internal corpus patterns for:
- content density;
- hierarchy;
- artifact/evidence integration;
- executive polish;
- section rhythm;
- co-brand strength.

The corpus is a lower-bound/knowledge source, not a template. A page may differ stylistically and still pass if it is stronger. Repeated weak historical patterns do not become acceptance standards.
