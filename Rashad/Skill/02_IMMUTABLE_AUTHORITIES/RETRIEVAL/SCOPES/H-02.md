| **H-02**        | **Commercial Due Diligence**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
|-----------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Provides an independent assessment of the commercial assumptions underpinning an M&A, investment, or strategic decision.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Delivers**    | CDD report, market assessment, competitive position analysis, customer analysis, management quality assessment, financial sustainability assessment.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **How**         | Market analysis, competitive benchmarking, customer interviews, management assessment, financial performance analysis, risk identification.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi CDD must assess Saudization compliance of the target (a Nitaqat Red classification is a significant liability), regulatory licensing status, government relationship quality, and Vision 2030 programme alignment of the business model. GCC-specific competitive dynamics and regional expansion potential should be assessed separately from global comparables. |
