| **\<thinking_instructions\>**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Step 1. SECTOR INNOVATION DIAGNOSTIC: Assess the current innovation maturity of this regulated sector across five dimensions: (1) Startup ecosystem density - how many relevant startups exist, at what stage, with what survival rate? (2) Regulatory innovation readiness - does the current regulatory framework enable or block innovation? (3) Corporate innovation appetite - are incumbents actively investing in innovation or defending market share? (4) Talent and capability - does the sector have the human capital to generate and absorb innovation? (5) Capital availability - is there patient capital available for InsurTech/FinTech/RegTech at seed, growth, and scale stages? Label every assessment: Stated Fact, Logical Inference, or Assumption. |
| Step 2. STRATEGIC INTENT: Define what winning looks like for innovation in this sector in 5 years. Be specific: number of innovations at scale, percentage of sector processes transformed, national ranking in sector innovation index, number of startups from zero to Series A, and Vision 2030 KPI contribution. This is the North Star against which all programme design decisions are made.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Step 3. INNOVATION ARCHITECTURE: Design the three-layer innovation system. Layer 1 is Open Innovation: ideas and challenges from the market - sector voice, hackathons, open calls. Layer 2 is Structured Development: converting ideas to solutions - accelerators, incubators, solution studios. Layer 3 is Market Integration: taking validated solutions to scale - go-to-market, regulatory fast-track, partnership facilitation. For each layer: specific programmes, target participants, expected outputs, and success metrics.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Step 4. GOVERNANCE AND OPERATING MODEL: Design who runs the innovation engine, how decisions are made, what authority the innovation function has, how it connects to the regulator's core mandate, and what happens when innovation outputs conflict with existing regulation. Define the Innovation Steering Committee composition, the Programme Management function, and the regulatory sandbox mechanism.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Step 5. IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP: Sequence the build over 18 months. Months 1-3: design and governance. Months 4-9: first programme launches. Months 10-15: full portfolio operational. Months 16-18: measurement, optimisation, and renewal case. Identify the three most important decisions that must be made in the first 30 days.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Step 6. CLOSE: State the single most important strategic choice in this innovation design, the single biggest risk to the strategy succeeding, and the metric that will most honestly tell leadership whether the innovation engine is working at the 12-month mark.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

| **\<methodology\>**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Apply the Open Innovation model (Chesbrough): the regulator is a platform connecting problem owners (incumbents), solution providers (startups), and market validators (distribution partners).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Use the Stage-Gate model for programme design: every idea that enters the innovation system must pass explicit gates before receiving further investment of time, money, or regulatory support.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| For Saudi regulated sectors: the SAMA FinTech Lab, SDAIA AI governance framework, and NCA cybersecurity requirements are precedents for how regulators design innovation enablement. Reference them explicitly where relevant.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Innovation strategy in a regulated sector must include a Regulatory Innovation Pathway - a defined process by which innovations that require regulatory change can be fast-tracked through a sandbox or pilot approval mechanism.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Label every projected number: Stated Fact (from existing data), Logical Inference (from analogous markets), or Assumption (stated basis). Innovation projections are inherently uncertain - intellectual honesty is what builds credibility with leadership.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
