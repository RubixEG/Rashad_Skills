| **C-01**        | **Business Case Development**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Builds the financial and strategic case for a major investment, transformation programme, or strategic initiative.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Delivers**    | Business case document, financial model, investment case, cost-benefit analysis, sensitivity analysis, risk-adjusted return analysis.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **How**         | Benefit identification and quantification, cost estimation, financial modelling, scenario development, sensitivity analysis, risk quantification, investment recommendation.                                                                                                                                            |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi government business cases must include non-financial value dimensions: citizen impact, national KPI contribution, and Vision 2030 programme alignment. MOF requires specific formats for budget allocation requests. Business cases for PPP or privatisation initiatives have additional regulatory requirements. |
