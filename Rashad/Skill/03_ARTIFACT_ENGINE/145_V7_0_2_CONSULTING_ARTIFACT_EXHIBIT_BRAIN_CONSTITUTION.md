# V7.0.2 — Consulting Artifact / Exhibit Brain Constitution

**STATUS: INHERITED CONSTITUTIONAL FOUNDATION — V7.2 EXECUTION LAW IS CURRENT**

## Constitutional definition

**An artifact is not a diagram.**

An artifact is a deliberately designed executive communication object that transforms evidence, argument, relationships, or decisions into the clearest possible visual form.

The simplest valid form must win unless additional visual complexity materially increases understanding.

Geometry primitives such as ring, hub, spine, stack, lane, matrix, timeline, or cards are **implementation techniques only**. They are never the primary hypothesis taxonomy and they carry no selection authority.

## Mandatory reasoning order

`Management Question → Answer / Thesis → Evidence → Counter-case → Executive Implication → Communication Strategy → Simplicity Gate → Visual Hypotheses → Actual Render Search → Actual-Output QA → Deck Director → Master`

No page may start from a preselected diagram family.

## Consulting Artifact Brain

Artifact production is governed by a Council-of-Councils brain, not a single engine or designer role.

### 1. Consulting Argument Council
Owns the management question, answer-first thesis, so-what, decision supported, counterargument, and evidence burden.

### 2. Executive Audience Council
Simulates CEO/GM, CFO, COO, CIO/CTO, CISO, CHRO, Strategy/Transformation, Procurement, Risk, and PMO decision needs.

### 3. Evaluator Simulation Council
Simulates technical, commercial, procurement, government, legal, cyber, data/privacy, and compliance evaluators where applicable.

### 4. Evidence & Epistemic Council
Separates FACT / INFERENCE / ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN / RECOMMENDATION / CALCULATION and blocks unsupported visual claims.

### 5. Communication Strategy Council
Chooses how the argument should be communicated before any geometry is invented.

### 6. Simplicity Council
Asks whether any artifact is needed at all and whether the same answer can be communicated with materially fewer elements.

### 7. Information Design & Perception Council
Owns hierarchy, focal point, reading path, evidence encoding, density, cognitive load, and visual perception.

### 8. Quantitative Exhibit Council
Owns number-led, chart, table, bridge, sensitivity, distribution, and quantitative comparison logic.

### 9. Systems & Architecture Council
Activates only when material system relationships, architecture layers, feedback, dependencies, or operating-model interactions genuinely require it.

### 10. Storytelling & Editorial Council
Owns conclusion-led titles, body compression, narrative continuity, and executive wording.

### 11. Arabic / RTL Composition Council
Owns physical RTL reading order, Arabic hierarchy, mixed-script behavior, numerals, typography, and BiDi integrity.

### 12. Creative Visual Council
Creates visual metaphor, image direction, illustration, and creative composition **after** the argument and communication strategy are locked.

### 13. Brand Council
Styles meaning after meaning is decided. Brand may not decide the analytical structure.

### 14. Production Council
Converts the selected concept into deterministic, editable, collision-safe output with verified assets and export fidelity.

### 15. Independent Exhibit Judge Council
Must be actor/context independent from production and must inspect the actual rendered output for release-candidate selection.

### 16. Artifact Red Team
Attacks generic layout reuse, diagram overuse, fake complexity, weak 5-second comprehension, unsupported hierarchy, and template substitution.

### 17. Deck Artistic Director Council
Judges the complete deck as one communication system and owns rhythm, variation, calm pages, dense pages, narrative pacing, and cross-page repetition.

### 18. Meta-Cognition / Expertise Router
Determines which specialist expertise is genuinely required and whether governed knowledge is ready. Registered expertise does not imply knowledge readiness.

## Expert universe, bounded activation

Rashad may maintain a large registered universe of consulting, executive, evaluator, quantitative, technical, creative, editorial, Arabic, production, accessibility, and QA expertise.

**Do not load the entire universe into each page.** Runtime must activate only the councils and experts required by the specific communication problem.

Missing specialty knowledge must produce an explicit readiness state; it must never be replaced by generic model confidence.

## Partner test before render

Before rendering any candidate, answer:

1. What is the answer?
2. What proves it?
3. What is the simplest communication form that makes the answer obvious?
4. What must the eye see first?
5. If the diagram is removed, is understanding materially reduced?
6. Could the page belong to another RFP by replacing labels only?

If a diagram can be deleted without loss of understanding, delete it.

## Hypothesis law

Forbidden:

`H1 = Ring / H2 = Spine / H3 = Hub / H4 = Stack / H5 = Lane`

Required example logic:

`H1 = Number-led / H2 = Evidence table / H3 = Comparison / H4 = Statement / H5 = System exhibit`

The selected winner may legitimately be the minimal statement or number-led page.

Candidate ID and generation order must never influence ranking.

## Complexity law

Complexity must be **earned by the information relationship**.

A system map, architecture, journey, process, or multi-node diagram is invalid when the same meaning can be communicated more clearly with a number, statement, evidence table, comparison, or decision gateboard.

## Visual reference law

Gold/reference pages are a **Visual Intelligence Corpus**. Extract principles such as hierarchy, density, focal point, whitespace, relationship treatment, evidence treatment, and reading path.

Never copy exact layouts as templates.

## Deck law

The Deck Artistic Director must prevent a multi-page product from becoming a collection of diagrams. Variation is analytical, not decorative.

A deck with systematic diagram overuse, positional winner bias, repeated composition grammar, or actual-render reuse fails even when every page individually passes mechanical QA.


## QA Brain coupling law

Framework certification never certifies a rendered product. For rendered outputs, the independent QA Brain must route the **Actual Pixel / Product Review Council** and the **Executive Simplicity & Artifact Skeptic Council** in addition to the existing QA councils. A page cannot become `RELEASE_CANDIDATE` without actual rendered-pixel review bound to the exact render hash.
