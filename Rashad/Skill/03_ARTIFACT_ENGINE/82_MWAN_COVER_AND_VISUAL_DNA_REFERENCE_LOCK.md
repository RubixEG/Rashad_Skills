# MWAN Cover & Visual DNA Reference Lock

STATUS: REFERENCE-ONLY VISUAL BENCHMARK — v2.6.4.7
SOURCE: Uploaded MWAN.pptx and last-version parity pilot.

## Reference role
MWAN is a visual-quality and production-method benchmark, not a source of client facts for other engagements.

## Cover grammar to preserve
The cover page style is now the default cover pattern for Arabic RFP Summary / competitive-understanding decks unless the owner explicitly chooses another style:
- landscape 16:9 full-page visual;
- physical left side: strong institutional visual scene / sector-specific imagery;
- physical right side: clean white/soft space for title, subtitle and summary;
- co-brand top-left: `Rubix | Client` on one optical baseline, same visible-height logic, transparent PNG assets;
- small output-type label or icon top-right;
- large bold Arabic title block on the right;
- restrained magenta accent line and magenta highlight;
- high-key government/consulting tone; no full-slide black/dark background;
- background depth and richness without hurting readability.

## Logo lock
Both logos must appear in the same horizontal signature line. The client logo must not appear visually weak due to transparent padding. Use optical visible-height measurement and clear-space rules.

## Acceptable cover imagery
Saudi flags, people, devices, screens, dashboards, sector operations, vehicles, buildings and public-sector signals are acceptable when contextually relevant. The prohibition is not the presence of these elements; the prohibition is fake official text, fake logos, incorrect facts, unreadable text, or false evidence.

## Continuity cues
- mostly white/light surface;
- magenta as primary accent;
- subtle geometric/hex background when useful;
- analytical icons and lines used with restraint;
- high-resolution raster quality;
- right-to-left reading hierarchy.
