## Supersession rule
The transcript is evidence of decision history, not an authority by itself. A historical decision becomes runtime-active only when it is compiled as `ACTIVE` in the current Decision Ledger and does not conflict with a newer v2.5/current-user authority.

## Conflict behavior
Never silently merge contradictory decisions. Prefer the higher authority within the same domain. If two same-level active authorities conflict and the difference materially changes the requested product, create a conflict record; ask the owner only when it is genuinely blocking.
