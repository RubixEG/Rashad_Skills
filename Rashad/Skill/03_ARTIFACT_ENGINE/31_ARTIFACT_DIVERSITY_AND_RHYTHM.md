# 31 — Artifact Diversity & Narrative Rhythm Authority

STATUS: ACTIVE QUALITY AUTHORITY — v2.3

## Purpose
Prevent a technically correct deck from becoming visually repetitive, card-heavy, or cognitively flat.

## Rhythm rules
- Do not repeat the same dominant artifact family on consecutive analytical pages unless the argument genuinely requires continuity.
- Change the dominant information relationship at least every three analytical pages when the content supports it.
- Alternate deliberately between synthesis, system, evidence/detail, and decision pages.
- Use one dominant focal point per page.
- A dense page should normally be followed by a lower-density synthesis/decision page when narrative permits.
- Tables are for precise lookup; maps/flows are for relationships; charts are for quantitative patterns; cards are for genuinely independent items.

## Diversity is not novelty
Do not create variety by changing shapes/colors randomly. Variety must come from the **information relationship**.

## Repetition detector
Flag for Artifact Council when:
- three pages use the same card/grid topology;
- two consecutive pages have identical visual hierarchy and density with different text only;
- every section opens with the same composition;
- diagrams use arrows/rings/hexagons decoratively without semantic edges;
- color becomes the only means of differentiation.

## Section rhythm target
A strong section often follows:
`promise → context/diagnostic → conclusion/design principle → solution/method → evidence/detail → implementation/control → transition`.

This is guidance, not a fixed page count.
