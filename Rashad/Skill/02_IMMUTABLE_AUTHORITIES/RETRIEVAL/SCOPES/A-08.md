| **A-08**        | **Vision and Mission Development**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Develops or refreshes the organisation vision, mission, and values to provide direction and motivate the organisation.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Delivers**    | Vision and mission statements, values framework, strategic intent articulation, internal communication plan, stakeholder validation process.                                                                                                                                                             |
| **How**         | Leadership interviews, stakeholder workshops, external benchmarking, draft development, validation and refinement, communication design.                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Vision and mission development in Saudi organisations must align with Vision 2030 national aspirations and the organisation specific mandate. For government entities: the vision must be consistent with the ministry mandate as defined in the relevant Royal Decree or Council of Ministers decision. |
