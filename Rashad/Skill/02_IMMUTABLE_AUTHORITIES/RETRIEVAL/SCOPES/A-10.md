| **A-10**        | **Joint Venture and Alliance Strategy**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs strategic partnerships, joint ventures, and alliances as an alternative to organic growth or full acquisition.                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Delivers**    | Partnership strategy, JV structure and governance design, alliance evaluation framework, partnership operating model, governance and value capture mechanism.                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **How**         | Partnership rationale development, partner screening, term sheet design, governance structure development, operating model design, relationship management framework.                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Joint ventures in Saudi Arabia and GCC require specific legal structure considerations: foreign ownership restrictions, Saudization requirements for the JV workforce, and sector-specific licensing that may require Saudi partner involvement. MISA approval pathways and the Companies Law (1437H) govern the structural options available. |
