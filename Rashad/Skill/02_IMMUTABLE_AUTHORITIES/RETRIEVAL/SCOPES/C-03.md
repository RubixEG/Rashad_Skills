| **C-03**        | **Cost Reduction Programme**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Identifies and designs a programme to reduce costs sustainably without destroying strategic capability or damaging culture.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Delivers**    | Cost reduction opportunity assessment, cost reduction programme design, initiative business cases, implementation roadmap, savings tracking framework.                                                                                                                                                               |
| **How**         | Cost structure analysis, opportunity identification across all cost categories, initiative prioritisation, implementation planning, saving validation methodology design.                                                                                                                                            |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi cost reduction programmes must assess Saudization compliance implications of any headcount changes. Government cost reduction must work within the constraints of civil service employment protections. Private sector must comply with Saudi Labour Law provisions on redundancy and end-of-service benefits. |
