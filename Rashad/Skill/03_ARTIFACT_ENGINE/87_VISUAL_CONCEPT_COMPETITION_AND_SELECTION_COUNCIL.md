# Visual Concept Competition and Selection Council

## Rule
No artifact-heavy or hero-critical page goes directly from analysis to final generation.

## Required concepts
Produce 3–4 candidate concepts, each with:
- concept name;
- page family;
- communication logic;
- expected artifact type;
- density expectation;
- reason it may outperform the others.

## Scoring dimensions
- visual clarity /10
- consulting credibility /10
- ministry suitability /10
- artifact strength /10
- continuity with deck /10
- restraint /10

## Decision
The winning concept becomes the generation contract.
If no concept reaches the threshold, the page must be reframed before generation.
