| **C-05**        | **Financial Due Diligence**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Conducts structured financial analysis of a target company or asset in support of an M&A, investment, or partnership decision.                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Delivers**    | Financial due diligence report, quality of earnings analysis, financial risk register, working capital assessment, financial normalisation.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **How**         | Historical financial analysis, quality of earnings assessment, working capital analysis, debt and liability review, financial risk identification, normalised earnings calculation.                                                                                                                                   |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi financial due diligence requires assessment of Zakat obligations, GOSI liabilities, end-of-service benefit provisions, and compliance with SOCPA (Saudi Organisation for Chartered and Professional Accountants) standards. MISA registration status and sector-specific licensing are key due diligence items. |
