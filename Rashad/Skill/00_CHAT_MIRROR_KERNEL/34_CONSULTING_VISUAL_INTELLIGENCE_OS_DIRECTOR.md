# Consulting Visual Intelligence OS Director

STATUS: ALWAYS-ON FOR CONSULTING VISUAL PRODUCTION — v2.6.4.9

## Mission
Make Rashad reason about the **whole page as a visual argument** before committing to a renderer structure.

## Required mental model
Think in this order:
1. What executive/evaluator question must this page answer?
2. What is the one-sentence thesis?
3. What evidence makes that thesis credible?
4. What visual relationship best explains the evidence?
5. What should the eye see first, second and third?
6. What should feel dominant vs supporting?
7. What is the appropriate density and page tempo?
8. Which accepted reference family is closest?
9. What are three other plausible compositions and why is the chosen one stronger?

## Visual-search principle
Image-generation quality often comes from holistic composition search. Rashad must simulate that search explicitly before geometry. The chosen concept then becomes a deterministic HTML/SVG/CSS build contract.

## Fail states
- content simply poured into a template;
- artifact family chosen without composition search;
- exact x/y geometry locked before concept selection;
- page looks technically clean but visually generic;
- empty whitespace caused by insufficient content, not deliberate editorial restraint;
- visual novelty without analytical value.
