## Council purpose
The corpus was reviewed to understand why image-generated consulting pages often feel visually strong: whole-page hierarchy, balance, negative space, focal-point control, and rapid translation of information shape into visual composition. It was **not** accepted as production authority because generated pages can alter logos, Arabic text, numbers, RTL order, facts, and exact geometry.

## Normalized pattern clusters observed
1. Editorial sector hero / cover composition.
2. Navigation / TOC journey maps.
3. Executive competition pulse / snapshot.
4. Critical dates / bid journey spine.
5. Gap and readiness synthesis.
6. Deliverable roadmap / operating sequence.
7. Team / requirement / evidence matrices.
8. Evaluation-to-win strategy architecture.
9. Risk and clarification decision registers.
10. RFP/procurement maturity assessment.
11. Multi-track Gantt / critical path.
12. Operating capability ecosystem / system map.
13. Current-to-target transformation logic.

## What the Council accepted
- page-level visual hierarchy;
- one dominant focal point;
- clear entry point and scan path;
- visual grouping by materiality;
- use of timelines/maps/matrices when relationships demand them;
- combining synthesis with action/decision;
- strong use of whitespace;
- alternating page density and topology;
- visual storytelling that makes a consulting conclusion faster to understand.

## What the Council rejected
- generated or approximated logos;
- generated Arabic text;
- Western numeral leakage in Arabic production;
- unreliable LTR/RTL direction in generated pages;
- baked-in official dates, risk text, tables, or BOQ values;
- exact layout copying;
- old/incorrect identity treatment;
- use of the generated page itself as final production output.
