# Consulting Visual Golden Suite

Before declaring the architecture production-ready, test at least:
1. cover with topic-specific left hero;
2. executive opportunity snapshot;
3. strategic reading / thesis page;
4. scope architecture;
5. 20-service / 30-POC factory flow;
6. delivery journey;
7. team/capacity architecture;
8. evaluation / criteria-to-win;
9. evidence readiness;
10. risk heatmap + response;
11. commercial / BOQ logic;
12. technical architecture;
13. clarifications;
14. assumptions/gaps;
15. bid decision cockpit.

Each case must pass content depth, visual concept selection, HTML/SVG render, browser critique, CVFS threshold and applicable QA Harness gates.
