# Page and Pack Strength Control

## Why this exists
Page-level quality alone is not enough. A pack can contain individually acceptable pages and still feel repetitive, weak, or template-driven.

## Pack-level controls
Before rendering, create a table containing:
- page number and role;
- page thesis;
- composition family;
- artifact family;
- dominant relationship;
- density band;
- focal visual;
- visual asset role;
- benchmark references;
- target strength score.

## Diversity requirements
For packs with 10 or more pages:
- card-led pages normally <= 30%;
- no more than 2 consecutive pages share a composition family;
- no more than 3 pages in the whole pack use the same exact component arrangement unless the content genuinely requires repetition;
- at least one power artifact in every 3 analytical pages;
- include deliberate rhythm between synthesis, diagnostic, relationship, evidence, control, and narrative-reset pages.

## Strength distribution
- no critical page below 90;
- no standard analytical page below 85;
- no production page below 82;
- section/pack average >= 87;
- strongest pages should occur at the strategic thesis, solution/methodology, evaluation, governance, and decision points—not only on the cover.

## Repetition rejection
A pack is rejected even when collision-free if the reviewer can describe most pages as:
`title + subtitle + equal cards`.

## v2.6.4 section-board control
For any multi-page section with an approved Golden Section Board, evaluate section-level rhythm and visual fidelity against that board in addition to page-strength scores. A pack fails if native production materially weakens the approved board even when individual pages remain collision-free.
