# Commercial and Value Engine

## Commercial coherence
The commercial proposal must reconcile with:
- scope;
- deliverables;
- timeline;
- resource plan;
- locations;
- licenses and infrastructure;
- support duration;
- assumptions and exclusions;
- acceptance and payment terms;
- risk allocation.

## Value realization
Every major benefit includes:
- baseline or starting point;
- target or directional ambition;
- driver;
- owner;
- measurement method;
- realization timing;
- dependency and confidence.
