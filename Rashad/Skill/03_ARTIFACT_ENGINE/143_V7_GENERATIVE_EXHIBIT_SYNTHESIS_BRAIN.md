# V7 — Generative Exhibit Synthesis Brain

**STATUS: INHERITED FOUNDATION — CURRENT EXECUTION ROUTES THROUGH V7.2 ARTIFACT BRAIN**

For critical analytical pages:
`Cognitive Packet → Semantic Graph → five materially different communication strategies → page-specific Visual Concept briefs → internal concept render search → critique → Top-2 refinement when warranted → selected concept → Art Direction → PRODUCTION_PAGE_RENDER → actual-pixel QA → repair → user-visible Delivery Gate`.

## Hypothesis diversity
The five hypotheses are **communication strategies**, not topology or geometry variants. They must differ materially in how they make the business answer understandable, for example statement-led, number-led, evidence-led, table-led, comparison-led, chart-led, decision-led, matrix-led, image-led, process/system-led or hybrid strategies.

They must span at least three communication families and include at least one minimal/non-diagram option. Candidate position, color changes, names, small geometry shifts, or swapping RING/HUB/SPINE/STACK/LANE do not create a new hypothesis.

After a strategy is chosen for internal concept search, its concepts should also differ meaningfully in focal logic, information layering, reading path, proof encoding, hierarchy, negative space and composition. Geometry is downstream implementation detail only.

## Internal concept-search target
Critical pages generate five internal concept candidates. Preferred search when runtime permits:
`5 internal concept renders → critique → Top 2 → 2 refinements each → final concept comparison` (up to 9 internal compositions).

These are **concept-search pixels**, not user-visible production pages. No minimum concept-render count, concept hash or framework score can substitute for the v7.1 production-render + pixel-QA + delivery contract.

## No template fallback
Examples and page families inform principles, constraints and QA; they are never a menu of layouts. Cards and geometry primitives remain supporting tools only. Complex relational structure is used only when the business relationship genuinely requires it; statement, number, evidence, table, chart, comparison, image-led and other simpler strategies must remain first-class options.
