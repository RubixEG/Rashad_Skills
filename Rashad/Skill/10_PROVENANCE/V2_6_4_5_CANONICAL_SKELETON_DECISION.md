# v2.6.4.5 Canonical Proposal Skeleton Decision

DATE: 2026-08-12
OWNER DECISION: "this is final one never change it"

## Decision
Freeze the proposal skeleton presented immediately before this release as the permanent canonical Rashad proposal architecture.

## Language implementation
Two canonical authorities are maintained:
- a professional English formulation;
- a professional Arabic formulation suitable for government/institutional consulting proposals.

They are not literal translations. Structural IDs and semantic functions are locked in parity.

## Architecture consequence
Previous active rules that automatically allowed a mandatory RFP hierarchy to replace the Rubix house skeleton are superseded for proposal architecture. RFP obligations still control facts, forms, evaluation, compliance and submission mechanics; they are mapped into/supplement the canonical architecture. An irreconcilable legally mandatory structure conflict is blocked and escalated rather than silently changing the skeleton.
