| **D-04**        | **Data Strategy and Analytics**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Defines the organisation approach to data as a strategic asset: governance, quality, integration, analytics capability, and reporting architecture.                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Delivers**    | Data strategy, data governance framework, data architecture design, analytics capability roadmap, data quality improvement plan.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **How**         | Data landscape assessment, data maturity assessment, governance design, architecture design, analytics use case prioritisation, capability gap assessment, roadmap development.                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi data strategy must comply with the Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL 2021) administered by SDAIA, the National Data Management Office standards, and sector-specific data regulations. Government data classification requirements and cross-ministry data sharing protocols must be integrated into the governance design. |
