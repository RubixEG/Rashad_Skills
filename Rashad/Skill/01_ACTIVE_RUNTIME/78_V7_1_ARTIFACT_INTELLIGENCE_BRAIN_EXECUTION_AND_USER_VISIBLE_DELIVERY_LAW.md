# V7.1 — Artifact Intelligence Brain Execution & User-Visible Delivery Law

**STATUS: CURRENT INHERITED ARTIFACT-DELIVERY FOUNDATION UNDER V7.2 — V7.2 EXECUTION-PROOF LAW CONTROLS ADMISSION**

## Purpose
Close the execution gap between consulting reasoning and the pixels delivered to the user. A correct Artifact Brain architecture is not sufficient: every user-visible visual output must prove that the Brain actually controlled concept selection, production rendering, pixel QA and repair.

## Artifact definition
An Artifact is an executive communication object that makes a business answer faster, clearer and more decision-useful. It may be a statement, number, table, chart, evidence composition, comparison, matrix, decision gateboard, process, system map, image-led page or hybrid exhibit. Diagram complexity is never the default.

## Mandatory separation
`Communication Strategy ≠ Visual Concept ≠ Geometry ≠ Production Render ≠ User-Visible Artifact`.

- **Communication Strategy** answers how the argument should be communicated.
- **Visual Concept** defines page-specific focal logic, composition, hierarchy, proof encoding, density and negative-space use.
- **Geometry** is an implementation technique only.
- **Concept Render** is internal search evidence only.
- **Production Render** is the actual user-facing page candidate.
- **User-Visible Artifact** is a production render that has passed actual-pixel QA and the repair loop.

`COMMUNICATION_STRATEGY_CONCEPT_RENDER_*` may never be used as a user-visible master.

## No-output shortcut law
No RFP Summary, proposal deck, PPTX, PDF or user-visible artifact may be created directly from a Content Pack, Markdown summary, section label or communication-strategy label.

Before a user-visible page is admissible, it must possess:
1. Page Contract;
2. Consulting Cognitive Packet;
3. Evidence lineage;
4. Artifact Intent;
5. Semantic Graph;
6. five materially distinct communication-strategy hypotheses across ≥3 families, including ≥1 minimal/non-diagram option;
7. five distinct concept-render hashes;
8. Artifact Skeptic results;
9. selected Visual Concept;
10. page-specific Art Direction brief;
11. `PRODUCTION_PAGE_RENDER` proof and hash;
12. verified brand/co-brand preflight;
13. material Claim → Evidence → Visual binding;
14. independent actual-pixel quality review bound to the exact production-render hash;
15. closed repair loop or explicit first-pass quality PASS;
16. deck-level actual-output QA when part of a multi-page product;
17. user-visible Delivery Certificate bound to the exact PPTX/PDF hash.

Missing any required item → `BLOCK_DELIVERY`. The system may continue internal thinking/search, but may not substitute a generic slide.

## User-visible output classifications
- `CONTENT_DRAFT` — content only; not an artifact.
- `INTERNAL_CONCEPT_DRAFT` — concept/search evidence; never user-visible as final artifact.
- `USER_VISIBLE_ARTIFACT_DRAFT` — production-rendered, actual-pixel-reviewed and repair-closed; visible Draft mark allowed.
- `RELEASE_CANDIDATE` — additionally independent release evidence + parity + proof index.
- `RELEASED` — only Rashad Brain Release Chair.

Legacy `ARTIFACT_DRAFT` wording resolves to `USER_VISIBLE_ARTIFACT_DRAFT`; it does not mean internal wireframe.

## Partner / Simplicity law
Before production rendering, ask:
- What is the one answer?
- What proves it?
- What is the simplest visual form that makes the answer obvious?
- What must the eye see first?
- If the diagram is removed, is understanding lost?
- Could this composition belong to another RFP by changing labels only?

If a simpler page communicates the same business truth, the complex page loses.

## Reference doctrine
Historical/golden examples teach quality principles only. They are not a menu of layouts. Exact composition copying or label-swapping is prohibited.

## Quality truthfulness
Framework/unit/stress certification never substitutes for QA of the actual engagement output. `PASS` on the Rashad OS package does not certify a future deck. User-visible delivery requires exact-file actual-output evidence for that deck.
