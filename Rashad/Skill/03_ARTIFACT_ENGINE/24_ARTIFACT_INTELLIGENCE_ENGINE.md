# 24 — Artifact Intelligence Engine

STATUS: **ACTIVE AUTHORITY — v2.3**
PURPOSE: Convert consulting meaning into a deliberate visual information system without template-first behavior.

## Core principle
A premium artifact is not a decorated slide. It is a **visual proof structure** that makes a material relationship easier to understand, evaluate, compare, decide, or execute.

Artifact quality is evaluated on two axes:
1. **Analytical fidelity** — the artifact preserves the argument, evidence, nodes, edges, uncertainty, and decision logic.
2. **Visual cognition** — the artifact creates hierarchy, focus, reading path, contrast, and spatial logic that reduce evaluator effort.

Neither axis may compensate for failure on the other.

## Page reasoning contract
For every analytical page, resolve in order:

1. `EXECUTIVE_QUESTION` — what decision/evaluator question is being answered?
2. `THESIS` — one sentence the reader should retain after five seconds.
3. `EVIDENCE` — facts/requirements/calculations supporting the thesis.
4. `IMPLICATION` — why the evidence matters.
5. `DOMINANT_RELATIONSHIP` — sequence, hierarchy, dependency, comparison, system, time, ownership, cause/effect, risk/control, decision, evidence, maturity, value, quantitative, or composite.
6. `ARTIFACT_INTENT` — what cognitive task must the artifact perform?
7. `ARTIFACT_FAMILY` — normalized family; bespoke design is allowed if no existing family preserves meaning.
8. `SEMANTIC_NODES` — all entities that must remain visible.
9. `SEMANTIC_EDGES` — all required relationships/dependencies.
10. `READING_PATH` — physical order, including RTL behavior.
11. `FOCAL_POINT` — the dominant takeaway.
12. `DENSITY_CLASS` — low / medium / high.
13. `EDITABILITY_CLASS` — native / hybrid / ingredient-only.
14. `PRODUCTION_CONSTRAINTS` — brand, RTL, numerals, assets, safe zones, minimum legibility.

## Artifact strength rule
A stronger artifact communicates **more relationship with less cognitive friction**, not merely more content.

Do not weaken an artifact to fit a layout. If it does not fit:
1. paginate within the same artifact family;
2. recompose geometry;
3. split the cognitive question;
4. return to Artifact Council.

Never collapse a lifecycle, system, dependency, governance, evidence chain, or decision architecture into generic equal cards just to fit.

## Composite relationships
Many consulting pages contain two relationships, for example:
- timeline + ownership;
- deliverables + acceptance evidence;
- evaluation weight + win response;
- system + feedback loop;
- risk + treatment + owner;
- workstream + dependency.

Composite pages are permitted only when one relationship is explicitly dominant and the secondary relationship reinforces it. If both compete for attention, separate pages.

## Visual cognition targets
The artifact should deliberately manage:
- `entry_point` — where the eye starts;
- `primary_scan` — first 3–5 seconds;
- `secondary_scan` — evidence/details;
- `decision_anchor` — where the conclusion/action lands;
- `whitespace` — hierarchy, not emptiness;
- `visual_weight` — proportionate to materiality;
- `edge_salience` — dependencies/relationships visible enough to be understood;
- `exception_salience` — risk/gap/conflict visually distinct without over-coloring.

## No template gravity
Historical/generated/reference pages may inspire topology, but never dictate exact coordinates, exact composition, branding, or client facts. The page is rebuilt from the current question and relationship.
