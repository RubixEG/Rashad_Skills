| **C-10**        | **Privatisation and PPP Advisory**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Advises on the privatisation of government assets or the design and structuring of public-private partnerships.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Delivers**    | Privatisation readiness assessment, privatisation strategy, PPP structuring recommendation, financial model, regulatory pathway, transaction documentation support.                                                                                                                                                       |
| **How**         | Asset assessment, market sounding, privatisation model selection, financial modelling, regulatory pathway mapping, transaction structure design, investor materials preparation.                                                                                                                                          |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi privatisation is governed by the National Centre for Privatization (NCP) framework and sector-specific regulations. Vision 2030 privatisation targets cover multiple sectors (water, airports, healthcare, education, sport). Each sector has specific privatisation requirements and regulatory approval pathways. |
