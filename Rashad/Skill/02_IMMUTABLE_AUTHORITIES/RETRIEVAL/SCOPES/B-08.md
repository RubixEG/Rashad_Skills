| **B-08**        | **Performance Management System Design**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs the performance management system that measures, manages, and improves individual, team, and organisational performance.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Delivers**    | KPI framework, performance management process design, performance review calendar, performance data architecture, incentive and consequence framework.                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **How**         | Current system assessment, KPI framework development, cascading mechanism design, review process design, data system requirements, pilot design and testing.                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Saudi & GCC** | NCHRM sets standards for government employee performance management. Any government performance management system must align with these standards and the civil service grade structure. For private sector: the performance management system must comply with Saudi Labour Law provisions on probation, termination, and end-of-service benefits. |
