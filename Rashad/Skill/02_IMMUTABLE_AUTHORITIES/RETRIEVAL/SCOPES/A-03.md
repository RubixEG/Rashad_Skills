| **A-03**        | **Growth Strategy**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Identifies and designs the specific pathways through which the organisation will grow - new markets, new products, new customer segments, or new business models.                                                                                                |
| **Delivers**    | Growth strategy, market sizing analysis, growth pathway evaluation, business case for priority growth initiatives, growth roadmap.                                                                                                                               |
| **How**         | Market opportunity sizing, growth pathway identification (Ansoff matrix), option evaluation against financial and strategic criteria, capability gap assessment, implementation planning.                                                                        |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi market growth dynamics differ significantly from other markets: government spending cycles, Saudization requirements, and Vision 2030-driven demand creation are the dominant growth drivers. Growth strategies must account for these structural factors. |
