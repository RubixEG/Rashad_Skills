# Section Visual Board Director — Always-On Multi-Page Visual Coherence Role

STATUS: ACTIVE / ADDITIVE / NON-DESTRUCTIVE
SCOPE: multi-page analytical sections and RFP Summary role expansions. The cover remains governed separately by the Cover Art Director.

## Purpose
Preserve the holistic visual reasoning quality that is often strongest when a multi-page section is conceived as one visual system rather than as isolated pages.

This role does not replace Artifact Intelligence, the deterministic composer, or any current content authority. It adds a visual-coherence bridge between approved page blueprints and native production.

## Mandatory trigger
Run this role when any one of the following is true:
- a logical RFP Summary role expands to two or more analytical pages;
- a proposal section contains three or more pages that must read as one argument;
- the section contains multiple related artifacts whose rhythm, emphasis, and visual continuity materially affect comprehension;
- independent page-by-page composition would likely create repetitive cards, disconnected layouts, or inconsistent hierarchy.

## Golden Section Board
After content, evidence, information relationships, Artifact Intents, nodes/edges, and preliminary page blueprints are approved, create one **Golden Section Board** for the section.

The Golden Section Board is a single large visual reference containing the section's page compositions together, normally 2–8 pages per board. If the section exceeds eight pages, create sequential boards under one frozen Section Visual Charter.

The board MUST be:
- reference-only;
- non-client-facing;
- non-releasable;
- free of production text;
- free of logos;
- free of official numbers/dates;
- free of client facts and labels;
- free of final tables or exact analytical content.

Use blank text zones, abstract placeholder bars, textless iconography, diagram topology, image zones, whitespace, visual weight, semantic accent placement, and page-to-page rhythm only.

If the generated board contains readable text, logos, official numbers, invented facts, or client-specific marks, reject and regenerate it.

## What the board is allowed to decide
The board may establish:
- section-level visual rhythm;
- focal point and visual mass per page;
- relative scale of primary and secondary artifacts;
- whitespace strategy;
- composition family variation;
- image/diagram/table-zone balance;
- relationship of neighboring pages;
- continuation or reset behavior;
- semantic accent distribution;
- macro geometry and reading flow.

## What the board may never decide
It may never invent or alter:
- facts;
- wording;
- Arabic text;
- numbers;
- dates;
- logos;
- tables;
- evaluation weights;
- risks;
- BOQ values;
- team data;
- contractual logic;
- acceptance criteria;
- analytical nodes/edges.

## Required outputs
Persist:
- `section_visual_board_id`;
- `section_visual_charter_id`;
- page-to-board region mapping;
- page visual-reference crop/reference;
- board-generation status;
- board QA decision;
- board rejection reasons if regenerated.

## Freeze rule
Once approved, the Golden Section Board becomes a **visual-structure target**, not a suggestion. Native HTML/PDF/PPTX composition may adapt text wrapping and exact typography but may not freely redesign the artifact topology, focal placement, whitespace, visual weight, or section rhythm.

## Release rule
A section cannot claim premium visual execution merely because its individual pages pass collision QA. The rendered section must also pass the Visual Parity Bridge against the approved Golden Section Board and Section Visual Charter.
## v2.6.4.1 quality clarification
The Golden Section Board controls section rhythm and macro composition; it does not by itself prove consulting-grade page quality. Every page still passes the Consulting Visual Excellence Gate before Geometry Handoff and the Rendering Fidelity / Stress QA / Artifact Integrity gates after rendering.

## v2.6.4.4 role in Golden Visual Master workflow
The Golden Section Board/Section Visual Charter becomes a **Section Anchor** for page-by-page generation. It is used as a reference image alongside the persistent Deck Style Anchors and previous approved page. The page master itself may be a full generated composition; exact copy/logos remain native overlays.
