# V6 Root-Cause & Architecture Decision

The v5.2 audit established that modelling semantic relationships materially improves Artifact Truth, but a relationship-correct page can still be sparse, widget-like or below partner-grade visual quality. V6 therefore does not discard v5.2. It moves it down one layer: **Artifact Truth** becomes the structural floor, while **Consulting Exhibit Synthesis** becomes the new visual-intelligence layer.

V6 also removes split enforcement: declared QA capabilities are not accepted unless the unified runtime invokes them in the mandatory release chain.
