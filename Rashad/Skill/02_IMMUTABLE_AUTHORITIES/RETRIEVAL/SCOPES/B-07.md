| **B-07**        | **Programme Management Office Design**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs the PMO structure, mandate, governance, and capability to manage a complex transformation programme or portfolio of initiatives.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Delivers**    | PMO mandate and charter, PMO operating model, reporting architecture, risk management framework, governance calendar, PMO capability requirements.                                                                                                                                                            |
| **How**         | PMO needs assessment, mandate and scope definition, operating model design, reporting template development, governance calendar design, capability gap assessment.                                                                                                                                            |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Government PMOs in Saudi Arabia typically require a formal ministerial decision or circular establishing their authority before they can operate effectively. Informal PMOs are rarely effective in the Saudi government organisational culture. Saudization of the PMO team must be planned from the outset. |
