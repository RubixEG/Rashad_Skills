| **G-06**        | **Customer Experience Design**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs the customer or citizen experience across all touchpoints to achieve the target service quality and satisfaction levels.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Delivers**    | Customer journey maps, experience gap analysis, redesigned experience, service standards, implementation roadmap, CX measurement framework.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **How**         | Customer research, journey mapping, experience benchmarking, gap analysis, experience redesign, service standard development, measurement system design.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi citizen experience design must account for the Arabic-first interface requirement, mobile-first usage patterns (Saudi smartphone penetration is among the highest globally), family-unit service delivery models, and gender-specific service preferences. Government CX must align with the National Service Standards developed by the General Authority of Statistics and MOF. |
