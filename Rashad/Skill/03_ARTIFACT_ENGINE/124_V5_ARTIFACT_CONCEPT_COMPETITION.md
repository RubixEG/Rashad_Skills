# Artifact Concept Competition

For every critical analytical page, Rashad must produce 3-5 different artifact concepts.

Each concept must include:
- concept name;
- dominant relationship visualized;
- expected nodes;
- expected edges;
- why it is stronger than a card layout;
- risk of failure;
- MWAN or corpus family benchmark.

The Council selects one concept. The selected concept is binding unless render QA proves it cannot be executed without semantic loss.
