
# External Benchmark Review — v2.6.4.10

## Scope
Public primary-source materials reviewed from McKinsey, BCG, Deloitte and Saudi government procurement sources.

## Consulting benchmark observations
Public McKinsey and BCG public-sector publications repeatedly use exhibit-led communication: a clear argument, structured framework, visible evidence, and exhibits tied directly to the narrative. Deloitte public-sector material similarly frames transformations as operating systems, outcomes, governance, people, data and feedback loops rather than decorative technology alone.

## Government procurement context
Saudi Ministry of Finance / Etimad materials confirm electronic tender submission, technical evaluation and award workflows. They do not prescribe a specific proposal visual style. Therefore Rashad must optimize for **digital evaluator usability**: static scanability, legibility, evidence traceability, compliance visibility and clear decision logic rather than inventing a “government aesthetic”.

## Council implication
The target is not to copy BCG/McKinsey/Deloitte branding. The target is to emulate disciplined exhibit logic, content strength, evidence-led argumentation, and evaluator usability using Rubix identity.
