# Artifact Anti-Template and Repetition Detector

## Purpose
Prevent the deck from becoming a repeated sequence of grids and equal cards.

## Checks
- repeated equal-card layout;
- repeated 2x2 or 2x3 grids;
- repeated center-circle-with-cards when relationships differ;
- too many pages with same visual grammar;
- lack of semantic edges despite claimed artifact status.

## Release rule
If 3 pages in a short sequence share the same card/grid grammar without analytical justification, the deck fails Visual Rhythm QA.
