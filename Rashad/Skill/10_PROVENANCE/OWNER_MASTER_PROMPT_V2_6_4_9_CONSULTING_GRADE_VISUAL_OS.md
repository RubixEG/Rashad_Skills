# Owner Master Prompt — Consulting-Grade Visual Intelligence & HTML/SVG/CSS Production

## Objective
Audit and upgrade the complete Rashad Skill package so that high-density RFP Summaries and proposal pages consistently reach consulting-grade visual and analytical quality without requiring the owner to provide slide-by-slide correction notes.

## Core problem to solve
Rashad currently reasons strongly about content, evidence and artifact type, but its production path can become visually conservative, sparse, repetitive or card-led. Direct image generation historically produced stronger compositions because the visual model searched the whole page as one composition before committing to geometry. Rashad must reproduce that **visual search behavior** while retaining exact content, Arabic RTL, current Rubix brand control and deterministic HTML/CSS output.

## Required design challenge
Do not solve this by adding more superficial style rules. Redesign the visual-production architecture so Rashad:
1. thinks like a consulting Partner + Information Designer + Art Director before rendering;
2. generates multiple competing visual concepts before choosing one;
3. uses MWAN and approved current outputs as reference-conditioned visual evidence, not as copy templates;
4. delays exact geometry until after a winning composition has been selected;
5. builds the approved composition in professional fixed-canvas HTML/SVG/CSS;
6. uses SVG and relationship-led geometry for systems, architectures, journeys, networks, Gantts, matrices and decision artifacts instead of defaulting to generic cards;
7. preserves content density, evidence, implications and evaluator usefulness;
8. performs browser screenshot critique and repair before export;
9. treats HTML/SVG/CSS as the production visual master, then projects that exact master to PDF and visual-mirror PPTX;
10. retains image generation as an optional visual-concept/reference engine and as an asset generator, not as the only path to consulting-grade quality.

## Mandatory cover rule
The cover uses a strong topic-specific consulting-grade editorial image on the physical left, with a clean right-side title/identity zone. The `Rubix | Client` signature remains on one optical line on the left and uses exact governed brand assets.

## Consulting-grade quality requirements
Pages must be argument-led, evidence-rich, visually restrained, analytically legible and appropriate for Saudi government / senior executive review. MWAN/reference quality is a lower-bound benchmark for information hierarchy, density, artifact richness and page polish—not a template to clone.

## Council requirement
Use a council including at minimum:
- AI Skill Architect / Context Engineer;
- Consulting Partner;
- Proposal Director;
- Senior Project Manager;
- Operations / Launch Lead;
- Governance & Decision-Control SME;
- Artifact / Information Design SME;
- HTML/SVG/CSS Composition Lead;
- Brand / RTL SME;
- QA / Release SME.

The council must audit the current Skill, the recent weak RFP Summary output, the MWAN reference pattern, authority conflicts, visual logic, content depth, card usage, color governance, geometry timing, renderer contracts and release gates.

## Non-regression
Preserve the immutable R-code prompt corpus, scope/mapping shards, owner-locked bilingual proposal skeleton, current Rubix assets, RTL rules, evidence truthfulness, RFP Summary product logic and QA Harness integration. No silent deletion or weakening of prior protected authorities.

## Deliverables
- upgraded complete Skill ZIP;
- professional audit report;
- authority-conflict report;
- non-regression report;
- new visual-production architecture;
- HTML/SVG/CSS composer contract;
- benchmark / self-critique scoring model;
- test cases and council verdict;
- final validation and checksum.

## Success condition
The owner should no longer need to repeatedly say “too many cards”, “too many colors”, “not consulting grade”, “weak artifact”, “too empty”, “logos weak/misaligned”, or “image generation thinks better”. These failure modes must be anticipated by the system and blocked before release.
