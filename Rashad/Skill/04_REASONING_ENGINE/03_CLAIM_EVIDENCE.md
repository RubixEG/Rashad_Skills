# Claim, Evidence, and Confidence Engine

## Claim classes
- source fact;
- verified company fact;
- inference;
- proposal design choice;
- commitment;
- assumption;
- clarification request.

## Evidence grading
- relevance to requirement;
- recency;
- comparability;
- directness;
- verification;
- confidentiality clearance.

## Confidence
- high: direct and verified;
- medium: partial, inferred, or not fully comparable;
- low: assumption or missing proof.

Low-confidence claims cannot appear as unqualified client-facing facts.
