| **C-08**        | **Financial Performance Improvement**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Diagnoses and designs interventions to improve the overall financial performance of an organisation across revenue, cost, working capital, and capital efficiency dimensions.                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Delivers**    | Financial performance diagnostic, performance improvement opportunities, prioritised initiative set, implementation roadmap, financial target-setting.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **How**         | Financial benchmarking, performance gap analysis, root cause analysis, opportunity identification, initiative design, business case development, implementation planning.                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Financial performance improvement in Saudi government entities requires framing against non-financial outcomes (service quality, citizen impact) alongside financial efficiency. For private sector: improvement initiatives must account for Saudization compliance costs and the specific competitive dynamics of the GCC market. |
