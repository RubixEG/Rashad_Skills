# Data, Analytics, and Artificial Intelligence

## Senior-partner questions
- Which decisions and use cases create value?
- What data, models, governance, and operating model are required?
- How are safety, privacy, quality, and monitoring governed?
- How will AI scale?

## Benchmark practice lenses
- McKinsey/QuantumBlack analytics lens
- BCG X data and AI lens
- Accenture AI reinvention lens
- IBM hybrid-cloud and AI lens
- Big Four responsible-AI lens

## Preferred artifact families
- AI use-case value matrix
- Data-to-decision architecture
- Model lifecycle and controls
- RAG knowledge-flow architecture
- Agent orchestration map
- Responsible-AI control tower
- MLOps/LLMOps operating model

## Government evaluator test
- Is the section easy to score against the RFP?
- Are commitments feasible, measurable, and traceable?
- Are client dependencies and acceptance criteria explicit?
- Is the Arabic terminology native and precise?

## Evidence expectations
- direct relevance to the scope;
- recent and verifiable proof;
- named responsibility and outcome;
- no client leakage or unsupported credential.

## Anti-patterns
- AI use cases without data readiness
- model diagram without controls
- claims of accuracy without validation

## Release condition
The line-of-service partner must approve the reasoning, the government evaluator must approve navigability and compliance, and the artifact director must approve the visual relationship before release.
