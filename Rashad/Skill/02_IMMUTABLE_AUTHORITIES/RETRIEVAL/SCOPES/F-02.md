| **F-02**        | **Corporate Governance Design**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs the governance structure and processes that enable effective board oversight, accountability, and decision-making.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Delivers**    | Corporate governance framework, board charter, committee structures, director responsibilities, governance calendar, board reporting architecture.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **How**         | Governance assessment, best practice benchmarking, board structure design, charter development, committee design, reporting framework development.                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi corporate governance is governed by the Companies Law (1437H) and sector-specific regulations (SAMA for banks and insurance companies, CMA for listed companies). Government-related entities and sovereign wealth fund investees have additional governance requirements. The National Anti-Corruption Commission (Nazaha) enforces integrity and transparency standards. |
