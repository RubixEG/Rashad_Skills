| Step 1. SCORE EVERY ISSUE on two dimensions applying the same standard to all: Strategic Impact (1-5) measuring how much resolving this changes the organisation ability to deliver on its mandate, and Urgency (1-5) measuring how quickly the situation deteriorates and what each additional month of delay costs. Show one sentence of reasoning per issue.                                                                                                                                                                |
| Step 2. CLASSIFY EACH ISSUE: Root cause or symptom? If symptom, name exactly what it is a symptom of. Flag any issue that appears critical but is actually downstream of another - treating it directly would produce temporary improvement followed by recurrence.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Step 3. IDENTIFY THE CRITICAL FEW: Name the 2-3 issues driving 80% of the problem. For each: one-line insight headline, root cause confirmation, quantified business or mandate impact (show the arithmetic), and data needed to confirm the ranking.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Step 4. NAME THE FALSE CRITICAL: Identify the one issue that looks most critical but is actually a symptom. State what it is a symptom of and why fixing it directly would fail or recur.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Step 5. CLOSE: One sentence each: (a) most important finding, (b) most important immediate action, (c) biggest risk if critical few are not addressed.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |

| **\<methodology\>**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
