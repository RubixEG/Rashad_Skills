| **I-02**        | **Programme Governance and Delivery**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Provides the governance structure, programme management methodology, and delivery assurance for a complex transformation programme.                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Delivers**    | Programme governance framework, PMO design and operating model, programme plan, risk and issue log, progress reporting framework, steering committee materials.                                                                                                                               |
| **How**         | PMO design, governance structure establishment, planning methodology design, risk management framework, reporting cadence design, steering committee facilitation.                                                                                                                            |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi government programme governance typically requires formal ministerial decision to establish. Steering committees with Deputy Minister or above participation require specific protocols. Programme reporting must align with the ministry existing governance and reporting structures. |
