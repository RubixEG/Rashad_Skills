| **I-04**        | **Agile Transformation**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs and implements the transition to agile ways of working to improve adaptability, speed of delivery, and stakeholder collaboration.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Delivers**    | Agile transformation strategy, agile operating model, agile governance framework, capability development plan, pilot design, scaled agile framework.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **How**         | Current maturity assessment, agile model selection, governance design, team structure design, ceremony and artefact design, capability building programme, pilot facilitation.                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Agile transformation in Saudi government organisations requires significant cultural adaptation: agile values of self-organisation and decentralised decision-making are in tension with hierarchical Saudi government culture. Successful agile adoption requires explicit leadership commitment and adaptation of agile methods to the Saudi institutional context. |
