# Visual Excellence & Rendering Integrity Council

STATUS: ACTIVE COUNCIL / TWO-STAGE QUALITY GOVERNANCE

## Purpose
Prevent the two recurring failure modes from being confused:
1. visually safe but weak/generic consulting pages;
2. strong approved pages that are degraded by rendering/export.

## Council A — Pre-Render Consulting Visual Excellence
Required roles by page materiality:
- Consulting Partner / chair;
- Proposal Director;
- Information Architect;
- Artifact Intelligence Director;
- Consulting Visual Benchmark Director;
- Saudi Government Proposal Suitability Director;
- Visual Restraint & Executive Discipline Director;
- relevant service-line SME when technical/commercial meaning is material;
- Arabic Editorial / RTL Director for Arabic pages.

Required decision:
`PRE_RENDER_VISUAL_COUNCIL = PASS | REWORK | REGENERATE | REDESIGN`

The council validates question, thesis, evidence, relationship, artifact family, nodes/edges, visual depth, benchmark quality, analytical legitimacy, executive restraint, government suitability and Ministry-Grade score.

## Council B — Post-Render Production Integrity
Required roles:
- Rendering Fidelity Director;
- Stress QA Director;
- Artifact Integrity Auditor;
- Arabic RTL / Bidi reviewer;
- Co-Brand Logo Director;
- Proposal Artifact Integrity & Prepress Director;
- Consulting Partner / release chair for critical pages.

Required decision:
`POST_RENDER_INTEGRITY_COUNCIL = PASS | FIX | RE-RENDER | BLOCK`

The council validates canvas, margins, text fit, overflow, overlap, clipping, topology, nodes/edges/connectors, image crop, font stability, RTL/LTR, brand, tables/charts/diagrams and cross-format parity.

## Quorum logic
Critical pages require Partner/Proposal Director + Artifact/Information role + relevant quality role(s). Rendering release requires Prepress + Artifact Integrity + RTL/Brand reviewers where applicable. A tool-generated PASS cannot substitute for council-required human/model visual inspection.

## No-compensation rule
- High render fidelity cannot compensate for weak consulting visual quality.
- High consulting visual quality cannot compensate for broken rendering fidelity.
- A high numeric score cannot waive a hard fail.
