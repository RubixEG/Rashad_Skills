
# Editorial Content Density & Readability Contract

Historical proposal analysis shows the firm's technical proposals are typically content-dense. Rashad must therefore avoid producing empty poster-like pages merely to look elegant.

## Page content expectations
Analytical page normally includes:
- thesis / answer;
- dominant evidence or relationship;
- supporting facts / proof;
- interpretation;
- implication/action;
- source trace.

## Readability
Density is acceptable only when hierarchy is clear and body text remains readable at 100% slide view. Expand pages instead of shrinking below the approved minimum.

## Balance
Whitespace should frame meaning, not substitute for missing content.
