| **J-06**        | **Education Sector Transformation**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Advises MOE, universities, private schools, and vocational training institutions on strategy, quality improvement, and transformation.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Delivers**    | Education strategy, institutional transformation plan, quality improvement programme, curriculum development advisory, EdTech strategy, Saudization plan for faculty.                                                                                                                                               |
| **How**         | Institutional diagnostic, accreditation gap assessment, curriculum analysis, faculty development plan, technology assessment, financial sustainability analysis.                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi education is governed by MOE for K-12, ETEC for testing, NCAAA for higher education accreditation, and TVTC for vocational training. Vision 2030 education targets include improving PISA scores, increasing higher education participation, and developing the skills required for economic diversification. |
