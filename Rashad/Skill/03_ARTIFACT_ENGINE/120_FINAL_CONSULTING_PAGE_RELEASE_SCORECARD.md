
# Final Consulting Page Release Scorecard

A critical analytical page must pass all applicable hard gates plus:
- Content Strength: 20
- Argument/Thesis: 10
- Artifact/Relationship Strength: 15
- Evidence/Traceability: 10
- Composition/Hierarchy: 15
- Typography/Readability: 10
- Brand/Palette: 5
- RTL/BiDi/Topology: 5
- Benchmark/Continuity: 5
- Evaluator Usability: 5

Critical threshold: **≥90/100**.

Hard blockers cannot be offset by the score.
