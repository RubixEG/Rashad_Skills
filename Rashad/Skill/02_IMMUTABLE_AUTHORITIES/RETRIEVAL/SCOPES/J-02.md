| **J-02**        | **Healthcare Sector Strategy and Operations**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Advises healthcare organisations (MOH, CBAHI-accredited hospitals, private healthcare groups) on strategy, operations, and transformation.                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Delivers**    | Healthcare strategy, clinical operating model, hospital efficiency programme, patient experience design, workforce planning, accreditation readiness.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **How**         | Healthcare diagnostic, clinical pathway mapping, patient journey analysis, workforce modelling, quality system assessment, financial sustainability analysis.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi healthcare is governed by MOH, with CBAHI providing accreditation and NHC (National Health Company) managing government healthcare assets. Vision 2030 healthcare targets include improving quality, reducing cost, and increasing private sector participation. Saudization requirements for clinical roles are complex. |
