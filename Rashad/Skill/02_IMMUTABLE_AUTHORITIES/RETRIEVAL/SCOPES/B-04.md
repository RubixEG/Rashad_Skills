| **B-04**        | **Governance Design**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
|-----------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs the governance architecture for an organisation, programme, or initiative: decision rights, accountability mechanisms, oversight structures, and escalation paths.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Delivers**    | Governance framework, Delegation of Authority (DOA) matrix, governance calendar, committee structures, reporting architecture, performance accountability framework.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **How**         | Current governance assessment, governance gap identification, DOA matrix design, committee structure design, reporting design, governance validation with leadership.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi government governance design must align with the relevant legal instruments establishing the organisation authority. DOA matrices for government entities must be consistent with the Civil Service Law and any sector-specific regulatory requirements. For private sector: board governance must comply with the Companies Law (1437H). |
