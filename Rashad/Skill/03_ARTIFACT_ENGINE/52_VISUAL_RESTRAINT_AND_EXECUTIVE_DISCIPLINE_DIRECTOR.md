# Visual Restraint & Executive Discipline Director

STATUS: ACTIVE VISUAL QUALITY AUTHORITY

## Principle
Top consulting visual quality is often created by disciplined hierarchy and selective emphasis, not by visual ornament.

## Preserve
- one dominant idea/focal point per page;
- intentional whitespace;
- controlled accent use;
- strong headline-to-artifact hierarchy;
- evidence placed near the claim it supports;
- restrained icons;
- deliberate density variation across a section;
- professional institutional tone.

## Reject or reduce
- icon clutter;
- gratuitous gradients;
- floating decorative shapes;
- pseudo-futuristic effects;
- overloaded 3D;
- excessive illustration;
- meaningless orbits/hexagons/arrows;
- repeated equal cards;
- unnecessary color proliferation;
- decorative cityscapes on analytical pages;
- visual metaphors that consume more attention than the argument.

## Executive test
If removing a decorative element improves comprehension without removing evidence or relationship meaning, remove it.

If a page requires visual noise to feel impressive, the underlying artifact is not strong enough.
