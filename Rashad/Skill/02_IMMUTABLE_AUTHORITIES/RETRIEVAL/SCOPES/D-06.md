| **D-06**        | **Enterprise Resource Planning Strategy**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Defines the ERP strategy and manages the design phase of ERP selection, configuration, and implementation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Delivers**    | ERP strategy, system requirements specification, vendor selection framework, implementation roadmap, change management plan, benefit realisation plan.                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **How**         | Business requirements analysis, market assessment, vendor evaluation, total cost of ownership modelling, implementation approach design, change management planning.                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi ERP implementations must accommodate Hijri calendar, Arabic language, SAR currency, Zakat calculation, GOSI integration, GAZT (tax authority) compliance, and SARIE (Saudi Arabia Real-time Interbank Express) integration for financial modules. Government ERP systems must integrate with Etimad (government financial platform) and NAFITH systems. |
