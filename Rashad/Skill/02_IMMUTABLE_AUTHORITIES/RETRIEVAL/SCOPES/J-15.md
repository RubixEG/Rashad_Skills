| **Saudi & GCC** | Monshaat (SME General Authority) is the primary government body for SME support. Vision 2030 targets SME contribution rising from 20% to 35% of GDP. SVC (Saudi Venture Capital) invests in venture and SME financing. BADIR programme (KACST) supports technology-based SMEs. Saudization of SME workforce is both a regulatory constraint and a strategic opportunity. |
