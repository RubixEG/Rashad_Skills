# RFP Summary Depth Acceptance Gate

STATUS: BLOCKING PRODUCT QA

The 24 logical roles are stable. The acceptance question is not whether all role names appear; it is whether each applicable role satisfies its Understanding Depth Contract.

## Blocking examples
Reject the Summary when:
- BOQ is summarized without line-by-line interpretation;
- Team shows only total headcount without role-level requirements and capacity analysis;
- Evaluation shows only pass mark without criterion/evidence/win-response logic;
- Commercial Exposure omits major cost drivers or pricing confidence;
- Risks are reduced to generic cards without cause/event/impact/evidence/mitigation/owner/bid implication;
- Clarifications do not state the decision changed by answer A vs B;
- Delivery Journey repeats scope instead of showing gates/dependencies/acceptance;
- a role is compressed into one page solely to maintain a fixed page count.

## Expansion rule
When a role fails depth or legibility on one page, expand it. Do not delete sections and do not change the canonical 24-role architecture.
