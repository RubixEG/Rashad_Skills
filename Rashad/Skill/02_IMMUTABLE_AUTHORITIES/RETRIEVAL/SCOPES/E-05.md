| **E-05**        | **Change Management**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs and manages the people, culture, and communication dimensions of a transformation programme to maximise adoption and minimise resistance.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Delivers**    | Change management strategy, change readiness assessment, stakeholder engagement plan, communication plan, training plan, resistance management plan, change impact assessment.                                                                                                                                                               |
| **How**         | Change impact assessment, stakeholder analysis, change readiness assessment, communication design, training needs analysis, resistance management planning, change reinforcement design.                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi organisational change management must account for the significant role of personal relationships and trust in gaining commitment to change, the importance of senior leadership visible sponsorship in motivating middle management, and the cultural sensitivity required when changes affect role authority or seniority structures. |
