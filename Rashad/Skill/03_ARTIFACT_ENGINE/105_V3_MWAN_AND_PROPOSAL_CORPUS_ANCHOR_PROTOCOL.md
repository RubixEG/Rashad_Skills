# 105 - V3 MWAN and Proposal Corpus Anchor Protocol

MWAN and the accepted proposal corpus are visual-intelligence anchors.

They teach:
- density;
- hierarchy;
- artifact family grammar;
- executive rhythm;
- panel-vs-system balance;
- government proposal tone;
- evidence layout.

They do not authorize:
- old logos;
- old colors;
- copy-paste layouts;
- old client data;
- weak repeated stock-photo patterns.

Every critical page should name its closest anchor family before composition.
