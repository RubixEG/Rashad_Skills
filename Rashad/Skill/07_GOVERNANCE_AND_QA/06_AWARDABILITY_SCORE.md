# Awardability Score

## Purpose
Estimate proposal competitiveness without pretending to predict the procurement decision.

## Score — 100
- pass/fail certainty: 15
- technical-score potential: 20
- evaluator confidence: 10
- differentiated value proposition: 10
- evidence relevance and recency: 10
- team compliance and credibility: 10
- delivery feasibility and acceptance: 10
- risk and contractual defensibility: 5
- commercial coherence: 5
- document quality and navigability: 5

## Confidence modifier
Each score is accompanied by confidence:
- high: direct RFP evidence and verified proof;
- medium: strong inference or partial evidence;
- low: assumption, missing appendix, or unverified proof.

## Rule
The score is a decision-support instrument, not a guarantee of award.
