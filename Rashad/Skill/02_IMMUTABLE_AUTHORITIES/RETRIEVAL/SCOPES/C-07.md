| **C-07**        | **Capital Allocation Framework**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
|-----------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs the framework through which an organisation allocates capital across competing investment opportunities.                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Delivers**    | Capital allocation framework, investment criteria, prioritisation methodology, portfolio review process, capital governance.                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **How**         | Current allocation assessment, criteria development, scoring methodology design, governance process design, calibration with historical investments, framework validation.                                                                                                                    |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi government capital allocation must align with MOF budget allocation processes, Vision 2030 programme priorities, and NTP performance targets. Private sector capital allocation frameworks must reflect the specific return requirements and risk tolerance of the ownership structure. |
