# Artifact Richness & Information Density Contract

## Richness principle
A strong artifact combines multiple evidence relationships into one visual system. It should reveal something a bullet list does not.

## Required layers for analytical pages
1. thesis layer;
2. dominant relationship artifact;
3. evidence/metric labels;
4. interpretation or implication layer;
5. source trace.

## Density bands
- CALM_EXECUTIVE: 30–45% purposeful occupied area;
- ANALYTICAL_CORE: 45–65%;
- DENSE_EVIDENCE: 55–75%;
- TECHNICAL/COMMERCIAL DRILLDOWN: 60–80% when typography remains readable.

Whitespace is a design asset; empty space caused by missing analysis is a defect.
