
# Content–Artifact Balance Contract

STATUS: HARD PAGE-INTELLIGENCE AUTHORITY — v2.6.4.10

## Principle
An artifact exists to make a relationship easier to understand. It cannot replace the actual consulting content.

## Pre-visual content lock
Before visual concept search, every analytical page has an approved `PAGE_CONTENT_PACK` containing:
- management/evaluator question;
- answer/thesis;
- 2–8 evidence units appropriate to page family;
- interpretation/so-what;
- implication/decision/action;
- source locators;
- caveats/confidence where material.

## Artifact necessity test
Use a dominant artifact only when at least one relationship is materially improved by visual encoding: sequence, dependency, hierarchy, causality, comparison, prioritization, architecture, ownership, flow, lifecycle, capacity, evaluation, risk, cost/acceptance linkage.

If the relationship is weak or the value is primarily explanatory, choose `EDITORIAL_EVIDENCE` rather than forcing an artifact.

## Content preservation
After visual composition, compare visible page content against the approved content pack. Layout repair may rephrase for fit only within approved editorial tolerance; it may not remove a material fact, implication, risk, condition, source or required label.

A beautiful page with weak content fails.
A dense page with no readable visual hierarchy fails.
