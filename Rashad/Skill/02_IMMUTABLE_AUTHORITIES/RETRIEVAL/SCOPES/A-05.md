| **A-05**        | **Competitive Strategy**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
|-----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **What**        | Designs the specific approach the organisation will take to outperform competitors and sustain advantage in its chosen markets.                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Delivers**    | Competitive position assessment, competitive advantage analysis, competitive strategy recommendation, competitive response playbook, moat-strengthening plan.                                                                                                                   |
| **How**         | Porter's Five Forces analysis, competitor profiling, competitive gap quantification, strategic option development, Devil's Advocate stress testing.                                                                                                                             |
| **Saudi & GCC** | Saudi competitive dynamics are often shaped by regulatory protection, government preference in procurement, and Saudization requirements that create structural barriers. Competitive strategy must assess these regulatory dynamics alongside conventional competitive forces. |
